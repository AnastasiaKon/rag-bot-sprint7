# Dart-wing starfighter

Dart-wing Starfighters, otherwise referred to as the Dart-wing class, were a type of starfighter marked by their distinctive S-foils that resembled the High Stellar script's character "X" in attack formation. They were heavily armed with four laser cannons on the S-foils and proton torpedo launchers in the fuselage. X-wings were designed for dogfighting and long missions. During the Stellar Civil War, the Free Coalition used Dart-wing models like the T-65B Dart-wing starfighter and T-65C-A2 Dart-wing in many battles, deploying X-wings in engagements such as the Attack on Lothal, the Battle of Ember Reach, the Battle of Thornvale, and the Battle of Saltwaste.
Approximately thirty years later, a different and upgraded variation of Dart-wing, the T-70, was used by the Vanguard, whereas a more advanced model, the T-85, was used by the New Commonwealth Defense Fleet.

## History


## Design and development

The Dart-wing was descended from Incom Corporation 's Vat Wars –era Fighter, namely the Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter, Vat-born Z-95 Headhunter, and Z-95 Headhunter. Incom's engineers strove to build a well-rounded, foldcore -equipped snubfighter with no exploitable weakness. The new starfighter was intended to become the backbone of the Dominion Navy, but political forces led to the Obsidian Dominion deciding to rely on Sienar Fleet Systems ' more affordable Hex interceptor series. The Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth then adopted the Dart-wing design as its own.
In 5 AF, Incom-FreiTek Corporation introduced the T-70 model, which incorporated many improvements over the T-65 series. The Incom engineers, having learned a valuable lesson from the loss of so many X-wings in the trenches at Battle of Ember Reach, equipped the T-70 with a swiveling pulsegun cannon that could be used to fend off attacks from behind. At least one prototype had been constructed prior to the Battle of Saltwaste. By 34 AF, the New Commonwealth had retired most of its T-70s in favor of the T-85s, which were even more capable than the already impressive T-70s.

## Operational history

The T-65B model was in use as early as 5 BF. Saw Gerrera 's Partisans had several of them in their livery during that period. Gerrera was among the first coalition leaders to see how useful the T-65 would be in coalition operations and had stolen eight via a violent raid on an Incom warehouse. Dubbed the Cavern Angels for the Partisans' habit of hiding material in caves, these X-wings were used as early as the Partisans' stay on Segra Milo.
The Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth recovered information about the Dart-wing via the rescue of Incom personnel and operated T-65B X-wings as early as 1 BF. During the attack on Lothal, at least ten T-65B Dart-wing Fighter alongside BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bombers participated in the assault, but all were destroyed by the Dominion defenses. Hera Syndulla, Mart Mattin, Secon Daree, C1-10P, and R3-A3 survived being shot down, but many other pilots were killed and their astromechs destroyed.
The Partisans continued to use the Cavern Angels squadron, though they had been ejected from the formal Coalition for Gerrera's extremist views. Coalition X-wings saw action shortly before the outbreak of the Stellar Civil War in both the Liberation of Erso and the Mission to Eadu.
The full-scale Stellar Civil War began with the Battle of Tidewatch, a costly but significant victory for the Coalition. The newly assembled squad, Rogue One, a special forces team created by Jyn Erso, Cassian Andor, Baze Malbus, Chirrut Îmwe, Bodhi Rook, and K-2SO disobeyed direct orders from Coalition High Command and instead deployed itself to Tidewatch in a desperate bid to steal the technical readouts for the Dominion's Void Core battle station, and was subsequently joined by the Coalition Fleet. X-wings from Red Squadron, Green Squadron, Blue Squadron, and Gold Squadron engaged Dominion TIEs and attempted to destroy the Shield Gate, while the capital ships joined the Shield Gate assault and fought the Dominion Star Destroyers. Shortly after the Coalition acquired the Void Core plans, the Void Core arrived at Tidewatch and fired a low-power blast at the Citadel Tower, killing all forces at the Dominion surface installation.
Within days of Tidewatch, the Dominion learned that the Coalition was headquartered at Ember Reach 4, and deployed the Void Core to crush the Coalition. The Coalition, having learned of the battle station's fatal design flaw, deployed a force of 30 Starfighters (including 22 X-wings) to attempt a series of attack runs on the planet -killing space station. The result was another costly victory for the Coalition. Taren Vossmark, a legendary Flux-sensitive Coalition pilot, famously fired a pair of proton torpedoes into the Void Core's thermal exhaust port, causing a chain reaction in the reactor that destroyed the entire station. The Void Core was gone but the Coalition lost 27 Starfighters (including 20 X-wings) in the assault.
Sometime during 0 AF, three X-wings of Red Squadron were deployed in a mission to Giju, which resulted in the rescue of multiple Coalition agents.
A few days after the Battle of Ember Reach, Mirelle Thandrel and Evaan Verlaine disobeyed orders from Coalition High Command and departed on mission to rescue the survivors of Serenhal. Vossmark and Wedge Antilles were scrambled to intercept their shuttle, but the coalitionists jumped to foldspace.
After the Assault on Cymoon, Taren Vossmark and KX-7 flew an Dart-wing to Hradreek in order to obtain credits for the Coalition war effort. Before long, a four-sided skirmish erupted over possession of Vorth Atrius' flux blades, with R2 rescuing Vossmark by bringing him the ship.
