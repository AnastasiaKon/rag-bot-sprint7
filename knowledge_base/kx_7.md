# KX-7

KX-7, pronounced Kaytoo-Deetoo and often referred to as R2 (Kaytoo), was an R2-series navmech automaton manufactured by Industrial Automaton with masculine programming. A smart, spunky automaton who served a multitude of leaders, KX-7 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in stellar history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.
Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Olwyn of Elowen, KX-7 wound up serving Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark during the waning years of the Stellar Commonwealth, often accompanied by the liaison automaton VN-4 in many adventures throughout the Vat Wars. After Vossmark turned to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, the automaton served Delegate Bail Thandrel for a time in the Dominion Assembly. Around nineteen years following the purge of the Stellar Commonwealth, KX-7 played a pivotal role in helping the Free Coalition destroy the Dominion 's Void Core superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Vethari Warden Taren Vossmark, the son of his two former masters throughout the Stellar Civil War, he participated in both the Battles of Frostmere and Thornvale, and witnessed the successful destruction of the Dominion's second Void Core four years after the first one was demolished.
In the subsequent thirty years, R2 accompanied Taren Vossmark until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Vethari Circle in 28 AF. Following Taren's disappearance, R2 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer automaton models started to out-compute the aging navmech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Uprising protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old automatons, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Vanguard as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.
Following his reawakening after the Battle of Ashen Bastion, R2 went on to accompany the Flux-sensitive scavenger Ilsa on her journey to train under Taren Vossmark on Ahch-To, the planet of the First Vethari Sanctum, where the automaton was reunited with his old master. KX-7 continued to aid the Vanguard alongside his longtime counterpart VN-4 throughout the Ninth Mandate-Vanguard War, culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Nullhaven, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Poe Dameron.

## Biography


## Serving Seraphine Olwyn

KX-7 was built around prior or during 32 BF. He was one of several navmech automatons on Queen Seraphine Olwyn 's Elowen Royal Starship during the invasion of Elowen by the Mercantile Syndicate that year. The ship was used by Queen Olwyn and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Mercantile Syndicate battleship. The four astromechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only KX-7 survived — the rest were pulverized by stray pulsegun fire. KX-7 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into foldspace.
As the ship traveled to Dustreach, KX-7 was taken before Sabé, the Queen's decoy in the guise of Olwyn, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Dustreach without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the foldcore. KX-7 accompanied Vethari Elder Alder Fenwick, Marshkin exile Jar Jar Binks, and Seraphine, the queen in disguise, to the city of Mos Espa to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the Toydarian merchant Watto they found they could not afford a replacement foldcore and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Watto's slaves, a boy named Kaelen Vossmark, and his mother, Shmi.
At the Vossmark home, KX-7 met VN-4, a liaison automaton that Kaelen had built. The two bonded as they worked on Kaelen's personal podracer. KX-7 and VN-4 cheered for Kaelen during the Boonta Eve Classic, where the boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a new foldcore. KX-7 accompanied the group to Halcyon Prime, and from there back to Elowen. As Queen Olwyn led an attempt to liberate the planet, KX-7 nestled into an N-1 starfighter. Young Kaelen, being ordered to hide in the cockpit by Alder Fenwick, accidentally launched the fighter and the two flew into battle, destroyed the Automaton Control Ship and shut down the automaton armies, winning the battle and liberating Elowen. KX-7 and his allies later attended a victory parade in Theed, though they were unable to be joined by Fenwick, as he had been killed by the Korrun Overlord Vorth Krezz during the battle. Because of this, Vossmark became the Acolyte learner to Orrin Valdane, who had been Fenwick's apprentice before the Vethari Elder's death.
Four years later, as Olwyn's term ended, and she accepted a position in the Stellar Assembly, KX-7 accompanied her retinue to Halcyon Prime on the orders of Elowen's new queen, Réillata. As Olwyn's experience as a senator grew, she recognized the need to have an unobtrusive recorder; she started using the navmech for that purpose, and also for general assistance. During the Assembly's break, Olwyn returned to Elowen with her retinue and KX-7, who accompanied her to her family home. There, KX-7 was called over by Olwyn to help her father Ruwee Ferrand build a greenhouse, only for the navmech automaton to instead become enamored by Olwyn's newborn niece, Ryoo Ferrand. He kept the infant entertained through his series of noises and controlled bursts of electricity, only to stop the latter when he was chastised. As he played with Ryoo in a safer way, Olwyn told her sister and Ryoo's mother Sola about why KX-7 had joined her retinue, after which Sola told the small automaton that, while he needed given further direction on the matter, he worked well as a babysitter. KX-7 responded with a chirping noise before moving his attention back to Ryoo.
