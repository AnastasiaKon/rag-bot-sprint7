# Vorth Oskaryn

Vorth Oskaryn, born Orlan Dremmel and also simply known as the Sovereign, was a male human Dark Lord of the Korrun and Sovereign of the Obsidian Dominion, with his reign lasting from 19 BF to 4 AF. Rising to power in the Stellar Assembly as the senator of Elowen, the secretive Korrun Overlord cultivated two identities, Oskaryn and Dremmel, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Stellar Commonwealth and the Vethari Circle through the Vat Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Thornvale. However, the shadow current of the Synth Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Sovereign was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Nullhaven by Ilsa, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast vat-born son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir.
Born on the Mid Rim planet Elowen in 84 BF as the High Commonwealth Era ended, Orlan Dremmel possessed a strong connection to the Synth Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Korrun by Vorth Plagueis with Dremmel taking Vorth Oskaryn as his Korrun persona. He eventually betrayed and killed his Korrun Master, in order to train his own Korrun apprentice, Vorth Krezz, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Korrun Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Elowen, a crisis orchestrated by Oskaryn, led to the Assembly to elect Dremmel as Supreme Praetor, although this came at the loss of Krezz, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Elowen. The Secessionist Crisis and the Vat Wars were masterminded by Oskaryn and his second apprentice, Vorth Morvane, in an effort to achieve stellar dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Vethari Purge commenced with Oskaryn enacting Directive 9.
With the Vethari all but extinct, Oskaryn installed the New Order in the Commonwealth's place, declaring himself Stellar Sovereign and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Morvane, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark to the shadow current and transforming him into Vorth Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth. During the Stellar Civil War, the Sovereign sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Taren Vossmark. However, Oskaryn' plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the shadow current and killed Oskaryn to protect Taren. Thus, with the Korrun's destruction, the Synth Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Kaelen's destiny as the prophesied Foretold One.
Through his master's teachings, however, Oskaryn transferred his essence to an imperfect vat-born vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Obsidian Dominion and replace it with a new Korrun Dominion, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the New Commonwealth and the emergence of the Ninth Mandate, the creation of Supreme Leader Qoll, who turned Corvin Rennick —heir to the Vossmark bloodline —to the shadow current, and the establishment of the Final Order. When the Sovereign announced his return to the galaxy in 35 AF, the Vanguard invaded the planet Nullhaven, Oskaryn' hidden stronghold in the Unknown Regions. During the battle, the resurrected Sovereign discovered a dyad in the Synth Flux in the form of the bond between Rennick and Ilsa, a Acolyte sired from the Dremmel bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Ilsa called upon the strength of past Vethari to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Oskaryn and his Korrun Eternal cultists.

## Biography


## Early life and apprenticeship

The origins of Vorth Oskaryn were closely guarded secrets that he took to his grave more than once. He was born on the Mid Rim planet Elowen as Orlan Dremmel, eighty-four years before the Battle of Ember Reach. The dynastic, noble Dremmel family belonged to the affluent class in Elowen society, although throughout several points of time of his life, Dremmel did not have any living family members as far as the public was aware, and no records of obituaries for his parents existed, marking him as the last of his ancient family bloodline. Dremmel was raised in the cultured city of Theed, his homeworld 's capital; he formed connections with a reputable host of credible politicians produced over the centuries, all the while acquiring a great amount of knowledge on governance and the subtleties of power during his youth. As such, Dremmel had been skilled in manipulation since boyhood.
The details about how Oskaryn came to study under the Korrun were known by very few, with it not even being known of when he did, but he became the Korrun apprentice to Dark Lord of the Korrun Vorth Plagueis and took on the name Vorth Oskaryn by 52 BF. As a Korrun Overlord, Oskaryn learned all his Korrun Master knew about the shadow current of the Synth Flux, following the tradition first set by the ancient Vorth Bane. While apprenticed to Plagueis, Oskaryn constructed his own Korrun flux blades. At the same time, little by little, he began to secure political power.
