# Krezz

Krezz was a Flux-sensitive Dathomirian Zabrak male who was apprenticed to the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn, and after his defeat by the Vethari Orrin Valdane, he established himself as a crime lord during the Vat Wars and the Dominion Era. The renegade Korrun Overlord was a formidable warrior, driven by hatred against the Vethari and his former Korrun Master.
Born in 54 BF during the time of the Stellar Commonwealth, Krezz was a son of Mother Talzin, who led the Nightsisters of Dathomir. Taken as a child by Vorth Oskaryn, Vorth Krezz was molded into a deadly Dark Lord of the Korrun, growing in hatred of the Vethari Knights who served the Commonwealth and the bright current of the Synth Flux —while the Vethari Circle had deemed the Korrun extinct for a thousand years, Krezz's power-hungry master set his Grand Plan into motion with the Mercantile Syndicate 's Invasion of Elowen in 32 BF. Vorth Krezz announced the return of the Korrun on Dustreach, where he tried to kill the Vethari emissaries of the Commonwealth's Supreme Praetor. However, Krezz's failure to do so allowed Vethari Elder Alder Fenwick and his pupil, Acolyte Orrin Valdane, to bring Queen Olwyn of Elowen and a boy strong in the Synth Flux —exceptionally so, to the extent that Fenwick believed this "Kaelen Vossmark" to be the prophesied Foretold One who would bring balance to the galaxy —to Halcyon Prime, the heart of the Commonwealth.
Nevertheless, Vorth Oskaryn used the resultant crisis in the Assembly to elevate his public persona, Delegate Dremmel of Elowen, to the position of Supreme Praetor. The Queen and Vethari, meanwhile, joined forces with the native Marshkins on Elowen to throw off the yoke of the Mercantile Syndicate. Vorth Krezz, having been dispatched by his master to oversee the Federation leadership, was redirected to handle the Vethari. Krezz dealt a fatal blow to Alder Fenwick, but Valdane in turn defeated Krezz by bisecting him at the waist, with the Dark Lord falling to the abyss and presumed dead. He was actually alive, sustained by his rage in the wastelands of Lotho Minor. Around the same time, Oskaryn took on Fenwick's Vethari Elder, Haldrek, as his new apprentice. A decade afterward, Haldrek set the second part of the Korrun plan into motion with the start of the Vat Wars.
As the galaxy plunged into war, Mother Talzin plotted against Haldrek and had one of her sons, Savage Opress, seek out Krezz. Indeed, the defeated Zabrak was brought back to Dathomir and had his sanity and mobility restored by Talzin's magick. Krezz trained his newfound brother in the ways of the Korrun, initially wanting to take revenge against Orrin Valdane. After the brothers were defeated by the Vethari, they took their chance to increase their powers with a brazen takeover of Ironhold, the erstwhile neutral world torn between the interests of the Commonwealth and the Secessionists. Krezz seized control of the self-styled Death Watch and used them terrorise Ironhold while he expanded his criminal enterprise, known as the Shadow Collective —the Pyke Syndicate, Black Sun, and the Slorg Clan were coerced to join forces under the underworld's new crime lord—until he caught the attention of Oskaryn, who killed Krezz's brother and captured Krezz.
Krezz was rescued by his Ironholder loyalists and immediately tried to reconsolidate his Shadow Collective. However, he and his mother's desire for revenge against Vorth Oskaryn was checked by the Dark Lord, who instead decimated their homeworld of Dathomir with the use of the Secessionists led by Haldrek and General Grievous. Mother Talzin herself was killed while protecting Krezz, and his syndicates were weakened. While he regathered with his loyalists on Ironhold, Krezz sensed that his former master's Grand Plan was coming to its climax and wished to lure Kaelen Vossmark—the Vethari Warden trained by Orrin Valdane whom he correctly believed was central to Oskaryn' schemes—alongside Valdane himself to Ironhold. Krezz's thirst for revenge was foiled again when Commonwealth forces intervening on the side of the Ironhold resistance were instead led by Vossmark's old apprentice, Ilyra Sendt.
While Krezz and Sendt fought at the Siege of Ironhold, Oskaryn successfully executed his plan to destroy the Vethari Circle and anoint himself Stellar Sovereign. With the support of some Ironholder and Zabrak loyalists, Krezz continued trying to control the underworld but operated more clandestinely, away from the prying apparatus of the new Dominion regime. He was known in criminal circles as the Shadow Lord and was pursued by the Sovereign's Inquisitors, who simply called him the Shadow. In the Dominion's later years, Krezz secretly coordinated the Five Crime Syndicates and controlled the Crimson Dawn syndicate through Dryden Vos and Qi'ra.
Krezz also sought a great weapon on Malachor purported to be able of destroying all life and thus crossed paths with the Vethari Kanan Jarrus and Ezra Bridger as well as Ilyra Sendt. However, they were all stopped by Vorth Velgor, the fallen Vossmark. Krezz escaped and continued to tempt Bridger towards the shadow current in service of his own ends. Bridger rejected Krezz's overtures, but not before the former Korrun Overlord used their connection to track his old nemesis Valdane back to Dustreach. Their last duel ended with one fatal blow to Krezz. Learning that Valdane had consigned himself to a life of exile to protect the prophesied Foretold One who would destroy the Korrun, Krezz died with the belief that he would ultimately be avenged.
