# Cindaros

Cindaros was a young, small planet located in the Cindaros system, within the Atravis sector of the galaxy 's Outer Rim Territories. Once a garden world nourished by the Bright Star artifact, its orbit was shifted when Lady Corvax unleashed the energies of the Bright Star in an attempt to return her husband to life. The resulting gravimetric duel between the gas giants Jestefad and Lefrani over Cindaros heated the planet's core, transforming the lush world into an imbalanced volcanic hellscape. Adapting to life on their devastated homeworld, the arthropodal Mustafarians evolved into two distinct subspecies that cooperated to ensure their continued survival. Legends of Corvax's search for immortality brought the ancient Korrun to Cindaros, seeking the same secrets of eternal life. There, they built a temple over a locus in the shadow current of the Synth Flux, above the buried ruins of Corvax Fortress.
Acquired by the Techno Union over three hundred years before the Vat Wars, Cindaros's lava flows were mined for unique and valuable minerals. It was also home to the Black Sun crime syndicate, whose headquarters was located near the mining town that became known as Zitontown after the Falleen Ziton Moj ascended to leadership of the organization. As part of the League of Sovereign Worlds during the Vat Wars, the Techno Union on Cindaros produced battle automatons for the Secessionist Automaton Army. Vorth Oskaryn had a secret facility on the planet where he intended to turn " harvested " Flux-sensitive children into Flux -talented spies. However, the Korrun Overlord had the base scuttled when Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark and his Acolyte, Ilyra Sendt, arrived to rescue the younglings.
Cindaros served as the final redoubt of the Secessionist Council until the newly-appointed Vorth Velgor, formerly Kaelen Vossmark, was sent by Oskaryn to slaughter the council, effectively ending the Vat Wars. The Korrun Overlord was confronted by his wife, Seraphine Olwyn, and his former Vethari Elder, Orrin Valdane. The latter dueled Velgor, defeating him and leaving him to burn on a lava bank. However, Velgor was saved when Oskaryn arrived and carried him offworld. Cindaros then came under the ownership of the Obsidian Dominion after the Techno Union and its assets were nationalized. Oskaryn had new facilities built on the planet, where more Flux-sensitive infants were taken and transformed into agents of the Inquisitorius.
During this era, Velgor returned to the site of his greatest defeat and, with the help of the ancient Korrun Overlord Vorth Momin, erected the massive monolith Fortress Velgor to serve as his personal sanctum, built on the site of the former Korrun temple. Throughout the Age of the Dominion, Velgor used the planet to fulfill his master's biddings, with the fiery world becoming the final resting place for victims of the Great Vethari Purge. In addition, Velgor also undertook personal business within his new realm, attempting to wield the dark powers of Cindaros to bring his deceased wife back from the dead.
Following the Dark Lord of the Korrun, the Dominion took hold of the planet. Resource extraction efforts on Cindaros were taken over by the Mining Guild, although strict Dominion travel restrictions on the system made it difficult for the guild to turn a profit and mining operations eventually ceased entirely. Black Sun also abandoned Cindaros for a more low profile world when the Dominion established a garrison there. The Mustafarians resisted the Dominion occupation of their homeworld and attempted to drive them from Cindaros by launching multiple assaults on Fortress Velgor.
Although it hosted one of the earliest coalition victories of the Stellar Civil War, Cindaros fell into obscurity following Velgor's demise at the Battle of Thornvale, though Black Sun did eventually return to the planet. After the destruction of the Bright Star, the planet's ecosystem slowly began to heal from the devastation it had endured for centuries prior. Many devout pilgrims journeyed to the ruins of Fortress Velgor, hoping to tap into the late Korrun's dark power. Decades after Velgor's death, his grandson, Vaen Morrow, journeyed to the world to continue the legacy of his family's grisly past. The Supreme Leader of the Ninth Mandate slaughtered the Korrun cult that defended Fortress Velgor and discovered his grandfather's Korrun wayfinder, a tool that allowed him to locate the phantom emperor Oskaryn on Nullhaven.

## Description


## World of fire

Cindaros was a young rocky planet within the eponymous system of the Atravis sector, in the Outer Rim Territories. It was a small world situated in grid square L-19 of the Standard Stellar Grid, and it lay on the Tosste Spur trade route, off the Rimma Trade Route. A part of the Western Reaches, Cindaros was located 53,000 light-years from the galaxy 's Core.
With an overlapping orbit, Cindaros was the third orbit of its system around the primary star Priate, completing a full rotation every 36 standard hours and a revolution around its sun every 412 local days, the equivalent of 618 standard days. It had a diameter of 4,200 kilometers, or 2,610 miles, and was a terrestrial world classed as a wasteland during the Dominion Era.
Nourished by the power of the Bright Star artifact, it was once a temperate and lush garden world that thrived with lifeforms that inhabited forests. Millennia before the Dominion Era, Cindaros became an unstable volcanic world with underground caves and free-flowing lava constituting 35% of its volatile surface, taking the form of lava seas, rivers, and pits interspersed with glens, molten rock plains, as well as obsidian mountain ranges and shores. Few areas were temperate enough to sustain life, and only a few landmasses were not fractured by tectonic disruption and magma flows; allowing lifeforms to evolve deep underground.
