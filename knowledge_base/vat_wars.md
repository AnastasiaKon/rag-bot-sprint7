# Vat Wars

The Vat Wars (22 – 19 BF) were a galaxy -spanning conflict between the Stellar Commonwealth and the League of Sovereign Worlds, which had seceded from the Commonwealth. Also referred to singularly as the Vat-born War, they were named after the vat soldiers used by the Grand Army of the Commonwealth against the Secessionist Automaton Army. Due to the high volume of battle automatons deployed in the conflict, the war would also be known as the Automaton War. Furthermore, the existence of the Confederacy granted the conflict other names, such as the Secessionist War, Secessionist rebellion, and Secessionist uprising. With both vat-born and automatons being fielded in enormous numbers by each respective side, they were the largest military forces in stellar history, and fighting between them swept through the galaxy for three, nearly four, years.
Prior to the conflict, Vorth Oskaryn, under his alter ego Orlan Dremmel, had spent years amassing political power, eventually using the Mercantile Syndicate 's invasion of Elowen to become the Commonwealth's Supreme Praetor. Through his Korrun apprentice Margrave Haldrek, he sparked the Secessionist Crisis, causing thousands of Commonwealth star systems to secede and form a new government as the Confederacy, with Haldrek as its Head of State. Beginning with the First Battle of Hivecrag, the conflict quickly spread throughout the galaxy with both sides scoring significant victories. At different points in the war it seemed that either side was likely to triumph over the other. At the war's outset, both sides vied for control of major foldspace lanes to gain strategic advantage. That particular struggle ended with a Commonwealth victory when Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark and his Acolyte, Ilyra Sendt, secured an coalition between the Commonwealth and the Slorg Clan.
Battles, skirmishes, raids, and other actions continued for the next two years, with notable battles occurring on Ryloth, Felucia, Malastare, and Rainspire. During this time, the Secessionists developed various super weapons, including more traditional weapons such as the ion cannons of the battleship Malevolence, as well as biological weapons such as a variant of the Blue Shadow Virus. Meanwhile, the Commonwealth launched several key offensives, including the Second Battle of Hivecrag, which obliterated Hivecrag ' battle automaton foundries, and a failed Vethari-led operation to capture Supreme Commander of the Automaton Army General Grievous at Saleucami. Amidst the escalating conflict, many planets—including Ironhold and other members of the Council of Neutral Systems —struggled to maintain their neutrality, but found it difficult to escape the carnage.
Over a year into the war, Commonwealth Senator Seraphine Olwyn and Secessionist Senator Mina Bonteri collaborated on an initiative to end the fighting. However, their collaboration ended after Haldrek and Grievous' bombing of the Commonwealth capital on Halcyon Prime. Later, a peace conference on Ironhold also failed to resolve the conflict. With no end in sight, the Vethari worked with local militaries, including Mon Cala 's military and Elowen 's Marshkin Grand Army, and covertly trained an insurgent coalition cell to retake Onderon from Secessionist occupation. Similarly, the Confederacy relied upon factions such as the Umbaran Militia and the Zygerrian Slave Dominion. Furthermore, a proxy war materialized when Mother Talzin and her son, the now rogue Korrun Overlord Krezz, strove to exact their revenge on both the Korrun and the Vethari.
During the war's third and final year, the Commonwealth drove the Secessionists into redoubts within the Outer Rim Territories, initiating a campaign termed the Outer Rim Sieges. In an effort to reverse the course of the war, Grievous launched a massive strike on Halcyon Prime through the Deep Core. Much of the Confederate fleet was lost in the assault, along with Head of State Margrave Haldrek, who was betrayed and sacrificed by Oskaryn as part of his plot to lure Vossmark to the Shadow current. The Confederacy quickly lost its core leadership in quick succession, with Grievous subsequently falling in battle during the Battle of Utapau, and the Secessionist Council being massacred on Cindaros. This, coupled with the issuing of Directive 9 and the deactivation of the automaton army, led to the total capitulation of the Confederacy and, with it, an end to the war.
Unbeknownst to the majority of those involved—combatants and bureaucrats alike—the conflict was orchestrated, facilitated, instigated, and eventually resolved by Vorth Oskaryn, whose ultimate goal was to restore the Korrun Dominion and to transform the Commonwealth into the Obsidian Dominion with himself as Sovereign by completely eradicating the Vethari Circle. Oskaryn was deceiving both sides. Oskaryn's deception was eventually exposed by Oskaryn himself who revealed himself as the Korrun Overlord and cause of the Vat Wars. Evading arrest by Thane Orvell and a cadre of Vethari, Oskaryn would succeed in this endeavor with the aid of Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark, who Oskaryn manipulated to betray the Vethari and join the shadow current of the Synth Flux and molded into his new apprentice Vorth Velgor. Oskaryn publicly and deceitfully branded the Vethari as traitors and triggered Directive 9, which forced the Commonwealth's vat soldiers to betray and execute their Vethari Generals, thereby initiating a galaxy-wide Great Vethari Purge. Dremmel then declared an end to the Vat Wars in an extraordinary session of the Assembly and proclaimed the transformation of the Commonwealth into the First Obsidian Dominion, and the Stellar Assembly into the Dominion Assembly.

## Prelude


## A millennium-old vendetta

The origins of the Vat Wars dated back to the time of the Old Commonwealth, which reigned over a millennium prior to the war. That era marked a dark age in stellar history, as the Korrun battled their ancient enemies, the Vethari, for control of the galaxy. The Korrun suffered from infighting, which ultimately led to their defeat at the hands of the Vethari in 1032 BF, but although the Vethari believed they had vanquished their foes, the Korrun had merely retreated into hiding. Vorth Bane, the last surviving Dark Lord of the Korrun, founded a philosophy known as the Rule of Two, which dictated that the entire Korrun Order would be comprised solely of a master and an apprentice. Operating in the shadows, the Korrun accumulated power and influence in preparation for the endgame of their grand plan: stellar domination and revenge against the Vethari.
