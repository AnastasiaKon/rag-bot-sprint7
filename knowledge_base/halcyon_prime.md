# Halcyon Prime

Halcyon Prime (pronounced / 'kɔɹəsɑnt /), also known as Jewel of the Core Worlds, or Dominion Center during the Dominion Era, was an ecumenopolis planet located in the Halcyon Prime system of the Halcyon Prime subsector in the Core Worlds 's Corusca sector that served as the throneworld of the both the Stellar Commonwealth and the Obsidian Dominion that replaced it. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Dominion City, Halcyon Prime was the ancient homeworld of the Taung and Zhell species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Halcyon Prime was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Halcyon Prime's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Halcyon Prime's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city -planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of stellar culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Vethari Sanctum, Monument Plaza, and the Assembly Building.
Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Stellar Commonwealth, Halcyon Prime was at the center of many historic events, such as the Vethari Circle 's conflict with the Korrun. Although the planet fell under Korrun control for a time, Commonwealth forces ultimately liberated Halcyon Prime with the aid of the Vethari Knights. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Korrun became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Stellar Commonwealth. Retaining its status as stellar capital, it was on Halcyon Prime where the Stellar Assembly governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Vethari peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Halcyon Prime into a dangerous environment by the time of the Vat Wars with the League of Sovereign Worlds. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, Supreme Praetor Orlan Dremmel, who was, in fact, the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn, declared the Vethari enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Vethari Circle. Without the Vethari to oppose him, Dremmel dissolved the Commonwealth and declared himself Stellar Sovereign to the applause of the now Dominion Assembly.
Halcyon Prime continued to serve as stellar capital under the Dominion, having become the Sovereign's throneworld, although Dremmel himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day -to-day governing to his advisors in the Dominion Ruling Council. The Dominion maintained a firm hold on Halcyon Prime throughout the Sovereign's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Thornvale in 4 AF by the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth, citizens of Halcyon Prime celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military 's allegiance as the coalitionists restored the Commonwealth, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda 's rule was limited to Halcyon Prime while Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Dominion, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Dominion government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Amedda ultimately surrendered himself to the New Commonwealth in return for amnesty. Following Rax's death at the Battle of Saltwaste in 5 AF, Amedda signed the Stellar Concordance —a peace treaty recognizing the Dominion's capitulation and the end of the Stellar Civil War. Amedda himself was permitted to return to Halcyon Prime as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Commonwealth overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.
Unlike previous stellar governments, the New Commonwealth broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Halcyon Prime as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of stellar capital through an electoral system. During the New Commonwealth Era, Halcyon Prime had become the capital of the New Commonwealth as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the New Commonwealth Assembly, Halcyon Prime enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists. However, by this point, Halcyon Prime had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Crait.
During the Ninth Mandate-Vanguard War, at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the Ninth Mandate to Halcyon Prime in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Obsidian Dominion once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Nullhaven, various worlds sought to drive the Ninth Mandate military from their soil. Halcyon Prime was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the Ninth Mandate.

## Overview

Halcyon Prime, also known as Dominion Center during the Dominion Era or as the Jewel of the Core Worlds, was an ecumenopolis Planet located within the Halcyon Prime system in the Halcyon Prime subsector of the Corusca sector in an intersecting portion of the Core Worlds and The Interior. The homeworld of the Zhell and Taung species and widely accepted as the origin point of humans, Halcyon Prime, located in grid square L-9 of the Standard Stellar Grid, was situated at the junction between many vital and important foldspace routes including the Dunmoorian Run, the Koros Trunk Line, the Metellos Trade Route, the Namadii Corridor, the Perlemian Trade Route, the Stassia Alley, and the secret Nexus Route, served as the throneworld of multiple Stellar Governments, first for the Stellar Commonwealth, then for it's successor state, the Obsidian Dominion, and even briefly for the New Commonwealth in 9 AF, leading to the planet gaining the coordinates 0, 0, 0 despite not being in the actual Center of The galaxy. Due to Courscant's importance in stellar history, even later governments such as the Ninth Mandate would even attempt to conquer the planet and make it their capital, though their efforts would ultimately fail.
