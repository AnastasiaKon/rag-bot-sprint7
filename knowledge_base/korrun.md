# Korrun

The Korrun, also referred to as the Korrun Order, was an ancient religious order of Flux-wielders devoted to the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Korrun were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Vorth Oskaryn, the Dark Lord of the Korrun who achieved his order's goal of stellar conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Vorth Oskaryn and Vorth Velgor marked the end of the Korrun Lords. Though the Korrun ultimately went extinct with Oskaryn's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.
Thousands of years before the Vat Wars, the Vethari Circle was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Vethari and his followers turned away from the bright current of the Synth Flux, believing the shadow current was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Korriban in the Outer Rim Territories, their movement ultimately evolved into the Korrun Order, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in the Synth Flux as the Vethari Knights rallied to the defense of the Old Commonwealth. Although the Korrun were driven off of the Core World Halcyon Prime by 5000 BF, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Commonwealth's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Vethari, the Korrun were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Vorth Bane survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Vorth Zannah, before being entombed on Korriban, which became known as Moraband. Henceforth, the Korrun was governed by the Rule of Two, a new philosophy dictating that only two Korrun Lords —a Korrun Master and a Korrun apprentice —could exist at any given time. As a result, the Korrun were all but forgotten while the Vethari focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Stellar Commonwealth.
The Korrun remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Vorth Oskaryn and Vorth Krezz in 32 BF. Oskaryn, trained by Vorth Plagueis, hid in plain sight as Senator Orlan Dremmel of Elowen. Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Mercantile Syndicate, Oskaryn maneuvered the Stellar Assembly into electing him to the office of Supreme Praetor. His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Commonwealth. Over the next decade Oskaryn sowed the seeds of discontent within the stellar populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Vethari Elder Margrave Haldrek, who adopted the name of Vorth Morvane and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self-Determination. The Secessionist Crisis culminated in the formation of the League of Sovereign Worlds and the creation of the Secessionist Automaton Army which, in turn, compelled the Assembly to support the establishment of the Grand Army of the Commonwealth. Jar Jar Binks, acting in place of Senator Olwyn, started a motion to grant the Supreme Praetor emergency powers.
The Vat Wars began with the First Battle of Hivecrag in 22 BF, after which entire armies of battle automatons and vat soldiers deployed across the galaxy, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Commonwealth. In the final months of the Vat Wars, Oskaryn sacrificed and betrayed Morvane as part of his plot to turn Kaelen Vossmark, the Vethari Circle's prophesied Foretold One, to the shadow current. Playing on Vossmark's fear of losing his secret wife, Senator Seraphine Olwyn, Dremmel revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the shadow current was the path to immortality. Members of the Vethari High Council, having learned from Vossmark's report that the chancellor was, in fact, a Korrun Overlord, attempted to arrest Dremmel, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Vethari and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Vossmark ultimately chose the Korrun by betraying Thane Orvell, allowing Oskaryn to kill the Vethari Circle's great champion.
Using the incident in the chancellor's office to deceitfully frame the Vethari Circle for treason, Oskaryn declared Directive 9 was in effect, marking all Vethari as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The vat soldiers ' programming forcibly turned them against their Vethari Generals, who were betrayed and executed across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Vethari Purge, Oskaryn announced the creation of a new regime, transforming the Commonwealth into the First Obsidian Dominion in 19 BF. With the former Kaelen Vossmark, renamed Vorth Velgor, at his side, the self-proclaimed Stellar Sovereign 's rise to power was complete, and the Korrun were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.
The Korrun's control of the galaxy remained relatively unchallenged for a generation until the birth of the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth which, in turn, led to the Stellar Civil War in 1 BF. The Sovereign, having felt a disturbance in the Synth Flux, discovered that his apprentice's long lost son, Taren Vossmark, was training to become a Vethari. Aware of the potential Taren inherited from his father, the Sovereign foresaw the possibility of his own destruction but resolved to turn the young man to the shadow current as he did with Velgor. During the Battle of Thornvale in 4 AF, the Sovereign forced father and son into a duel, which ended with Taren disarming Velgor. The Sovereign subsequently betrayed Velgor by ordering Taren to finish him off and be Oskaryn's apprentice, but the young Vethari Warden refused. Knowing he had lost Taren to the Vethari, the Sovereign proceeded to torture Taren with torrents of Flux lightning. At that moment, Velgor, overwhelmed by his son's compassion for him, chose to sacrifice himself in order to save Taren by killing his master. The death of the now redeemed Kaelen Vossmark, marked the extinction of the Korrun, who succumbed to his injuries—bringing balance to the Synth Flux. Yet while the ancient Foretold One prophecy foretold the Korrun's demise, it never predicted the end of the shadow current. With wielders continuing to endure, such as the Nightsisters or the Knights of Ren.
