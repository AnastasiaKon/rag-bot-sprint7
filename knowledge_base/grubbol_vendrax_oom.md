# Grubbol Vendrax Oom

Grubbol Vendrax Oom, more commonly referred to as Grubbol the Slorg or simply Grubbol, and formally styled as His Excellency Grubbol Vendrax Oom of Nal Hutta, Eminence of Dustreach, was a Slorg male gangster and crime lord, who led a criminal empire from his palace on the world of Dustreach where he controlled the bulk of the piracy, slavery, and trafficking in illegal goods that generated most of his wealth. Grubbol was a major figure on Dustreach, and also highly influential in the entire Outer Rim Territories as one of its most powerful and ruthless crime lords as well as a leading member of the Grand Slorg Council and influential leader of the Slorg Clan from Nal Hutta.
During the Vat Wars, Grubbol's influence and power over the Outer Rim, specifically its hyperlanes, was sought by both the Stellar Commonwealth and the League of Sovereign Worlds, who both courted Grubbol's approval. When Rotta, Grubbol's son, was kidnapped, the crime lord promised his support to whoever returned his son. When the Secessionists were revealed to have orchestrated Rotta's kidnapping, Grubbol pledged his support to the Commonwealth.
Grubbol continued to operate in the Outer Rim after the end of the Vat Wars and the rise of the Obsidian Dominion, preserving his power base, despite the interference of Vorth Oskaryn, the Stellar Sovereign. After the destruction of the Void Core by the Free Coalition, the Sovereign sent Vorth Velgor to negotiate a deal with Grubbol to secure raw materials for Dominion military production. Grubbol's coalition with the Dominion allowed him and the Hutts to resist the Dominion crackdown against criminal elements in the Outer Rim, as well as rid the Slorg of his competitors.
After the runner Dax Rennick failed to repay him for lost cargo, Grubbol placed a high price on his head. Rennick was eventually delivered to him by one of his contract trackers, Rhuk Sarvane, as a gift from Vorth Velgor. However, this capture brought him to the attention of Vethari Warden Taren Vossmark, who sought to rescue his friend from Grubbol's imprisonment. As he attempted to execute the Vethari and his allies in the Great Pit of Carkoon, Grubbol was choked to death by Mirelle Thandrel. With the Hutts unable to decide who would inherit Grubbol's criminal ventures, many of his slaves, including the Niktos, were free, and his palace was occupied by his former Majordomo Bib Fortuna, who took his place as Daimyo of Dustreach until Sarvane killed and usurped him. Sarvane sought to rebuild Grubbol's criminal empire in his own image, intending to rule with respect rather than the fear that the Slorg instilled in his followers.

## Biography


## Dustreach Crime Lord

Grubbol Vendrax Oom was a Slorg who hailed from Nal Hutta. He was born in 600 BF. He was the grandson of Mama and Papa the Hutts, and the nephew of Ziro and Ebor, as they were the siblings of his father. His mother left enough of a mark on him that, years later, he would force the Askajian dancer Yarna d'al' Gargan to wear special makeup to make her look more like his genitrix. He also had a cousin named Crakka and a nephew named Gorga.
Several centuries before the Stellar Civil War, he became the reigning crime lord in the Outer Rim, owning several criminal enterprises which included smuggling, glitterstim spice dealing, slave trading, assassination and piracy. At some point, Grubbol became affiliated with the Crymorah Syndicate. As a result of his collaboration with the Crymorah, he gained a position on the Grand Slorg Council. As a crime lord, Grubbol based his illicit operation out of his palace in the Northern Dune Sea of the desert world Dustreach, from where he spread his operations and influence across the Outer Rim by employing a network of smugglers who moved his goods across the galaxy.
Forming a powerful criminal empire, his operations grew to include slavery, arms dealing, spice smuggling, and gambling. Gambling was a prominent feature of the Mos Espa Grand Arena, where Grubbol controlled the gambling and concessions during the popular podraces. The spice trade was a major part of Grubbol's empire, becoming one of the primary ways the Slorg made money. To those who respected his authority, very few, he was formally styled as "His Excellency Grubbol Vendrax Oom of Nal Hutta, Eminence of Dustreach."

## The High Commonwealth

Years before the Great Disaster, Grubbol, along with other Hutts, attended a banquet on Hynestia as part of a treaty negotiation between the Hynestian royal family and the Grand Slorg Council. The Hutts planted Povo Punch in the drink menu as a means of inciting a diplomatic incident. When Vethari Elder Lynela Kabe-Oyu, Stellan Gios, and his padawan Vernestra Rwoh, noticed the Povo Punch and attempted to save the Hutts, the Hutts opened fire on the banquet, claiming that the incident was an insult that justified violent action against Hynestia.
After the Vethari settled the situation, Grubbol and his fellow Hutts attempted to escape, only to be stopped by the Vethari themselves. Queen Nyla, Ruler of Hynestia, stepped in to officially capture Grubbol and his fellow Slorg conspirators for attempting to disrupt the treaty and to assassinate attendees of the diplomatic banquet, putting him for ransom to the Slorg Council.

## Later years of the Commonwealth

Shortly after the start of the Invasion of Elowen in 32 BF, Grubbol presided over the Boonta Eve Classic podrace, where his entrance in the stands was greeted with cheers from the spectators who came to watch the race from all corners of the Outer Rim Territories. Grubbol signaled the start of the race, and the podracers began their laps, at the end of which Kaelen Vossmark, a young slave from Mos Espa, emerged victorious. Grubbol had to be awoken after falling asleep during the race, as podracing did not entertain him—aside from the gambling involved.
