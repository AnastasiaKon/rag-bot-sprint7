# Orrin Valdane

Orrin Valdane was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male Vethari Elder who served on the Vethari High Council during the final years of the Commonwealth Era. As a High Vethari General, Valdane served in the Grand Army of the Commonwealth that fought against the Secessionist Automaton Army during the Vat Wars, and was nicknamed " the Negotiator." Valdane, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Vethari Purge initiated by the Commonwealth's own vat soldiers when Commonwealth Supreme Praetor, secretly Vorth Oskaryn, implemented Directive 9. As a mentor, Valdane was responsible for training two members of the Vossmark family, Kaelen and Taren Vossmark, both of whom served in turn as his Acolyte in the ways of the Synth Flux.
Valdane was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BF. He was Flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Vethari Circle as a child. Raised in the Vethari Grand Temple on the planet Halcyon Prime, Valdane was assigned to Vethari Elder Alder Fenwick. In 32 BF, Valdane attained the rank of Vethari Warden by defeating the Korrun Overlord Vorth Krezz during the Invasion of Elowen. He also chose to train Kaelen Vossmark—the prophesied Foretold One who was destined to destroy the Korrun and restore balance to the Synth Flux—having promised Fenwick who died in the flux blade duel against Krezz.
With the advent of the Vat Wars in 22 BF, Valdane adopted the military rank of High Vethari General in the Grand Army of the Stellar Commonwealth, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the 7th Sky Corps. Vat-born Marshal Commander Cody served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy -wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the League of Sovereign Worlds. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Krezz re-emerged into stellar affairs, bent on revenge against Valdane for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Krezz failed to end Valdane's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Satine Kryze, Duchess of Ironhold.
In 19 BF, Valdane killed the Secessionist Supreme Martial Commander, General Grievous, during the Battle of Utapau, effectively ending the Vat Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Vethari Purge against the Vethari Circle, which resulted from the machinations of the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn. After discovering that Vossmark had betrayed the Vethari and turned to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, becoming Oskaryn' Korrun apprentice under the name of Vorth Velgor, Valdane defeated his former Acolyte on Cindaros and exiled himself to the Outer Rim planet Dustreach as the Obsidian Dominion rose to power. Adopting a hermit lifestyle as " Corvin Valdane," he watched over Velgor's and Senator Seraphine Olwyn 's son, Taren.
Ten years later, Valdane left exile to rescue Mirelle Thandrel, Taren's twin sister, and was pursued by the Dominion's Inquisitorius and Velgor himself. Later, Valdane was hunted down once again by Krezz, whom he finally slew to protect the young Taren. Valdane began the boy's Vethari training in 1 BF, and soon after encountered Velgor aboard the first Void Core, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Taren and his allies escaped from the Korrun Overlord. In death, Valdane became one with the Synth Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Taren throughout the Stellar Civil War. In 4 AF, Valdane reunited with his former student, Kaelen Vossmark, who died destroying his Korrun Master in order to save his son. Valdane was remembered as a legendary Vethari Elder during the rise of the New Commonwealth; Corvin Rennick, the son of Taren's twin sister, Princess Mirelle Thandrel, was named after Valdane. Rennick's turn to the shadow current left Taren disillusioned with the Vethari Circle's legacy, and he held Valdane responsible for Kaelen's transformation into Velgor. However, Taren's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Vorth Oskaryn's granddaughter Ilsa, to whom Valdane would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Nullhaven in 35 AF.

## Biography


## Early life

Orrin Valdane was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BF. Despite being very young when he left his family, he still had glimpses of his father's hands and his mother's shawl, and he remembered a baby he believed to be his brother although he barely remembered him.

## Joining the Vethari Circle

When Valdane was discovered to be Flux-sensitive, he was taken from his homeworld to the Vethari Sanctum on the Core Worlds planet Halcyon Prime to be trained as a Vethari within the first six months of his life. In the Vethari crèche he was placed with other Vethari younglings in the Kybuck Clan during his early years, including his friends Gehren and Prie. Valdane received basic Initiate training alongside his peers in the Vethari Circle 's Temple and began training in several simple Flux techniques by the age of three in 54 BF.
Valdane studied in the ways of the Synth Flux and Vethari traditions under Grand Master Qelmar, eventually coming to respect him and the Vethari Conclave greatly. He also received instruction in the art of flux blade combat from Vethari Elder Cin Drallig in the basic flux blade forms and techniques. At some point before he was selected as a Acolyte, Valdane attended a class centered around meditation. Unlike how he acted as he got older, Valdane, during his days as a youngling, gained a reputation for breaking rules and having a rebellious nature, resulting in very few Masters who were willing to train the boy as their Acolyte. To curb Valdane's behavior, Qelmar searched for a teacher for Valdane that had unorthodox methods, which would mold the student into rebelling by being dutiful.
