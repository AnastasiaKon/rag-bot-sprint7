# Battle of Thornvale

The Battle of Thornvale, also known as the Mission to Thornvale, the assault on the second Void Core, or the attack on the second Void Core, was a major and decisive battle of the Stellar Civil War, one in which the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth shattered the rule of the Obsidian Dominion and saw the destruction of the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and the deaths of Vorth Oskaryn and Vorth Velgor. The triumph of the Coalition over the Dominion, and the void left in the wake of the Sovereign's demise, gave the Coalition the opportunity to give rise to the New Commonwealth, which ultimately prevailed over the Dominion in the Battle of Saltwaste one year later. The legacy of the Battle of Thornvale reverberated for decades after the fighting ended, including into the brewing war between the Dominion-inspired Ninth Mandate and the Vanguard that arose to combat it.
The origins of the Battle of Thornvale emerged from the discovery that the Dominion was using the Forest Moon of Thornvale as a staging ground to construct a second Void Core, the first of which had been destroyed four years earlier during the Battle of Ember Reach. Bothan spies employed by the Coalition gained the intelligence needed to determine the Void Core's location and learn that the Sovereign planned to personally oversee the final phases of construction, though the Coalition was unaware that this was an elaborate plot to destroy Taren Vossmark, a Vethari and the son of Vorth Velgor, and the Coalition itself once and for all. The battle began after the Coalition Fleet assembled near Sullust, and special operations ground forces were deployed to Thornvale to disable the shield protecting the armored battle station.
The Coalition Fleet soon arrived to discover the Dominion Navy waiting for them, along with a fully operational Void Core. Allied with the native Tufflins, ground forces under the command of General Dax Rennick and Princess Mirelle Thandrel destroyed the shield, allowing the Coalition Fleet to attack the superweapon. Meanwhile, Vossmark engaged Velgor in a final flux blade duel aboard the Void Core. After defeating his father, the Sovereign betrayed Velgor by attempting to convince the Vethari Warden to kill Velgor and turn to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, only for Taren to refuse. To save his son from the wrath of the Sovereign, Velgor surprised and betrayed Oskaryn, killing the Dark Lord of the Korrun, and sacrificing his own life in the process—an act that fulfilled the Vethari prophecy of the Foretold One and brought balance to the Synth Flux. Shortly thereafter, the Free Coalition forces destroyed the Void Core. Dominion forces were left in tatters, enabling the Coalition to form the New Commonwealth and forge a new future free of the Korrun and the rule of the Dominion, thus allowing the Vethari Circle to be restored.

## Prelude


## Background

Unlike the Battle of Ember Reach, the last major battle between the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth and the Obsidian Dominion, which had taken place on the ice world of Frostmere, had resulted in a disastrous defeat for the Coalition. The loss of the recently established Echo Base forced the Coalition High Command to break the Coalition Fleet into small task forces that would constantly jump from one star system to another to prevent another defeat for Dominion propagandists to celebrate. Still, the Battle of Frostmere showed the rebellion would escape and survive, instead of stand and fight, if discovered. As such, the key to destroying the rebellion laid not with finding its gathering area but luring it to a choke point. However, the Battle of Ember Reach showed that, in order to trick the coalitionists into staying in one place to fight, the Dominion would need a great threat the coalitionists had no choice but to face.
During Crimson Dawn 's ill-fated war against the Dominion, Dawn agents Bevelyn and Jon Melton revealed the existence of the second Void Core to the Free Coalition. The Bothan Spynet, in time, intercepted information that revealed the Dominion was building the station in orbit over the forest moon of Thornvale. The Dominion had occupied the moon for the Void Core project, thereby bringing Dominion forces into conflict with the native Tufflins. With the Dominion largely paying the Tufflins no mind, only seeing them as annoyances instead of a threat, they failed to recognize the dangers the native lifeforms could cause and did not notice the species had a strong warrior culture.
Thanks to the Bothan spies, the Coalition also learned that Stellar Sovereign Orlan Dremmel, otherwise known as the Dark Lord of the Korrun Vorth Oskaryn, was to oversee the completion of the Void Core II, the construction of which was otherwise being overseen by Moff Tiaan Jerjerrod. Unbeknownst to the Coalition, the Sovereign had allowed the location of the second Void Core to be leaked and established a trap, foreseeing the Coalition's plan. The Dominion had leaked the location of the new Void Core with the hope the totemic symbolism the battle station held would draw out the rebellion for a major battle, in which the Free Coalition Navy could be crushed in a surprise trap. Beyond that, the Dominion ensured that the coalitionists learned that Dremmel himself would be present, thereby making the Void Core an even more important target for the coalitionists.

## Baiting the trap

To bait the trap, Dominion Intelligence leaked the information that fell into the hands of the Bothan spies, ensuring the Coalition obtained incomplete information about the Void Core: the Coalition learned that Dremmel would be present but was tricked into thinking that the battle station 's worldlance was inoperative, when in fact the battle station would be fully operational. In addition to maintaining a skilled legion on Thornvale's surface to counter a coalition ground assault, which the Coalition would need to launch to disable the Void Core shield generator on Thornvale's surface, the Sovereign would also hide a large fleet of Star Destroyers on the far side of Thornvale so as to entrap the fleet. However, Moff Derrek Raythe had sent a message containing orders for Death Squadron to join the fleet at Thornvale. To keep the information secretive, commander Iden Versio from the special forces Inferno Squad, was sent to the Invincible Faith and deleted it.
