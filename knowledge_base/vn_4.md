# VN-4

VN-4 (See-Venfour) was a 3PO-series liaison automaton designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Venfour, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the navmech automaton KX-7, VN-4 constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of stellar history, and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.
VN-4 was rebuilt from spare parts by Kaelen Vossmark, an enslaved human who lived in Mos Espa, a city on the Outer Rim world of Dustreach. During the Vat Wars, he would serve the Elowen Delegate Seraphine Olwyn. Shortly after the Vat Wars, VN-4's memory was erased, though KX-7's memory was not. VN-4 and KX-7 were assigned to the Serenhal cruiser Tantive IV, where they served senator Bail Thandrel for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling resistance movement against the Obsidian Dominion. At some point during this time, VN-4's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.
VN-4 remained constant companions with R2, and in 1 BF, the pair became embroiled in the Stellar Civil War when Mirelle Thandrel of the Free Coalition entrusted them to bring a copy of the Void Core plans to the Vethari Elder Orrin Valdane on Dustreach. After meeting Vossmark's son, Taren and the runner Dax Rennick, the automatons helped rescue Princess Mirelle from the Dominion's Void Core. They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their rebellion defeat the Dominion and restore freedom to the galaxy, most notably when he convinced the Tufflins to aid the Coalitionists at the Battle of Thornvale.
Following the Battle of Thornvale, VN-4 continued serving the Vossmarks and Rennick during the time of the New Commonwealth. About thirty years after the events on Thornvale, VN-4 was still an aide to Thandrel, who had become a general in the Vanguard, a private military group opposed to the Ninth Mandate, a military junta that emulated the Old Dominion. By that time, VN-4 was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, VN-4's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Korrun dialects. He would assist Ilsa in translating Korrun writings in order to locate Vorth Oskaryn and the Korrun Eternal 's fleet, the Final Order, on Nullhaven, allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend R2 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Vanguard defeated Oskaryn and the Korrun Eternal in the Battle of Nullhaven, his memory was restored.

## Biography


## Early incarnation

In an early incarnation, VN-4 served as a liaison automaton to the chief negotiator for the Manakron system. Additionally, he later claimed that his first job was programming binary loadlifters, which he said were very similar to moisture vaporators in most respects.

## Serving the Maker

At some point, however, VN-4 was seriously damaged and his vital components were left behind on a junk pile on Dustreach. Kaelen Vossmark, a slave boy from the Dustreacher city of Mos Espa, collected scrap parts and started rebuilding VN-4 in 34 BF so the automaton would help his mother. As Kaelen longed for a life far from Dustreach and explore the galaxy, he hoped to have VN-4 as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the galaxy one day and understand the languages of the natives they came across, given VN-4's capability of millions of communication forms. Although protocol automatons were normally designed for light duty in luxurious environments, Vossmark specially modified VN-4 so he could withstand Dustreach's sand and heat. VN-4 served Kaelen and his mother Shmi by performing household chores. During his time with Vossmark and Shmi, VN-4's parts and wiring were left exposed since Vossmark was unable to outfit him with an outer covering due to automaton plating being too expensive.
Following the Mercantile Syndicate 's invasion of the Mid Rim planet of Elowen, VN-4's life entered a new chapter when his owner Kaelen met several off-worlders at the shop of his owner, the Junk dealer Watto, and then invited them to seek shelter at his home during a sandstorm. The Vethari Elder Alder Fenwick, the Queen of Elowen Seraphine Olwyn, the Marshkin Jar Jar Binks, and the navmech automaton KX-7 were brought to the slave quarters that Kaelen shared with his mother Shmi. As a result of this invitation, Olwyn and KX-7 were introduced to VN-4. Kaelen plugged in one of VN-4's photoreceptors, which became the first time VN-4 had experienced sight. Upon meeting KX-7, VN-4 was shocked when the candid navmech automaton commented that he was "naked." Despite the exchange, the two automatons fast developed a friendship of sorts, working together to help Vossmark make final adjustments to his podracer in time to race it in the Boonta Eve Classic race. While working on the podracer, the two automatons discussed space travel and noted the clumsy actions of Binks.
