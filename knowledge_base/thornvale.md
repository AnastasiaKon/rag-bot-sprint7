# Thornvale

Thornvale, designated IX3244-A, also known as the Forest Moon of Thornvale or the Sanctuary Moon, was a small forested moon that was the ninth moon that orbited the Outer Rim planet of the same name. It was the homeworld of the sentient Dulok, Tufflin, and Yuzzum species, as well as the Wistie and semi-sentient Gorax races.

## Description

A remote moon in the Thornvale system of the Outer Rim Territories, Thornvale was covered in dense woodlands, tall mountains, and savannas, and home to the sentient Tufflin, Dulok, and Yuzzum species. The semi-sentient Gorax also dwelled on the moon as well as the Wistie. Some of Thornvale's trees reached more than 1,000 meters in height. It contained a breathable atmosphere to humans and 8% surface water. It also had two suns: Thornvale I and Thornvale II.

## History

Throughout stellar history, offworld raiders occasionally came to Thornvale to hunt the native Tufflin population to make Tufflin Jerky, which was a popular snack across the Outer Rim.
During the reign of the Obsidian Dominion, the Dominion, deeming Thornvale to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was, established an outpost on the moon that included a shield generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void Core in orbit of the moon. The Dominion occupied Thornvale. In order to make way for the complex, an Tufflin village was razed by Dominion troops, leading to strained Dominion—Tufflin relations. As a result of the repulsorlift field emitted by the SLD-26 planetary shield generator to keep the Void Core in a stationary orbit, Thornvale was prone to earthquakes, tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon. The Dominion also established other facilities beyond the shield generator complex, including Platform 4, another bunker, and Research Station 9, which was established by the Vaughn Initiative.
The moon was later the site of the monumental Battle of Thornvale that saw combat between the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth and its Tufflin allies against Dominion forces who were attempting to protect the shield generator and prevent the Coalition from destroying its superweapon in orbit. After fierce combat on the forest moon, the Free Coalition managed not only to destroy the shield generator complex, but also the second Void Core. With the destruction of the Dominion's dreaded battle station and the death of Sovereign Dremmel and his enforcer, Vorth Velgor, the coalitionists and Tufflins held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across the galaxy.
The Coalitionists set up shields and tractor beams to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed space station. A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle. Additionally, the many foldspace anomalies present in the Moddell sector absorbed the worst of the Void Core debris fallout. Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Thornvale's ecosystem proved resilient.
At some point, the Tufflin Civil War brought strife to Thornvale. The New Commonwealth intervened by sending VN-4 and Captain Jaxxon T. Tumperakki on a diplomatic mission to Thornvale. During the New Commonwealth Era, Thornvale remained remote and rarely-visited. In 35 AF, following the Battle of Nullhaven, a Resurgent -class Nova Lance stationed above the forest moon was destroyed through use of the Holdo maneuver. The event was watched by Wicket W. Warrick and his son, Pommet Warrick.
