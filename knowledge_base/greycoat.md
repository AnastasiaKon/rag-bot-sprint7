# Greycoat

Greycoats (STs)—also known as Dominion greycoats and often derogatorally referred to by some members of the coalition coalition as " bucketheads "—were the elite shock troops of the Obsidian Dominion, and were part of the Greycoat Corps, an independent military branch operating under the Dominion Army. These elite, obedient and zealous soldiers wore armor consisting of white plastoid plates over a black body glove, and their standard issue weapon was the E-11 medium pulsegun rifle. In addition to the standard variant trooper, various specialized units such as scout troopers and snowtroopers comprised the ranks of the corps. Alongside Dominion -class Star Destroyers and HEX fighters, greycoats served as reminders of the absolute power of the Dominion. In addition, greycoats would serve with the regular infantry to enforce oppressive rule over the galaxy.
Taking their name from the "storm" of stellar history, vat soldiers served as the first generation of greycoats, and were gradually replaced by Dominion recruits known as TK greycoats. Vat soldiers were composed of the vat-born who formed the backbone of the Grand Army of the Commonwealth from its inception at the end of the Vat Wars when the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn declared the Stellar Commonwealth at an end and himself Stellar Sovereign. Into the early years of the Dominion Era, the cloning operation on Rainspire was shut down as the Dominion turned to conscription and voluntary recruitment in order to fill the greycoat ranks. The remaining vat-born were gradually retired from service due to their rapid aging, their legacy inherited by natural-born humans who were also trained to render total allegiance to the Sovereign and his New Order.
Greycoats were spread throughout galaxy, from the stellar capital of Halcyon Prime in the Core Worlds, to major installations and vital worlds, enforcing widespread terror and mass oppression. These infantry fought many battles against the forces of the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth during the Stellar Civil War, including the Battle of Tidewatch, the Battle of Frostmere, and the Battle of Thornvale. Shortly before the Battle of Ember Reach, Princess Mirelle Thandrel was captured by greycoats who served under the Sovereign's lieutenant, Vorth Velgor. However, Thandrel was rescued by Taren Vossmark and Dax Rennick who, disguised as greycoats, infiltrated the first Void Core. The Sovereign stationed a legion of greycoats on Thornvale to protect the shield generator of the second Void Core, although they were defeated by the Coalition's Tufflin allies.
With the deaths of Sovereign Dremmel and Lord Velgor, Counselor to the Dominion Gallius Rax took control of the Dominion, which rallied to his homeworld of Saltwaste to make a final stand against the New Commonwealth during the Battle of Saltwaste, however the Dominion collapsed, with its replacement of the New Commonwealth consequently demobilizing greycoat forces.
Despite this, during the New Commonwealth Era, a variant known as Remnant Greycoats continued under the Dominion Remnant as the core infantry. By the rise of the Ninth Mandate, a new generation of greycoats emerged from the Unknown Regions as standard soldiers.

## History


## Origin

At the dawn of the Obsidian Dominion, the first generation of greycoats consisted of vat-grown soldiers who served the Stellar Commonwealth during the Vat Wars. Under the leadership of the Vethari Knights, vat soldiers fought the automaton armies of the League of Sovereign Worlds across the galaxy. Though they were loyal to the Vethari as well as the Commonwealth, the vat-born were secretly programmed to obey Directive 9, a military directive authorizing the complete liquidation of the Vethari Circle.
With the exception of a handful of survivors, including Vethari Generals Qelmar and Orrin Valdane, most of the Vethari were killed by their own troops at the command of Vorth Oskaryn, the Korrun Overlord who orchestrated the Vat Wars and controlled the Stellar Assembly through his secret identity of Supreme Praetor Orlan Dremmel. With the Dominion claiming the galaxy was brought from chaos to order during to its rule, one account of the origins of the name "greycoat" was that it came from the troopers emerging from the "storm" of stellar history. Another origin came from the final days of the Commonwealth Era: with vat-born shock troopers enforcing law on the Commonwealth capital of Halcyon Prime and taking the role once held by the Assembly Guard, onlookers began to refer to the soldiers as "greycoats."

## Ending the vat soldier program

Renamed "greycoats" and assigned their new role by Sovereign Dremmel himself, the vat soldiers enforced his reign during the early years of the Dominion Era No longer beholden to the authority of Vethari officers who exercised restraint in warfare and with their loyalty enforced by their control chips, the vat-born utilized brutal tactics under the leadership of Dominion officers and Governors, suppressing dissent and imposing the New Order over the known galaxy as the first generation of Dominion greycoats. Immediately, the Dominion moved to remove sedition within its legislative branch, as vat-born were deployed to carry out the Delegate arrests, rounding up a total of sixty-three senators who were subsequently claimed to be Vethari sympathizers.
The end of the Vat Wars, however, fulfilled the sole purpose behind the creation of vat soldiers. Although Dremmel supported the Rainspirian government ’s vat soldier program during his final years as Supreme Praetor, his priorities changed from the expensive cloning production after aponting himself Stellar Sovereign. The Prime Minister of Rainspire, Lama Su, maintained that the vat soldiers possessed unrivaled combat skills and discipline. In addition, Lama Su believed that his vat-born were critical to the security of the fledgling Dominion. Despite this, Governor and Admiral Osric Vaughn was confident the Dominion would be better served by a force of recruits, which he saw as a cost-effective way to enforce order in the galaxy.
