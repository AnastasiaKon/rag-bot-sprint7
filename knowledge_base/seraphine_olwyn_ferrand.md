# Seraphine Olwyn Ferrand

Seraphine Olwyn Ferrand, also simply known as Seraphine Olwyn, was a human female politician who represented the people of Elowen during the final years of the Stellar Commonwealth. Prior to her career as a senator in the Stellar Assembly, Olwyn was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Elowen. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Vat Wars. However, her secret marriage to the Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.
Born in 46 BF during the Age of the Commonwealth, Seraphine Ferrand was raised by her parents, Ruwee Ferrand and his wife Jobal, on the peaceful Mid Rim world of Elowen. Despite the humble background of the Ferrand family, Seraphine was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Olwyn" in 32 BF. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Senator Orlan Dremmel, Olwyn's reign was put to the test by the Mercantile Syndicate 's blockade and subsequent invasion of Elowen. Unable to fight the Federation's army of battle automatons without assistance, the queen beseeched the Stellar Assembly on Halcyon Prime for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Vethari Circle and the Marshkin Grand Army, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Finis Valorum enabled Dremmel to become the Supreme Praetor.
Following her reign, Olwyn joined the Assembly and struggled to resolve the Secessionist Crisis through diplomacy, becoming a leading voice in the Loyalist Committee by 22 BF. After surviving an assassination attempt planned by the Mercantile Syndicate, she was removed from the capital and sent back to Elowen under the protection of Acolyte Kaelen Vossmark. Though the Vethari Code forbade personal relationships, deeming attachment, and possession unbecoming of a Vethari, the two gradually became closer as their feelings for each other grew. In the aftermath of the First Battle of Hivecrag, Olwyn secretly married her Vethari protector, despite her own reservations about their relationship and the consequences they would face if the truth ever came to light. Their subsequent interactions were limited throughout the Vat Wars, with Olwyn continuing to advocate peaceful solutions in the Assembly while Vossmark served on the front lines as a Vethari General in the Grand Army of the Commonwealth.
In the waning months of the pan-stellar conflict, Olwyn revealed to her husband that she was pregnant. As a result of Vossmark's fear of loss, he was seduced by the shadow current of the Synth Flux, believing that by becoming a Korrun Overlord he would gain the power to prevent his wife from dying in childbirth. Olwyn witnessed Sovereign Dremmel's declaration that her fears had come true; the Commonwealth had become the Obsidian Dominion. After she learned of her husband's transformation into a Korrun, she confronted him on Cindaros in the hopes that he had not truly changed, but he instead attempted to kill her. She ultimately died of a broken heart after giving birth to the twins Taren Vossmark and Mirelle Olwyn Vossmark. With her dying breath, she insisted that there was still good in the former Kaelen Vossmark, who embraced the persona of Vorth Velgor throughout the reign of the Dominion.
Olwyn's children played a major role in the fragmentation of the Dominion and the redemption of her husband. Like her mother, Mirelle entered public service and became a flag-bearer for the democratic ideals of liberty and justice as a leader in the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth and its successor, the New Commonwealth, before becoming the founding general of the Vanguard against the Ninth Mandate —a successor state to the Obsidian Dominion. Taren, on the other hand, was trained in the ways of the Vethari like his father before him and was a celebrated war hero of the Free Coalition. Through Taren's efforts to redeem his father back to the light, Olwyn's last words proved true when Kaelen killed the Sovereign during the Battle of Thornvale.

## Biography


## Early life

A human female of the Ferrand family, the young woman best remembered as Seraphine Olwyn was born Seraphine Ferrand on the bountiful planet of Elowen in 46 BF. She was the younger daughter of Ruwee and Jobal Ferrand and the sister of Sola Ferrand. Her father was a friend of Senator Onaconda Farr, who represented the solar system of Rodia in the Assembly of the Stellar Commonwealth. Ferrand was very fond of the Rodian, whom she referred to as "Uncle Ono."
As a child, she baked every day and traveled with her father on relief missions to offer aid to suffering planets. When Ferrand was seven, she traveled with her father and Farr to Shadda-Bi-Boran to help relocate the population in the wake of an ecological disaster. Unfortunately, the effort resulted in failure, as the Shadda-Bi-Boran could not adapt to their new homeworld. It led her to declare later in life that she would try 10,000 times in order to save one person.

## Rise to queenship

Although her parents were of modest origins, Ferrand was identified as a gifted and brilliant young girl at an early age. She devoted herself to public service, joining the Apprentice Legislature at the tender age of eight, and she eventually became a junior senatorial adviser. She attended the Theed Royal Academy to learn how to become a political leader.
