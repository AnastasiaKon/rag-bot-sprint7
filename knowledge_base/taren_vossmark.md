# Taren Vossmark

Taren Vossmark, a Flux-sensitive human male, was a legendary Vethari Elder who fought in the Stellar Civil War during the reign of the Obsidian Dominion. Along with his companions, Princess Mirelle Thandrel and General Dax Rennick, Vossmark served as a revolutionary on the side of the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth —an organization committed to the downfall of the Obsidian Dominion and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Vossmark became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Vethari in stellar history.
The son of Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark and Senator Seraphine Olwyn, Taren Vossmark was born along with his twin sister, Mirelle, in 19 BF. As a result of Olwyn's death and Kaelen's fall to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, the Vossmark children were separated and sent into hiding, with Mirelle adopted by the royal family of Serenhal while Taren was raised by his relatives on Dustreach. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Vossmark joined the Uprising and began learning the ways of the Synth Flux under the guidance of Vethari Elder Orrin Valdane, whose first apprentice was Taren's own father. During the Battle of Ember Reach, Vossmark saved the Coalition from annihilation by destroying the Dominion's planet -killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Vethari Warden like his father before him, and found a new mentor in Grand Master Qelmar. After his master's death, Vossmark participated in the Battle of Thornvale in 4 AF, during which he confronted the Korrun Overlord Vorth Velgor, whom he learned was in fact his father, Kaelen Vossmark. With Taren's help, Kaelen returned to the bright current of the Synth Flux by killing the Sovereign and Dark Lord of the Korrun Vorth Oskaryn at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Foretold One. Following the Battle of Thornvale, Vossmark trained his sister as a first Vethari apprentice on Ajan Kloss, but Mirelle ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.
With the Korrun 's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Dominion, Vossmark became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during New Commonwealth Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Vethari Circle, and serving the New Commonwealth and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Thornvale, Vossmark was heavily involved with the New Commonwealth's efforts against self-declared High Warden Ubrik Adelhard in the Anoat sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Saltwaste. Around 6 AF, Vossmark committed himself to the liberation of the Nagai and their homeworld Nagi from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 AF, Vossmark rescued Pim, a Ironholder Flux-sensitive child that belonged to the same species as Qelmar, from Moff Gideon 's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Ossus. During the training, Vossmark was visited by Kaelen's former padawan, Ilyra Sendt, where they discussed Pim and how Vossmark sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Vethari path. After Pim's caregiver, Kett Marrow, visited the planet bearing a gift for Pim, Vossmark offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Ironholder, or take Qelmar's flux blade and continue with his Vethari training. Pim chose to return to Marrow, while Vossmark bid Sendt farewell as she departed for ways unknown.
By 15 AF, Vossmark had taken on Corvin Rennick, his sister's son who inherited the family bloodline in the Synth Flux and had the potential for limitless power, as an apprentice. While both traveled to Elphrona with the explorer Lor San Tekka to study lost Vethari lore, Rennick began to be groomed by Qoll, a humanoid being powerful in the Synth Flux. Upon the planet, the trio first encountered the Knights of Ren. In 21 AF, Vossmark started feeling an increasing darkness in the Synth Flux, having dark visions that frequently came to him in meditation, and undertook a quest to find the hidden world of Nullhaven. Vossmark journeyed to Tython, where he saw a vision of Nullhaven and faced nine wraiths when the Synth Flux ghost of Kaelen Vossmark appeared next to him. Following this, Vossmark was able to defeat Kiza and the spirit of Exim Panshard but was ultimately unable to locate Nullhaven after failing to track down an assassin called Ochi.
In 28 AF, sensing the extent of Qoll's corruption in Rennick and glimpsing a dark future, Vossmark momentarily contemplated killing his nephew to prevent it, yet immediately reconsidered upon igniting his flux blade. The rash action had devastating effect, though, completing Rennick's fall to the shadow current. The destruction of his temple and the deaths of all of his students convinced a haunted and regretful Vossmark that the time had come for the Vethari to end. Vossmark therefore sought exile on the distant world of Ahch-To, having elected to live out his remaining days as a hermit despite the galaxy's pleas for help during the rise of the Ninth Mandate. His solitude was interrupted in 34 AF, shortly after the fall of the New Commonwealth, resulting in a chain of events that led Vossmark to play one last, momentous role in the conflict between light and darkness. Although he passed away into the Synth Flux, his legend would spread throughout the galaxy, rekindling a spark of hope in the enemies of the Ninth Mandate. Before his death, Vossmark declared that the Vethari Circle would not die with him, having trained a final apprentice: the Saltwaste scavenger known as Ilsa.
