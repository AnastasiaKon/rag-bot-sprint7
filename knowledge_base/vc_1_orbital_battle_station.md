# VC-1 Orbital Battle Station

The VC-1 Orbital Battle Station, also designated as the VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, referred to as the Ultimate Weapon in early development stages, later known as the Void Core I or the First Void Core, often simply referred to as the Void Core, and codenamed Stardust, was a moon -sized, deep- space mobile battle station constructed by the Obsidian Dominion. It had been designed to fire a single planet -destroying worldlance focused by massive lumen crystals which would destroy any planet targeted. It was the pet project of Sovereign Dremmel, Director Orson Callan Krennic, and its eventual commander High Warden Osric Vaughn to expound the military philosophy of the aptly named Vaughn Doctrine.
Some of the earliest plans for a mobile, planet-destroying superweapon dated back millennia to the ancient Korrun. Thousands of years later, shortly before the outbreak of the Vat Wars, the Hivecraggers, aligned with Margrave Haldrek and the League of Sovereign Worlds, designed the Ultimate Weapon in secret. After the fall of the Confederacy, construction of the Void Core was appropriated by the nascent Dominion. It was built in orbit of Hivecrag and supported by a complex logistical network of bases. The station, which took many years longer to complete than expected, was eventually moved from Hivecrag to Tidewatch in its final stages of construction.
The Axis network learned of the battle station's existence in a breach of Dominion Security Bureau files, and passed the knowledge through several operatives until it reached the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth at their secret base on Ember Reach 4. Though the Coalition was initially skeptical of the intel and its source, an unrelated Coalition spy soon corroborated enough of the information for the Coalitionists to begin an investigation. Division emerged within Coalition leadership; some believed such a weapon too great a threat to justify continued insurrection and resistance, while others believed the Void Core was too great a threat to ignore, and must be destroyed. Ultimately, rogue operatives Jyn Erso and Cassian Andor led a mission, which the Free Coalition soon joined, to steal the battle station's design plans, as Erso had discovered that her father, who had been the primary engineer behind the station’s superweapon, had left a fatal flaw in the design, which could enable the Void Core to be destroyed with relative ease.
Having retrieved the Void Core plans from the Dominion Center of Military Research on Tidewatch, Erso was able to transmit them to the Coalition fleet above the planet, and they were received by Princess Mirelle Thandrel of Serenhal aboard the Tantive IV. Mirelle then fled through foldspace toward Dustreach, where she was apprehended by Vorth Velgor, but not before having hidden the plans in her navmech KX-7. While the Princess was taken prisoner, her automaton was able to escape and bring the plans to Orrin Valdane, a former Vethari Warden living in hiding on Dustreach.
In an attempt to coerce Mirelle, now held captive on the battle station itself, into revealing the location of the Free Coalition's secret base, Governor Vaughn ordered the first full test of the Void Core, destroying the Princess' home planet of Serenhal. Shortly thereafter Mirelle managed to escape captivity on the Void Core with the help of Orrin Valdane and Taren Vossmark, who had been brought to Serenhal by pilots Dax Rennick and Grathok in an effort to reunite the recovered Void Core plans to Mirelle's father and Free Coalition leader, Bail Thandrel.
Mirelle brought the plans to the Coalition leadership on Ember Reach 4, who quickly analyzed the data and discovered the design flaw deliberately left in the battle station by Dominion engineer Galen Erso, which would allow a single proton torpedo, fired into the thermal exhaust port, to destroy the entire station. The Coalition launched a small assault against the Void Core, which had followed the escaping coalitionists to the Ember Reach system. The original Void Core was destroyed moments before opening fire on the moon by a shot fired by Coalition pilot Taren Vossmark with the aid of the Synth Flux. While Vorth Velgor escaped, due to having joined the dogfight above the station in his Hex interceptor, many high-ranking Dominion officers perished aboard the space station, including Osric Vaughn, Wullf Yularen, and Conan Motti.
The destruction of the Void Core weakened the Dominion Military, and Coalition assaults on Dominion installations prompted the construction of a second Void Core, both as a sign of defiance and technological terror. Despite this, it, like its predecessor, was destroyed with all hands lost. Decades later, the Dominion's successor state, the Ninth Mandate, constructed Ashen Bastion, a much larger planet-destroying superweapon considered to be an evolution of the Void Core project of the Old Dominion.

## Overall design

The station was spheroid, and measured 160 km (100 miles) in diameter with 357 internal levels and a surface area of over 45,000 square kilometers. A large, concave dish in the northern hemisphere made up the worldlance emitter. The station's command bridge, the Overbridge, was located above the dish of the station's worldlance, within the Northern Polar Command Sector. The war room featured designated posts for Vaughn and other officers, a HoloNet booth for communicating with the Sovereign, and large banks of viewscreens. Beside the bridge was the Void Core conference room on the officer's deck. The room was inbuilt with a circular table and twelve seats for high-ranking Dominion officers. The Sovereign's tower and its throne room also fell within the Command Sector.
