# Murkwell

Murkwell was a remote, unexplored planet in the Murkwell system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the Synth Flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Vethari Grand Master Qelmar during his exile after the destruction of the Vethari Circle. Taren Vossmark received advanced training in the ways of the Synth Flux under Vethari Elder Qelmar on Murkwell, and it was later the place of Qelmar's death and transformation into the Synth Flux.

## Description

Murkwell was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Tash species and numerous creatures—such as bogwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, sleens, vine snakes, and swamp slugs —living on world. Vethari Elder Qelmar would also make a home for himself on Murkwell during his exile. Murkwell was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Murkwell were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, gnarltree, and yarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.
Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the Synth Flux, it shared a mysterious time -related quality with such locales as Mortis and Ahch-To. The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the shadow current of the Synth Flux on Murkwell. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Murkwell until Qelmar's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the bright current of the Synth Flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Dominion Era, Murkwell's ecosystem saw balance until Qelmar's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Murkwell once more.
Murkwell had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.

## History


## Commonwealth Era

During the High Commonwealth Era, an academic paper was written that described Murkwell as a world strong in the Synth Flux.
During the Commonwealth Era, Halka Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Murkwell in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Stellar Commonwealth and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants. No trace of the team was discovered.
Near the end of the Vat Wars, Vethari Grand Master Qelmar traveled to the planet Murkwell after hearing the voice of deceased Vethari Elder Alder Fenwick. He ventured into the swamp, where he communed with Fenwick, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Qelmar of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Flux and the Cosmic Flux. Then, Qelmar entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Vethari fighting the vat-born and then the mysterious Korrun Overlord.

## Dominion Era

After his failure to defeat the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn following the formation of the Obsidian Dominion, Qelmar exiled himself to Murkwell. Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat, he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Synth Flux. The lifeboat served as Qelmar's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Murkwell's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Qelmar built a new home for himself. He positioned it near to the shadow current cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. However, the Murkwell wet season created a need for the Vethari Elder to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.
At some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Murkwell in search of Qelmar. After saving her from a monster, Qelmar used the Synth Flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Qelmar watched from afar.
Years later, Taren Vossmark of the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth traveled to Murkwell under the direction of late Vethari Elder Orrin Valdane in hopes of finding Qelmar and being trained in the ways of the Vethari Circle. Arriving with his navmech KX-7, Vossmark crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Vethari, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Qelmar at the time. The old master took Vossmark back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Vossmark. Initially reluctant to train Vossmark due to his age and lack of patience, Qelmar was eventually convinced to teach him by the Synth Flux spirit of Valdane.
