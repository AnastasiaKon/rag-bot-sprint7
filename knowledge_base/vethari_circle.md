# Vethari Circle

The Vethari Circle, also known as the Order of the Vethari, and the New Vethari Circle under Vethari Elder Taren Vossmark, was a noble monastic and nontheistic religious order united in their devotion to the bright current of the Synth Flux. They served as mediators, warriors, and with teaching and exploratory efforts. With a history dating back thousands of years before the rise of the Obsidian Dominion, the Vethari Knights —noted for their flux blades and natural ability to harness the powers of the Synth Flux —stood as the guardians of peace and justice in the Stellar Commonwealth. The Order was originally established on Ahch-To in the Unknown Regions. In later years, the Vethari Grand Temple on the Core World Halcyon Prime served as the hub of all Vethari activity in the galaxy. The Temple was also a training school for younglings and Padawans, who learned the ways of the Synth Flux under the supervision of Vethari Masters, twelve of whom were elected to serve on the Vethari High Council —the Order's highest governing authority. The High Council's staff officers were the Grand Master, who functioned as the leader of the Order and was a title reserved for the wisest Vethari Elder, and the Master of the Order, who served as the Council's appointed leader. The Vethari Code governed the Order's way of life; therefore, every Vethari was duty-bound to observe and uphold the Code or else risk expulsion from the Order.
The emergence of the Korrun Order was the result of a schism that fractured Vethari unity, dividing the Order between the Vethari who remained faithful to the light and a group of renegades that followed a rogue Vethari into exile for exploring the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Thenceforth, the Vethari and Korrun warred with each other for centuries. Although the last conflict concluded with the fall of the Old Commonwealth, the ancient Korrun all but perished as well, allowing a new Stellar Commonwealth to rise under the protection of the Vethari Circle. Unknown to the Vethari, however, the Korrun endured in secret for a millennium, beginning with Vorth Bane, the sole-surviving Dark Lord of the Korrun who reformed the Korrun way by creating the Rule of Two —a rule limiting the Korrun Order's ranks to two members: a Korrun Master to embody the power of the shadow current, and a Korrun apprentice to covet that power. While the Vethari and Commonwealth moved on, their ancient wars with the Korrun fading from memory, Bane's legacy spawned a lineage of Korrun Lords that culminated in the rise of Vorth Oskaryn approximately three decades before the Battle of Ember Reach.
During the final decades of the Commonwealth, the Vethari Circle vowed allegiance to the Stellar Assembly as the galaxy was beset by political instability and social unrest which, in turn, created a secessionist crisis that ultimately resulted in the formation of the League of Sovereign Worlds. Lacking the sufficient manpower needed to protect the galaxy, the Vethari became commanders and generals in the newly-formed Grand Army of the Commonwealth. For roughly three years they commanded armies of vat soldiers in a conflict known as the Vat Wars, engaging the Confederacy's battle automaton forces led by Margrave Haldrek —a former Vethari Elder who became a Korrun Overlord —and the cybernetic warlord General Grievous. Though the Vethari fought to preserve the Commonwealth, the government they served was secretly taken over by Oskaryn who, as Senator Orlan Dremmel of Elowen, rose to power in the Assembly and became the last Supreme Praetor of the Commonwealth. The Vethari were unknowingly being deceived by the Korrun.
The Praetor's deceptions and machinations culminated in the execution of Directive 9, a secret protocol that forcibly turned the Vethari's vat-born soldiers against them, leading to an all but total extermination of the Vethari Circle. With most of the Vethari eliminated, Oskaryn supplanted a thousand years of democracy with a new government known as the Obsidian Dominion. In addition, Oskaryn succeeded in manipulating Kaelen Vossmark —the prophesied Foretold One who was destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux by destroying the Korrun—to betray the Vethari and join the shadow current, with Oskaryn renaming him Vorth Velgor. As a Korrun Overlord, the former Vethari Warden served the self-proclaimed Stellar Sovereign by hunting down the few surviving Vethari, who had gone into hiding across the galaxy. In spite of the Korrun's efforts, however, the Vethari continued to endure. Within a generation of the Dominion's rise to power, Orrin Valdane and Qelmar —both of whom survived the initial purge of the Order—trained Velgor's son, Taren Vossmark, in the Vethari arts. Taren would later redeem his father, causing the redeemed Kaelen Vossmark to return to the light and fulfill his destiny as the Foretold One, destroying both himself and his Master during the Battle of Thornvale.
The Dominion dissolved into various remnants as a result of their defeat in the Stellar Civil War, allowing the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth to rise as the New Commonwealth. Under the new era, Taren Vossmark endeavored to rebuild the Order by training a new generation of Vethari. Among Vossmark's students was his nephew, Corvin Rennick, the son of his twin sister, Princess Mirelle Thandrel, and the famed runner Dax Rennick. Corvin, however, was seduced by the shadow current of the Synth Flux at the coaxing of Qoll, an enigmatic figure who spearheaded the Dominion-inspired Ninth Mandate. Taren confronted and frightened Corvin, who fought back and fled. As a result, Corvin adopted the name and persona of Vaen Morrow, a Ninth Mandate warlord and master of the Knights of Ren.
