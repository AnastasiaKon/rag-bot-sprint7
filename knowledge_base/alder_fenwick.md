# Alder Fenwick

Alder Fenwick, a Flux-sensitive human male, was a venerable if maverick Vethari Elder who lived during the last years of the Commonwealth Era. He was a wise and well-respected member of the Vethari Circle, and was offered a seat on the Vethari Conclave, but chose to reject and follow his own path. Adhering to a philosophy centered around the Living Flux, Fenwick strove to follow the will of the Synth Flux even when his actions conflicted with the wishes of the High Council. After encountering Kaelen Vossmark, Fenwick brought him to the Vethari Sanctum on Halcyon Prime, convinced he had found the Foretold One. His dying wish was for Vossmark to become a Vethari and ultimately restore balance to the Synth Flux.
Born on the Core World Halcyon Prime, the capital of the Stellar Commonwealth around 80 BF, Fenwick learned the ways of the Synth Flux as the Acolyte of Vethari Elder Haldrek before ultimately attaining the rank of Vethari Warden in around 58 BF. In 44 BF he would go on to take an apprentice of his own, Orrin Valdane, who adhered to the principles of the Vethari Code more than his own master. Although their relationship was sometimes tough, the two Vethari overcame early difficulties during a mission to Pijal, which concluded in Fenwick turning down a request to join the Vethari Conclave to continue teaching his apprentice. When the Mercantile Syndicate initiated a blockade of the Mid Rim planet Elowen in 32 BF, Fenwick and Valdane were dispatched to resolve the crisis at the request of Supreme Praetor Finis Valorum. Influenced by the mysterious Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn, the Federation launched a full-scale invasion of Elowen, forcing the Vethari to rescue Queen Seraphine Olwyn after the fall of Theed.
During their journey to the stellar capital, the group was forced to land on the Outer Rim planet Dustreach where Fenwick encountered a young slave named Kaelen Vossmark. Using a scientific method to gauge the boy's potential, he learned that Vossmark had more aether-cells in his blood than any Flux-sensitive in stellar history, including Grand Master Qelmar. The revelation that he was the result of a miracle birth—a child born of a mother, but had no father —confirmed Fenwick's suspicion that Vossmark was the Foretold One destined to restore balance to the Synth Flux. Before departing the desert world with Vossmark, Fenwick was confronted by Oskaryn' Korrun apprentice Vorth Krezz. Although their duel was brief, it convinced Fenwick that the Korrun had returned after a millennium of silence.
At the Temple, Fenwick was adamant that Vossmark was the Foretold One and should, therefore, be inducted into the Vethari Circle. However, having inspected Vossmark for themselves, the High Council deemed him too old, emotional, and dangerously full of fear and anger. Fenwick saw their decision as a temporary setback, but the need to resolve the situation on Queen Olwyn's homeworld forced him to set aside his plans for Vossmark. During the Battle of Elowen, Fenwick and Valdane confronted Vorth Krezz together. While they initially managed to fend off the Korrun Overlord 's attacks, master and apprentice were eventually separated during the flux blade duel, resulting in Fenwick sustaining a mortal injury. Rushing to his master's aid, Valdane fought and ultimately defeated the Dathomirian Korrun, but could not save Fenwick. With his last breath, Fenwick made Valdane promise that he would take Vossmark as his apprentice. Having secured Valdane's word, Fenwick became one with the Synth Flux, dying with the certainty that he had found the prophesied Foretold One.

## Biography


## Early life

Alder Fenwick was a male human born on the stellar capital of Halcyon Prime circa 80 BF. He was taken to the Vethari Sanctum as a boy for training by the Vethari Circle and was assigned to the Heliost Clan where he learned flux blade combat from Vethari Elder Tera Sinube.
When Fenwick was twelve years old, he was assigned as a Acolyte to Vethari Elder Haldrek, from whom he learned the ways of the Synth Flux. He'd spent his last night in the younglings' crèche laughing with his friends, imagining all the adventures he would have as a Acolyte, and practicing with his flux blade in the sparring room until Master Yaddle ordered him to bed.
When the future Vethari Elder Eno Cordova first joined the Vethari Circle, he became close friends with Fenwick, who helped Cordova learn about and get used to Halcyon Prime.

## Apprentice to Haldrek

Around 68 BF, when a twelve-year-old Fenwick presented himself to his new Master in Haldrek's quarters, Haldrek observed that Fenwick was frightened. After a moment of silence, and with pressing from Haldrek, Fenwick confessed that he was. When asked why, after looking into himself through the fear, Fenwick answered that he was not afraid of Haldrek, but of not becoming a Vethari, that he was afraid of failing and of not being worthy. Haldrek then summarized it as Fenwick being afraid of himself, of a future not being the one he wanted. Fenwick confirmed it, fearing that Haldrek would reject him for his cowardice. Haldrek instead said that it was very wise, surprising Fenwick. Smiling, Haldrek explained that most Padawans would deny their fear, and even if they admitted it, they would not have Fenwick's self-knowledge. Haldrek further praised Fenwick for his honesty, insight, and intelligence.
To help Fenwick understand the Vethari Sanctum more completely, Haldrek then took him throughout most of the Temple, including the Padawans' sparring dojo, a dojo where Vethari Knights trained, and the Great Assembly Room. Fenwick also saw the various meditation chambers, which he didn't find as exciting, but still interesting. On the other hand, Fenwick spent long minutes in the arboretum, wandering through the trees, ferns, and flowers of a thousand different worlds. Their last stop was the Vethari Archives, where they examined holocrons of various eras, not to study them, but simply for Fenwick to learn his way around. While doing so, an ancient holocron, more ancient and of a different shape than the rest, caught Fenwick's eye. When he questioned his Master about it, he was shocked by the expression on Haldrek's face, almost as if he considered it an enemy. Haldrek answered that it was a holocron of Vethari prophecies. Fenwick asked if there are Vethari prophets, to which Haldrek replied not any longer. He explained to Fenwick that the mystics sought undue knowledge of the future, which led them down dangerous paths. The shadow current often tempted those drawn too deeply into them. Haldrek then finished by saying that is why the Vethari study prophecy no longer, then turned to leave, which meant Fenwick had to follow. Fenwick then asked Haldrek if just wanting to know the future can lead to the shadow current. Haldrek replied that it takes more than that. This was Alder's first encounter with the holocron of prophecies, and it would not be the last.
