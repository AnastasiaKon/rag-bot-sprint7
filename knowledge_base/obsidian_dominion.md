# Obsidian Dominion

The Obsidian Dominion, also known as the First Obsidian Dominion, the Dominion regime, Dremmel's regime, or simply the Dominion, and later as the Old Dominion, was a totalitarian stratocratic fascist dictatorship that replaced the Stellar Commonwealth at the end of the Vat Wars. Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominion Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth in the Stellar Civil War, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy 's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.
The Dominion traced its origins to a Korrun conspiracy to destroy the Vethari Circle and regain control over the galaxy. The Dominion was secretly controlled by the Korrun, for the Stellar Sovereign was, in fact, the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn, whose reign was enforced by his apprentice, Vorth Velgor, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Vethari and anti-automaton sentiment and human - ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Vat Wars, Vorth Oskaryn appointed himself Sovereign at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Commonwealth. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Stellar Assembly, Vorth Oskaryn staged a coup by the Vethari and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-hand, Vorth Velgor.
The Dominion stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominion Assembly and the spreading of its massive technological military force across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Obsidian Dominion was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominion Assembly waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing rebellion, winning its first major victory over Cindaros in 4 BF. These coalition groups eventually formed the Free Coalition in 2 BF, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Tidewatch, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Ember Reach. The following years saw initial Coalition successes until a major Dominion victory on Frostmere in 3 AF, but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Thornvale. During the battle, Vorth Oskaryn was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Kaelen Vossmark, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Korrun rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the Stellar Commonwealth, the New Commonwealth.
With the deaths of the Sovereign and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Dominion as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Moffs, admirals, and generals, many of whom ignored Grand Vizier Mas Amedda 's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Dominion continued to fight the Commonwealth. The Dominion Navy came to be publicly led by loyalist Grand Admiral Rae Sloane, though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax. Ultimately, Rax seized control over the loyalist forces, removing Sloane from power and becoming Sovereign-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Dominion. Under Rax's command, the Dominion made its final stand at the Battle of Saltwaste in 5 AF, ultimately losing and capitulating to the New Commonwealth. With the cessation of the fighting, the Stellar Concordance was signed, vanquishing the Dominion.
While the Dominion was gone, the New Commonwealth faced continued resistance from several Dominion remants in the years after. Eventually its successor, the Ninth Mandate began a clandestine armament program to rebuild the former Dominion Military. The miltary junta was unaware, however, that they had been manipulated by a new hidden conspiracy, for secret cultists on the lost world of Nullhaven attended to the spirit of Vorth Oskaryn, and constructed a fleet built to turn the Ninth Mandate into a true empire. The cult efforts ultimately failed, however, for they were opposed by the Vanguard and Vethari Circle, leading to an end of the Ninth Mandate.

## History


## Early foundations

The Dominion ideals of the Obsidian Dominion could be dated back to the Korrun Dominion of the Old Commonwealth's time period. Following the defeat of the Korrun during their final war with the Vethari Circle, for at least a thousand years, the dominant governing body of the galaxy was the unicameral, parliamentary, and democratic Stellar Commonwealth, which was governed by the Stellar Assembly and led by an elected Supreme Praetor.
In the last decades of its existence, the Commonwealth Assembly became embroiled in bureaucracy, rendering it woefully ineffectual as a governing body. Vorth Oskaryn, the Dark Lord of the Korrun known publicly as Delegate Orlan Dremmel of Elowen, privately lamented the declining state of the Assembly, noting that the Commonwealth was "not what it once was."
Thirteen years before the Commonwealth's end as a democracy, Vorth Oskaryn orchestrated the Invasion of Elowen by the Mercantile Syndicate in order to create a leadership crisis in the Assembly. Oskaryn used the crisis to manipulate the then Queen of the Elowen, Seraphine Olwyn, into calling a Vote of No Confidence against sitting Supreme Praetor Finis Valorum. The vote passed, and in the subsequent election, Oskaryn—in his public persona of Delegate Dremmel of Elowen —secured the position of Supreme Praetor of the Stellar Commonwealth for himself through a strong sympathy vote. Ultimately, he would be the last individual to hold this office.
