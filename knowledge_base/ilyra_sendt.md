# Ilyra Sendt

Ilyra Sendt was a female Hornveil who was a Flux-sensitive outcast from the Vethari Circle. After the Vat Wars, she helped establish a network of various coalition cells against the Obsidian Dominion. Sendt was discovered on her homeworld of Shili by Vethari Elder Plo Koon, who brought her to the Vethari Sanctum on Halcyon Prime to receive Vethari training. Following the outbreak of the Vat Wars, Vethari Grand Master Qelmar assigned the young Sendt to be the Acolyte learner of Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark, who nicknamed her " Snips " when she joined him at the Battle of Christophsis. Whereas Sendt was eager to prove herself, Vossmark had a reputation for recklessness, and they had a rather difficult start as master and apprentice. Yet they worked together to rescue Rotta, the son of crime lord Grubbol Vendrax Oom, and returned Rotta to his father, thus facilitating a crucial coalition between the Slorg Clan and the Stellar Commonwealth.
As a commander in the Grand Army of the Commonwealth, Sendt found a mentor in Vat-born Captain Rex of the 501st Legion, with whom she and Vossmark collaborated to lead frontline campaigns against the League of Sovereign Worlds. In her first command position at the Battle of Ryloth, Sendt disobeyed orders and lost her entire squadron of pilots, but she rebounded and helped secure a victory at Ryloth. Her assignments pitted her against a variety of opponents, from General Grievous and Korrun apprentice Asajj Ventress to contract trackers such as Cad Bane and Aurra Sing. Over time, she matured into a respected leader, contributing to key Commonwealth victories like the Second Battle of Hivecrag and the Battle of Mon Cala. Sendt even died during a series of events on Mortis, but the Daughter, a Flux wielder who personified the bright current, sacrificed herself to resurrect Sendt and bring her back to life.
Sendt gained much experience on and beyond the war front, leading a group of Vethari younglings to revolt against their Trandoshan captors, helping rescue a colony of Hornveil from enslavement by the Zygerrian Slave Dominion, and serving as an advisor to the Onderon coalitionists —with her close friend, Lux Bonteri, among them—as they liberated their world from the Confederacy. In a stark turn of events, Sendt was framed for the bombing of the Vethari Sanctum hangar and other homicides, and she escaped into the Halcyon Prime Underworld to clear her name. Though she formed an unlikely coalition with Asajj Ventress, she was detained by Commonwealth forces and was consequently barred from the Vethari Circle. Ultimately, Vossmark uncovered the true culprit, Sendt's friend, and fellow Acolyte Barriss Offee, and prevented his former apprentice from being convicted of sedition. Nevertheless, the ordeal dislodged Sendt's faith in the Vethari. She refused the Vethari High Council 's offer to rejoin the Order, instead departing in search of a new path. Sendt briefly returned to lead Commonwealth forces during the Siege of Ironhold and faced Krezz and his allies. Directive 9 was enacted shortly after the Korrun Overlord's capture and Sendt alongside Rex, after freeing Rex from the control of Directive 9, escaped the rest of her 501st division. Faking her death, she was forced into hiding.
After the Obsidian Dominion came to power, Sendt went into hiding on Thabeska and Raada disguised as " Ashla." Following the evacuation of Raada, Sendt joined Delegate Bail Thandrel's growing coalition movement. She became the manager of his intelligence network and adopted the codename " Fulcrum." As Fulcrum, Sendt provided intelligence to various coalition factions including Hera Syndulla 's Spectres coalition cell. Sendt took a particular interest in Syndulla's coalitionists because one of their members, a Vethari named Kanan Jarrus, had begun to train his own Acolyte, Ezra Bridger. In addition, Sendt's efforts against the Dominion brought her into contact with the Dark Lord of the Korrun Vorth Velgor—who, unknown to her, was actually her former master, Kaelen Vossmark—as well as the Dominion Inquisitors known as the Fifth Brother and the Seventh Sister, but it also gave her the chance to reunite with Rex. Following the mission to Malachor, Sendt became lost to the rubble and shadows of the Korrun temple and was believed by many, including Jarrus and Bridger, to have perished during a duel with Vorth Velgor.
However, Sendt was unexpectedly saved from Velgor's wrath by a future Ezra Bridger, who pulled her through a portal into the World Between Worlds, a mystical plane of the Synth Flux connecting all time and space. After a confrontation with Vorth Oskaryn, who desired access to the dimension, Sendt returned to her time, promising Bridger that she would find him once she returned. After the end of the Stellar Civil War, Sendt joined Sabine Wren in her quest to find the missing Bridger, who had disappeared during the Liberation of Lothal, and trained Wren in the ways of the Vethari until parting ways. Sendt later journeyed to the city of Calodan on the forest planet Corvus looking for information on the location of Grand Admiral Tessek, who had disappeared alongside Bridger, from Magistrate Morgan Elsbeth. Needing to rescue the city from Elsbeth's rule, she allied herself with the Ironholder Kett Marrow, who had been on a quest to bring the Flux-sensitive youngling Pim to the Vethari. After freeing Calodan, Sendt refused to train Pim and directed Marrow to the planet Tython instead. She later visited Taren Vossmark at his new Vethari Sanctum and, when Vossmark found Pim in his care following a summons from the seeing stone, oversaw his training of the child temporarily.
