# Ilsa Vossmark

Ilsa Vossmark, also known simply as Ilsa, was a Vethari member of the Vanguard during the war against the Ninth Mandate. The human woman was a scavenger on the planet Saltwaste, but her life was changed by tumultuous events in the last days of the New Commonwealth Era. Ilsa's decision to help the automaton BB-8 set her on a course to discovering the Synth Flux awakening within her, leading her to seek out Vethari Elder Taren Vossmark for training. Despite her commitment to the Vethari Circle and the bright current of the Synth Flux, Ilsa felt the pull to the shadow current due to her anger as well as the adversarial relationship she had with Corvin Rennick, a fallen Vethari who joined the Ninth Mandate as a warrior named Vaen Morrow; they shared a unique bond as a dyad in the Synth Flux.
Ilsa was born on Hyperkarn in 15 AF during the rise of the New Commonwealth. Her father, Dathan, was a bioengineered Strand-Cast cloned from the genetic template of Vorth Oskaryn, the Korrun Overlord who ruled the Obsidian Dominion as Sovereign Dremmel until his death and resurrection following the Battle of Thornvale. The vat-born and his wife, Miramir, chose to live as simple junk traders in order to hide their daughter, who inherited Oskaryn' connection to the Synth Flux, from his followers. Even after the deaths of her parents, Ilsa was unaware of her Dremmel bloodline and was forced to survive on her own in the Saltwaste desert, and became a skilled mechanic, pilot, and combatant in the process. Her isolated life was disrupted shortly before war broke out across the galaxy in 34 AF between the Ninth Mandate and the Vanguard. Determined to help the Vanguard, Ilsa gained several new allies including Finn, a renegade greycoat, as well as the veterans Dax Rennick and Grathok aboard the Wandering Kestrel. In the course of their journey, she discovered her latent Flux powers and sought guidance under Taren Vossmark, who taught her the art of the Synth Flux despite his decision to end the Vethari legacy. After Vossmark sacrificed himself to save the Vanguard, Ilsa assumed his mantle as the last Vethari and became the apprentice of General Mirelle Thandrel.
With the return of Vorth Oskaryn in 35 AF, Ilsa set out to find the dark world of Nullhaven and prevent the Korrun from being reborn through the resurrected Dark Lord. It was through Vaen Morrow that Ilsa discovered her origins as Dremmel's granddaughter, and upon confronting the resurrected Sovereign in person, she was nearly swayed to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, thus falling into his trap of transferring his spirit into her, which will allow him to possess her body. However, Ilsa was saved by Corvin Rennick after he renounced his dark alter ego. Aided by the spirits of all the Vethari, Ilsa died vanquishing her grandfather, causing Rennick to make the ultimate sacrifice in order to bring Ilsa back to life. The two embraced and shared a kiss as Rennick peacefully vanished in Ilsa's arms. Although Rennick was the last of the Vossmark bloodline, Ilsa assumed the name "Vossmark" to honor their memory and in recognition of her chosen family. Sometime later, the now self-proclaimed Ilsa Vossmark became a Vethari Elder.

## Biography


## Pre-birth

In 1 BF, during the Stellar Civil War and more than a decade before Ilsa's birth, her voice was heard by Acolyte Ezra Bridger during his brief visit to the World Between Worlds —a place that existed beyond time and space.
Ilsa and Corvin Rennick were the culmination of a prophesied Flux dyad as well as the Rule of Two. Inspired by the Doctrine of the Dyad, the Rule of Two mandated that only two Korrun could exist at a given time, a Korrun Master and a Korrun apprentice, though its ultimate goal was to create a dyad between two Korrun Lords. Vorth Plagueis tried to create a dyad with his apprentice, Vorth Oskaryn, who, in turn, tried to create one with Rennick's grandfather, Kaelen Vossmark. Despite years of searching, the Korrun never found a way to form a dyad on their own. Oskaryn regarded the Rule of Two as a pale imitation and an unworthy but necessary successor to the older, purer doctrine of the dyad that would be ultimately fulfilled by Rennick and Ilsa.

## Early life

Born on the planet Hyperkarn in 15 AF, Ilsa was the daughter of Dathan and Miramir. While their names were lost to history, Ilsa's father was the cloned son—an artificial genetic strandcast —of the resurrected Vorth Oskaryn, making her the granddaughter of the fallen Stellar Sovereign and Dark Lord of the Korrun. Although Oskaryn had not anticipated his son's marriage with Miramir, he sought to claim their child who, unlike his son, had a strong connection to the Synth Flux. As such, Ilsa was a receptacle strong enough to hold her grandfather's spirit, which was trapped in an imperfect cloned body. To protect Ilsa from her grandfather, her parents escaped into hiding, choosing to become ordinary junk traders to keep their daughter safe.
Ilsa's parents had a difficult life; they struggled to survive on the desert planet Saltwaste while raising the daughter whom they loved. Since the Sovereign's son and his wife absconded with their child, the Korrun cultist Ochi, on orders from Oskaryn himself, endeavored to find the strandcast and his daughter. Ilsa's grandfather had foreseen what she had the capability of becoming, recognizing her immense strength in the Synth Flux, and that such power was not mere ordinary power. It was his own power. Because of this, Oskaryn was determined to make certain that one day, Ilsa would embrace her destiny as a Dremmel, and of course, the heir of his Korrun legacy and successor as Stellar Sovereign. At the same time, two Coalition heroes, Vethari Elder Taren Vossmark and former General Jorek Maltrane, began investigating a disturbance in the Synth Flux after Maltrane had overheard a drunk Ochi discussing his orders to kidnap the young Ilsa. However, the two never learned the girl's name nor saw the family face-to-face.
