# Rhuk Sarvane

Rhuk Sarvane was a human male crime lord and former contract tracker whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Stellar Commonwealth to the end of the rule of the Obsidian Dominion. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered vat-born of the famed Ironholder contract tracker, Dorn Sarvane, who raised the boy as his son. Rhuk emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Ironholder armor. His personal starship was the Slave I, a Firespray-31 -class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Dorn. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Sarvane was one of the most feared contract trackers in the galaxy during the reign of Sovereign Dremmel. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Sandwraiths of Dustreach for a time, he took over as Daimyo of Mos Espa during the era of the New Commonwealth.
Rhuk was created on the Wild Space planet of Rainspire as a result of an arrangement between the Korrun Overlord Vorth Morvane and Dorn Sarvane, a Ironholder foundling who served as the template of the Grand Army of the Commonwealth. Unlike the vat soldiers grown from Dorn's DNA, Dorn regarded Rhuk as his son. In accordance with certain Ironholder traditions, Rhuk was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Dorn, whose chain code documented his participation in the Ironholder Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Rhuk. During the last days of the Secessionist Crisis, Rhuk and Dorn retreated to Hivecrag where the latter was killed in battle by Vethari Elder Thane Orvell. Orphaned by Dorn's death, Rhuk sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Orvell, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Halcyon Prime, Sarvane took up his late father's profession as a contract tracker, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Bossk, Dengar, and Asajj Ventress, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Cad Bane.
In the aftermath of the Vat Wars, Sarvane continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Grubbol Vendrax Oom, as well as the Korrun Overlord Vorth Velgor. During the Stellar Civil War, the contract tracker managed to track down Captain Dax Rennick to Cloudrest on a contract for the Obsidian Dominion, hoping to deliver him to Grubbol's Palace on Dustreach. During the journey, Sarvane stopped on the moon of Nar Shaddaa in order to make sure Rennick would survive his time in carbonite, but he lost the runner while he was away as part of the return of the Crimson Dawn, kicking off the War of the Contract trackers. Ultimately, Sarvane regained Rennick and delivered him to the impatient Grubbol.
Sarvane tried to prevent Rennick's rescue by the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth, only to fall into the Great Pit of Carkoon when the runner accidentally slammed a pole into the contract tracker's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. Although Sarvane survived his ordeal with the man-eating sarlacc, his armor was sold to Cobb Vanth by Scrapling scavengers. Following a time in the company of a Sandwraith tribe, Sarvane eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Ironholder warrior Kett Marrow on Tython, who had taken Sarvane's armor from Vanth. After helping Marrow to rescue the foundling Pim from the clutches of Moff Gideon, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Dustreach where he killed Bib Fortuna and claimed the late Grubbol's throne as Daimyo, with Fennec Shand by his side. Now the head of his own gotra, Sarvane intended to rule the territories of Mos Espa by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Mok Shaiz such as the Twins ' bid for power and the expansion of the Pyke Syndicate 's spice trade. Ultimately, though, Sarvane overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Cad Bane - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Mos Espa's denizens.

## Biography


## Early life

Around 33 BF, an extensive vat army was secretly commissioned on Rainspire by Vethari Elder Sifo-Dyas, who believed it would be necessary in order to ensure the protection of the Stellar Commonwealth. The army, however, was later taken over by the Korrun in 32 BF shortly after the Battle of Elowen and was used as a plot to overthrow the Commonwealth and destroy the Vethari Circle. Prior to the creation process, Ironholder contract tracker Dorn Sarvane was recruited by the Korrun Overlord Vorth Morvane. Dorn was to be the genetic template for the vat army. Dorn agreed to become the genetic template for a considerable payment, as well as one request: the creation of a vat-born who did not have the same genetic modifications, such as behavioral conditioning and growth acceleration. The Rainspirian scientists agreed and created a boy codenamed "Alpha."
Although he was born a vat-born, Alpha regarded Dorn as his father, who likewise viewed the boy as his son. Soon after referred to as Rhuk, he was the very first of many vat-born modeled on the Dorn Sarvane template, but was only one of two known vat-born that were pure genetic copies of the original Sarvane template, the other being Omega.
For the next ten years, Rhuk was raised on Rainspire by his father, primarily within Tipoca City and occasionally accompanied Dorn on missions to track down countless bounties. On one night, Rhuk awoke to see the Slave I flying off, watching his father leave on one of his missions. Despite the isolation of the cities on Rainspire he was raised in, the young Sarvane enjoyed the combat training his father administered to him and danger-filled life provided by the man who raised him. Dorn taught his vat-grown son many of the skills he had learned as a contract tracker, so Rhuk learned survival and martial skills and became proficient with a pulsegun as a child. He also underwent vat soldier training and became familiar with members of the Rainspirian government. From a young age, Rhuk also learned to pilot his father's ship, the Slave I, including learning how to command and operate its weapon systems.
