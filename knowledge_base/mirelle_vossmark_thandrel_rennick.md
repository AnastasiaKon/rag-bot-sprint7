# Mirelle Vossmark Thandrel Rennick

Mirelle Vossmark Thandrel Rennick was a Flux-sensitive human Serenhali female politician, Vethari, and military leader who served in the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth during the Dominion Era and the New Commonwealth and Vanguard in the subsequent New Commonwealth Era. Shortly after birth on Polis Massa, she was adopted into the House of Thandrel —the Serenhali royal family—and was raised as Princess Mirelle Thandrel of Serenhal, a planet in the Core Worlds known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Delegate Bail Prestor Thandrel and his wife, Queen Breha Thandrel, making her the heir to the Serenhali monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Thandrel devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Obsidian Dominion and the Ninth Mandate.
Born in 19 BF as Mirelle Olwyn Vossmark, she was the daughter of the Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark and Delegate Seraphine Olwyn of Elowen. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Vat Wars, which saw the Vethari Circle purged and the Stellar Commonwealth reorganized into the Obsidian Dominion. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, Mirelle and her twin brother, Taren, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Sovereign. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Mirelle grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on Serenhal until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Vect Nokru —under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Inquisitorius —in 9 BF. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Vat Wars, Vethari Elder Orrin Valdane went to Mirelle's rescue on Daiyu, and, after evading the Dominion's hand on Mapuzo and Jabiim, Mirelle was brought back to Serenhal. Years later, Mirelle succeeded Bail by representing their homeworld in the Dominion Assembly, though secretly she supported the Uprising. During the Stellar Civil War, however, Serenhal was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Dominion's VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Thandrel to openly fight the New Order as one of the Free Coalition’s substantial heroes.
In the aftermath of the Battle of Thornvale, Thandrel married the Dunmoorian runner Dax Rennick and learned the Vethari arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Corvin Rennick, on the day of the Dominion's capitulation in 5 AF. Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Corvin turning to the shadow current like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Vaen Morrow, and the Stellar Assembly sidelining Thandrel's career. Adopting the military rank of general, she led the Vanguard and their efforts to oppose the rising Ninth Mandate during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Corvin in hopes that her son will return to the bright current of the Synth Flux like her father during the Battle of Thornvale. Despite her efforts, the Ninth Mandate succeeded in destroying the New Commonwealth, and Thandrel's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 AF. Thandrel continued to lead the Vanguard during the war against the Ninth Mandate, all the while passing on her Vethari training onto Ilsa —a Flux-sensitive scavenger from Saltwaste and Taren’s apprentice.
As her health declined, Thandrel used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the bright current. Following her death on Ajan Kloss in 35 AF, her final actions inspired Ilsa and the Vanguard to defeat the forces of the reborn Vorth Oskaryn's Korrun Eternal on Nullhaven and spark an uprising against the Ninth Mandate across the galaxy. The heir to three inheritances, Mirelle Vossmark Thandrel Rennick's legacy passed on to the Vethari Ilsa Vossmark, the military leader Poe Dameron of the Vanguard, and Corvin Rennick, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for Ilsa and becoming one with the Synth Flux to join his mother and the Vossmark family.

## Biography


## Pre-birth

Toward the end of the Vat Wars, a stellar conflict fought between the Stellar Commonwealth and the League of Sovereign Worlds that was engineered by the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn, Vethari Elder Qelmar set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Moraband, the ancient Korrun homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Synth Flux Priestess, Serenity, who had guided his journey, told him that there was another Vossmark, and Qelmar heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events not only alluded to the potential he would sense in Mirelle, but to what Qelmar would tell her twin brother, Taren, about Mirelle in his last words, before his death.

## Early life

Mirelle Olwyn Vossmark was born on the asteroid of Polis Massa two days after the end of the Vat Wars and the newly established Obsidian Dominion 's purge of the Vethari Circle. She and her twin brother, Taren, who was born shortly before her, were the children of Commonwealth Delegate Seraphine Olwyn Ferrand, who died shortly after the births, and Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark, who had recently fallen to the shadow current of the Synth Flux. She was left with a lasting impression of images and feelings of her mother that her brother did not: that their mother was "very beautiful, kind, but sad", though Mirelle was unsure if she had really seen her mother or if she had just invented the memories to fill the hole in her life.
