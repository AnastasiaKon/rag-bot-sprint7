# Dax Rennick

Dax Rennick, known only as Dax until being given the surname Rennick, was a human male runner and hero of the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Wandering Kestrel, a leader in the Free Coalition and an instrumental figure in the Stellar Civil War during the reign of the Obsidian Dominion. Although he did not believe in the Synth Flux controlling his destiny, Dax came to believe in the cause of stellar freedom, joining Vethari Taren Vossmark and Princess Mirelle Thandrel in the fight for the Uprising. Following the war, Dax and Mirelle welcomed a son, Corvin Rennick, who became the dark knight Vaen Morrow after being corrupted by the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Mirelle hoped that Dax, as Corvin's father, could find a way to break the shadow current's hold on their son. Dax Rennick became legendary across the galaxy as both a runner and war hero of the Uprising.
As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Dunmoor, Dax yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scrumrat named Qi'ra, Dax lived as a street thief with Dunmoor's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Proxima went bad, causing Dax to flee Dunmoor. He was given the full name "Dax Rennick" by an Dominion recruitment officer while entering the Dominion Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Dominion cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Dominion Armored Division. On Mimban, Dax met the thief Tobias Beckett and wound up deserting the Dominion and joining his crew to steal unrefined coaxium from Kessel in order to pay a debt to Dryden Vos, a kingpin with the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Qi'ra, Dax was forced to kill his mentor Beckett after a brutal double-cross. Dax also became rivals and close friends with fellow runner Jorek Maltrane and the Brennok Grathok, the latter who became his best friend. Maltrane owned the Wandering Kestrel, a fast ship with which Dax made the Kessel Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Jorek in a game of sabacc.
With only Grathok remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Corellian runner, Dax Rennick embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Grubbol the Slorg, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Dustreach. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, Rennick owed a great deal of money to Grubbol, who then placed a bounty on the runner's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Dax and Grathok were hired to charter Taren Vossmark, Orrin Valdane, and the automatons KX-7 and VN-4 to Serenhal, avoiding Dominion greycoats who were searching for Taren's automatons. However, as they find Serenhal destroyed by the Dominion's new superweapon, the Void Core, Rennick became caught up in the rebellion against the Dominion, rescuing Princess Mirelle Thandrel, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Coalition base on Ember Reach 4, and saved Taren from the Korrun Overlord Vorth Velgor in the Battle of Ember Reach, allowing Vossmark to destroy the Void Core. Rennick fought with the Uprising for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Dominion.
After the Battle of Frostmere, Rennick sought refuge with Thandrel—with whom he had fallen in love—Grathok, and VN-4 on Cloud City, which was operated by his old friend Jorek Maltrane. There, they were captured by Vorth Velgor as part of a trap to bait Vossmark to the city. Rennick was frozen in carbonite and taken to Grubbol's Palace on Dustreach by the contract tracker Rhuk Sarvane, where he remained encased in carbonite for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Coalition Fleet, Rennick was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Thornvale. Rennick led the Pathfinders to Thornvale, where he, Thandrel, Grathok, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Tufflins, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Coalition Fleet to destroy the superweapon.
The Battle of Thornvale, which saw the deaths of Sovereign Orlan Dremmel and Vorth Velgor, allowed the Uprising to form the New Commonwealth. In the early days after Thornvale, Rennick continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Dominion. He married Thandrel, and the two had a son, Corvin Rennick. The boy struggled with the shadow current of the Synth Flux—like his mother, who was the daughter of Vorth Velgor and twin sister of Taren Vossmark, Corvin was Flux-sensitive —so Thandrel sent him to be trained as a Vethari by Vossmark. Corvin was seduced to the shadow current by Qoll, Supreme Leader of the Ninth Mandate, resulting in the destruction of Vossmark's attempt to rebuild the Vethari Circle.
Having lost their son, Rennick left and returned to smuggling, and even lost the Wandering Kestrel to numerous thieves. He continued to smuggle until he was brought back into the fight by a scavenger named Ilsa and a defected Ninth Mandate greycoat named Finn, who carried with them a automaton, BB-8, that had part of a map to Vossmark's location. Rennick, who retrieved the Kestrel from Ilsa and Finn, returned to Thandrel, who led the Vanguard against the Ninth Mandate, and fought in the battle to destroy the Ninth Mandate superweapon known as Ashen Bastion. During the battle, Rennick saw his son, who had taken the name Vaen Morrow, and tried to convince him to return home. Instead, Ren stabbed his father with his flux blade. Mortally wounded, Rennick fell to his death in the bowels of the Starkiller weapon. However, Rennick's heroic memory survived and his spirit remained alive and appeared in a Flux vision a year later to convince Ren to abandon the shadow current and defeat the resurrected Vorth Oskaryn, after a duel on the remains of the second Void Core between Ilsa and Ren, being enough for Corvin Rennick to return and eventually sacrifice his own life to help Ilsa end Oskaryn and the Korrun for good.
