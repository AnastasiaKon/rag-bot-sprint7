# Serenhal

Serenhal, located in the Core Worlds, was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains. During the waning decades of the Stellar Commonwealth, it was ruled by Queen Breha Thandrel and represented in the Stellar Assembly by her husband, Delegate Bail Prestor Thandrel. Serenhal was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.
Following the rise of Orlan Dremmel's Obsidian Dominion, Serenhal played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Free Coalition, a movement that sought to restore the Old Commonwealth's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Coalition, the planet was destroyed when High Warden Osric Vaughn decided to test the worldlance of the Void Core, a moon -sized battle station developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Mirelle Thandrel, adopted daughter of Bail and Breha Thandrel and heiress to the royal house of Serenhal.

## Description

From space, the planet of Serenhal, located in the star system of the same name within the galaxy 's Core Worlds, appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains, with patches of green grassy hills. Serenhal was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." Serenhalis were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Serenhal included the Cloudshape Falls, the Glarus Lagoons, and the Isatabith rain forest.
A variety of grapevines grew on Serenhal, which notably allowed to produce Toniray and emerald wine. The local fauna included the Serenhali wolf-cat, nerfs, and a species of white-winged bird.
The cities on Serenhal were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment. For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Serenhali architecture made use of white synthstone, which inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Outer Rim Territories, like the floating colony of Cloud City, and the planets Lothal and Garel.
Despite the lyrics of the traditional Serenhali lullaby " Mirrorbright " referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.

## History


## The Old Commonwealth

The peaceful world of Serenhal was among the oldest members of the Stellar Commonwealth, the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Serenhal must complete a Challenge of the Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Serenhali heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled on Halcyon Prime, the Vethari High Council was known to have a council on Serenhal. Serenhal at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique architecture. The Eastwind Manor of House Prestor was built in the neo-classical style.
Figuring that she would like the world, Yana Ro wished to visit Serenhal after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Hand cult. Ro suggested as such to Shea Ganandra, whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with. During the High Commonwealth Era, Serenhal was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the Commonwealth Defense Coalition treaties, was required to lend starships and personnel to be commanded by Commonwealth military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Serenhal after the crisis ended. Serenhal was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 BF, to aid the Commonwealth in fighting the Nihil, a group of marauders who caused the Great Foldspace Disaster.
At some point between 40 BF and 28 BF, the heir apparent of Serenhal, sixteen year old Princess Breha Thandrel, took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Breha fell from the famous Alderaaian mountain Appenza Peak and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her heart and lungs replaced with pulmonodes. After her fall Breha was officially invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Serenhal. Years later the people would still speak of Breha's accident.
Breha Thandrel eventually succeeded her mother as queen of Serenhal.
By the time of the Invasion of Elowen, Serenhal had not seen conflict in generations. The planet was represented in the Stellar Assembly by Delegate Bail Antilles. That year, the then- Supreme Praetor of the Commonwealth, Finis Valorum, was ousted from office, and Antilles was nominated for the position in the resulting election. However, the Serenhali senator was defeated by Delegate Orlan Dremmel, who represented the sovereign system of Elowen.
In 28 BF, former queen of Elowen Delegate Seraphine Olwyn and her entourage were invited to Serenhal by Queen Breha and stayed at the royal palace for a week before returning home to Elowen.
