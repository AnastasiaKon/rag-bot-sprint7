# Jorek Balthen Maltrane

Jorek Balthen Maltrane, otherwise known as Jorek Maltrane, was a human runner, gambler, and entrepreneur who became Baron Administrator of Cloud City and, later, a general in the Free Coalition. Born on Socorro, he was the owner of the Wandering Kestrel before losing it to Dax Rennick in a game of sabacc on Numidian Prime. Afterward, Maltrane put an end to his days as a runner and became an entrepreneur, setting up a small mining operation on the planet Lothal before eventually becoming the leader of Cloud City in the skies of the planet Cloudrest.
During the Stellar Civil War, Vorth Velgor arrived on Cloud City to lay a trap for his son, Taren Vossmark. As part of the trap, the Dark Lord of the Korrun forced Maltrane into tricking a group of Coalitionists, including Rennick and Princess Mirelle Thandrel, leading them to Velgor himself. Though Velgor promised to leave Cloud City without an Dominion presence, Maltrane felt the deal had been altered to the point where he could no longer tolerate it. Maltrane alerted his citizens to the Obsidian Dominion 's presence and impending occupation and ordered an evacuation. He helped the Coalitionists try to rescue Rennick, who had been frozen in carbonite, from Rhuk Sarvane, but the contract tracker escaped.
Maltrane joined the Free Coalition and set out to find Rennick. After locating him in the palace of Grubbol the Slorg on Dustreach, Maltrane aided in his rescue. The Coalitionists returned to the fleet, and Maltrane became a general, volunteering to lead the assault on the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station during the Battle of Thornvale. During the battle, he piloted the Wandering Kestrel into the battle station's core, firing the shot which destroyed it.
In the years of peace after the Dominion's dissolution, Maltrane started a family and fathered a daughter named Kadara Maltrane. However, while she was only an infant, his daughter was kidnapped by the Ninth Mandate. Six years later, he embarked on a quest to the desert planet of Pasaana with Taren Vossmark to find answers about the growing darkness in the Synth Flux, but failed to uncover anything. Seeking to bury the pain of those memories, Maltrane settled on Pasaana and led a solitary lifestyle where he was known as the " Hermit " to the Aki-Aki of the Lurch Canyon. He lived alone for some years while keeping contact with the Rennick family and was later asked by Thandrel's Vanguard for his aid in the war against the Ninth Mandate.
In 35 AF, Jorek helped the Vanguard and reunited with his old friend, Grathok. Maltrane played one more role in the fight for stellar freedom as he brought an assembled Citizens' Fleet to reinforce the Vanguard at the battle at Nullhaven, from which the Vanguard emerged victorious.

## Deals and adventures

A member of the tragic Maltrane family, Jorek Balthen Maltrane, otherwise known as "Jorek" Maltrane, was born on the planet Socorro. During his youth, he became a runner and a gambler, excelling in a card game known as sabacc. He traveled throughout the galaxy and piloted a freighter called the Wandering Kestrel, which he won through a game of sabacc at the gas giant Cloudrest. The ship saved his life on numerous occasions. Sometime before 10 BF, Jorek won a subtropical moon in the Oseon Belt during a game of sabacc. Jorek also developed a partnership with L3-37, a custom self-made piloting automaton.
During his time with L3, Jorek nearly lost her to a Slorg after making a bad bet in a game of sabacc on Gonda. At one point, Maltrane was involved in an incident with a Dawinian and their companion. He also came to meet the Tonnika sisters, even showing Brea Tonnika how to work the controls on the Kestrel. At another point, Brea and Senni Tonnika made a mess of the room they were staying in on the city-world of Halcyon Prime in order to get back at Maltrane. At some point, Maltrane met a automaton mechanic who worked on the Mid Rim planet Kijimi, with that mechanic coming to owe Maltrane a favor.
Sometime prior to the Battle of Ember Reach, Jorek and L3-37 were ambushed in the Halthor sector when an Dominion interdictor dropped out from foldspace. As a result of the ambush, Maltrane lost a majority of his shipment of walkers that he acquired from an Er'Kit arms dealer in the Noonian sector. Jorek also had dealings with Crimson Dawn on Felucia. He completed his debt to Crimson Dawn and its leader, Dryden Vos. At another point, Maltrane visited Pantora and successfully convinced Dominion Vice Grand Administrator Prita Sven to let him leave the planet with a freighter full of poached forlyn carcasses.
Maltrane and L3-37 at some point was arrested for smuggling and was sent to the planet Hynestia. Jorek and L3 was asked by Queen Forsythia Jin of Hynestia to deliver an artifact to the Obsidian Dominion for making up for a gherlian fur failed delivery. Princess Rinetta Gan knew about the real purpose of the artifact from her teacher Zel Gris. It was supposed to function as an energy source and make up for the lack of sunlight on the planet Livno III. Without it, Livno III would fall to ruin and destruction. Rinetta tried to force Maltrane and L3 to comply with her new orders after sneaking onto the Wandering Kestrel as a stowaway. Her plan failed as Maltrane traveled to Neral's moon to settle a debt with Ne'eda Frip. When the exchange went wrong and escaped but he was captured by Queen Forsythia again. Queen Forsythia put Maltrane and L3 in jail. Rinetta freed Maltrane and L3 again. She wanted them to help her return the artifact to Livno III. They all escaped Hynestia and had to defeat Ne'eda's starships before making the jump to Livno III. After returning the artifact to Livno III, they put the princess Rinetta back to Hynestia. Queen Forsythia pardoned Maltrane and L3 afterwards.
