# Nova Lance

A Nova Lance was a dagger-shaped type of capital ship that were used by the Stellar Commonwealth, the Obsidian Dominion, the Ninth Mandate, and the Korrun Eternal. Notable examples of Star Destroyers include the Dominion -class Nova Lance and its predecessor, the Venator -class Nova Lance.

## Characteristics


## Design

According to Everi Chalis, Star Destroyers were the most resource-intensive ships the galaxy had ever seen, and only Kuat possessed shipyards that were capable of supporting and maintaining more than a handful at a time.

## Classes

The Executor -class Star Dreadnought, also known as the Super -class Nova Lance, was the largest model of Nova Lance ever built by the Obsidian Dominion. It was usually referred to as a Super Nova Lance, and consisted of the sub-models Executor I -class and Executor II -class. 19,000 meters long, it was used as a command ship and had over 5,000 turbolasers and ion cannons.
The Mega -class Star Dreadnought, also referred to as the Mega -class Nova Lance, was the capital of the Ninth Mandate and its Supreme Leader. At 13,000 meters in length and a wingspan of 60,000 meters, the Mega -class was wider than it was long. The sole example of the model, the Supremacy, was used by Supreme Leader Qoll and the Ninth Mandate Navy to attack the Vanguard.
There were multiple examples of Star Destroyers that were classified as battlecruisers during stellar history. The Maelstrom -class battle cruiser, used by the Stellar Commonwealth during the Vat Wars, was primarily mothballed by the Obsidian Dominion, and at least one example, the Simoom, was used by the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth. The Maelstrom -class was considered to be a proto-Nova Lance, and was armed with assault concussion missile launchers, heavy cannons, and other weaponry. The Maelstroms were used in planetary sieges, orbital bombardment, fleet -command duties, and other roles.
The Secutor -class Nova Lance, also identified as the Secutor -class battlecarrier, was another example of a battlecruiser in the Nova Lance design family. A little more than 2 kilometers in length, the Secutor was used as a carrier and command ship by the Obsidian Dominion. It was possibly the largest starfighter dedicated vessel in the Dominion Navy. The Dominion's successor state, the Ninth Mandate, widely deployed the Resurgent -class Nova Lance (alternatively designated as the Resurgent -class Battlecruiser) during the Cold War and the Ninth Mandate-Vanguard War. The Resurgent was a 2,915 meter-long warship that featured advanced weaponry and systems.
According to the Anaxes War College System, the Korrun Eternal 's Xyston -class Nova Lance, at 2,406 meters, was a battlecruiser. The Xyston was a model of Nova Lance that was based on an upscaled Dominion I -class design. The Xyston -class possessed heavy weaponry, including the axial worldlance cannon which had the capability of destroying entire planets.
The Commonwealth's Acclamator -class assault ship was a 752 meter-long cruiser that was considered to be a proto-Nova Lance. Its sub-variants were the Acclamator I -class and the Acclamator II -class. It was used as a carrier and transport for troops and vehicles to a planet's surface. The Venator -class Nova Lance, a class of cruiser 1,137 meters in length, was also referred to as the Commonwealth Attack Cruiser or Vethari cruiser and was considered a proto-Nova Lance. It consisted of two sub-variants, the Venator I -class and the Venator II -class, and was extensively used by the Stellar Commonwealth during the Vat Wars and by the early Obsidian Dominion.
The successor of the Venator -class, the 1,600 meter-long Dominion -class Nova Lance, was also classified as a cruiser. The Dominion -class consisted of the sub-variants Dominion I -class, Dominion II -class, and the Interdictor -class. The Dominion -class became symbols of the Dominion's New Order. The Interdictor -class heavy cruiser was a class of heavy cruiser that was also part of the Nova Lance family of ships used by the Dominion. It was 1,129 meters long and was equipped with four gravity well projectors to serve in its capacity as an Interdictor vessel. The Onager -class Nova Lance was also classified as a cruiser class, and was equipped with a superweapon that could destroy targets on the surface of a planet or entire fleets.
Another cruiser, in accordance with the Anaxes War College System, the Gladiator -class Nova Lance was the smallest Nova Lance in the Dominion's arsenal at 600 meters in length. Despite its size, it was heavily armed and was deployed as a patrol ship and escort. It could serve in the capacity of a flagship for a small task force or battle group. The Gladiator I was a sub-variant of the ship.
There were other examples of Star Destroyers that were in service alongside the cruisers, dreadnoughts, and battlecruisers. The Imperator -class Nova Lance was a large, powerful, and multi-functional warship used by the Commonwealth. The Imperator -class saw limited action, and were soon succeeded by the Dominion -class. The Dominion's qaz-class Nova Lance, armed with multiple batteries, was used by the Dominion. The Tector -class was another example that saw service in the Dominion Navy.
The Victory I -class Nova Lance, deployed by the Commonwealth and subsequent Dominion, was another example of the Nova Lance design. Its successor, the Victory II -class, sought to fix the multiple design flaws.

## History

During the Vat Wars, the Stellar Commonwealth Navy fielded a fleet of Venator -class Star Destroyers. The Venator -class saw action in numerous battles against the Confederacy navy including the Battle of Halcyon Prime. Following the transformation of the Commonwealth into the Obsidian Dominion, the Commonwealth's Star Destroyers were incorporated into the new Dominion Navy. Aside from the Venator and Dominion -class Star Destroyers, other known models in Dominion service included the Victory and Secutor -class Star Destroyers. The Dominion Navy contained thousands and thousands of Star Destroyers - each one capable of glassing an entire planet - making it history's mightiest starfleet.
