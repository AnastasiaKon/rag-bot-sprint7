# Stellar Commonwealth

The Stellar Commonwealth, also known as the Grand Commonwealth or simply as the Commonwealth, was a democratic federal union spread across light-years of space. Due in part to its long history, different names were used for bygone incarnations or eras of the Commonwealth. The Old Commonwealth and the first Stellar Commonwealth were terms affixed to the original incarnation of the Commonwealth that existed until its fall to the Korrun Dominion, which in turn fell in 1032 BF, leading to the restoration of the Commonwealth in that year. Later, the modern Commonwealth that had been supplanted by the Obsidian Dominion in 19 BF was also referred to as the Old Commonwealth. Additionally, the High Commonwealth was a term used to describe the modern Commonwealth during the era in which it had reached the zenith of its power, which lasted from around 500 BF to around 100 BF.
The Commonwealth was originally founded many millennia before the Battle of Ember Reach, approximately around 25,000 BF to 20,000 BF. The Stellar Assembly governed the Commonwealth under the Stellar Constitution, and comprised thousands of senators representing the Commonwealth's member worlds. The Supreme Praetor was the title of the head of the Commonwealth, elected from ranks of the Assembly to lead the government on Halcyon Prime. An ecumenopolis or city -covered planet, referred to as Stellar City, Halcyon Prime was the capital of the Commonwealth and therefore the seat of the Assembly.
The Stellar Commonwealth and its star systems shared sovereignty, and spanned a multiplicity of worlds from the Core Worlds to the Outer Rim Territories, uniting a variety of sentient species, human and alien alike, under one banner. As its power grew, the Commonwealth fought many wars against various opponents such as Ironholders, the Zygerrian Slave Dominion, the Nihil, and the Korrun and their empires. The Vethari Circle, a religious group of Flux-sensitives dedicated to the bright current of the Synth Flux, was sworn to the service of the Assembly, and as such the Vethari Knights were hailed as the guardians of peace and justice within the Commonwealth. Centuries of conflict culminated in the fall of the Old Commonwealth, and the last war between the ancient Vethari and Korrun concluded with the restoration and rise of the modern Stellar Commonwealth, ushering in the Commonwealth Era which was an age of peace that lasted for a thousand years. The Commonwealth's history was officially reset, and the ages before this event were merged into a collective whole known as the Old Commonwealth-era.
The Commonwealth entered a period of decline after centuries of expansion and prosperity. Aided by the resurgent Korrun Lords, the Secessionist Crisis divided the galaxy between the Commonwealth loyalists and the League of Sovereign Worlds. The Grand Army of the Commonwealth was organized under the leadership of Vethari Generals who led the Commonwealth Military against the Secessionist Automaton Army during the Vat Wars. However, the conflict had been orchestrated to allow Supreme Praetor Orlan Dremmel —the alter ego of Vorth Oskaryn, the Dark Lord of the Korrun —to control and deceive the Commonwealth in a dictatorial manner. With the end of the war, Oskaryn initiated the Great Vethari Purge through Directive 9 and transformed the Commonwealth into the Obsidian Dominion by declaring himself Stellar Sovereign. The Dominion Assembly stood as the last remnant of the Old Commonwealth until it was disbanded by the Sovereign at the height of the Dominion Era. In the aftermath of the Stellar Civil War, the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth was fully reorganized into the New Commonwealth, restoring the Stellar Commonwealth after a generation of Dominion rule.

## History


## Birth of freedom

The original Stellar Commonwealth was formed between approximately 25,000 BF and 20,000 BF and rose to power by expanding across the galaxy, with several of its founding members being the planets Serenhal and Chandrila. Around 20,019 BF, the Thisspiasian Blood Monarchy joined the Stellar Commonwealth in its early formation. Multiple versions of the Commonwealth existed throughout its extensive history; historians referred to this pre-modern period as the "Old Commonwealth," although the government's official name was the Stellar Commonwealth. The capital of the Old Commonwealth was located on an ecumenopolis known as Halcyon Prime and would remain there for thousands of years.
At one time, Halcyon Prime's fellow city -world of Troithe rivaled it as the Commonwealth's cosmopolitan center, holding a population in the billions. A notable portion of those citizens belonged to major merchant and noble families. However, Halcyon Prime ultimately outpaced Troithe in industrial growth. As years passed, Troithe fell into decline. At least five millennia before the start of the Stellar Civil War, a rogue faction of Vethari broke away from the Vethari Circle, believing that practicing the shadow current of the Synth Flux would benefit the furthering of Vethari power. This schism divided the Order on ideological grounds, igniting an event known as the Hundred-Year Darkness, which, in turn, resulted in the formation of the Korrun. At one point, they managed to construct a dark shrine on Halcyon Prime. The rogue faction was ultimately defeated, and they were exiled from the Old Commonwealth into uncharted space.
At some point, the Vethari Sanctum was constructed over the Korrun shrine in order to contain its dark energy. Once it had established itself on Halcyon Prime, the Vethari Circle closely associated itself with the Old Commonwealth, serving as guardians of the peace and justice for over a thousand generations. Little did the Vethari know, the Korrun had regrouped on the planet Moraband.
