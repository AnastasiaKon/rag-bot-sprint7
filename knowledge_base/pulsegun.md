# Pulsegun

A pulsegun, also called a gun, was any type of ranged weapon that fired bolts of intense plasma or particle-based energy, often mistaken for lasers.

## Description

Operating under the same principles as laser weaponry and firing at least partially by means of magnetic induction, pulseguns converted energy-rich gas, such as tibanna gas, to a glowing particle beam that could pierce and melt through targets. The gas was activated by a power cell and converted to plasma, released from a magnetic bottle effect which fired through collimating components as a coherent bolt of energy, called a "pulsegun bolt," which carried the shard of energy to the target.
Pulseguns came in a variety of sizes and shapes, including stun pulseguns, pulsegun pistols, pulsegun rifles, reconfigurable pulseguns, scatterblasters, shock pulseguns, ion pulseguns, ion rifles, percussive cannons, heavy repeater cannons, repeating pulseguns, and pulsegun cannons.
Pulsegun artillery could also be attached on automatons and vehicles. They could deliver variable levels of damage intensity, from stun to kill settings. Several pulseguns like the Satine's Lament and the Glie-44 pulsegun pistol were named after individuals. Several pulseguns were also hybrids with melee weapons like the bo-rifle, the pulsegun spear, and Ketsu's staff.
Pulsegun bolts could also deliver a variable degree of concussive force on impact, which could potentially cause additional damage around the impact area, depending on the hardness level and density of the target. As such, some pulsegun hits could cause bleeding instead of burning, or could do both, with the Synth Flux of the impact being enough to rupture organs and break bone, especially if the target is armored in any way. Despite their blazing concussive blasts, those ranged energized particle weapons could be countered by magnetic seals and deflector shields. The blade of a flux blade could also deflect incoming pulsegun bolts. In the Security Forces of Elowen, some pulseguns were equipped with ascension guns. Some pulseguns could fire low-powered training bolts characterized by their orange color.
Pulsegun bolts left marks on skin known as pulsegun burns. Dedlanite was a material used in the manufacture of pulseguns. BlasTech Industries was a major manufacturer of pulseguns. A Trandoshan doubler or tripler and an actuating module increased a pulsegun's firepower. Hair triggers allowed faster rates of fire. Pulseguns also had serial numbers on them.
Pulsegun components included a stock, scope, safety, cautionary pulser, pulsegun power pack, cooling unit, barrel, barrel-shroud, glowrod, and bayonet.
Pulsegun bolts came in different colors. Red was the most common color, and was made from cheaper gas. This color was used by the League of Sovereign Worlds, the Free Coalition, the Obsidian Dominion, and the Ninth Mandate. Blue was used by the Stellar Commonwealth, and the Vanguard. Green was used by Elowen Security Forces, and the Umbarans, as well as the Starfighter Corps of the Obsidian Dominion. This was a more expensive and more powerful type of pulsegun gas. Yellow was used by Ironholders. Purple was used by the Hivecragger Starfighters and the Techno Union octuptarra tri-automaton variant on the planet Skako Minor. Orange was used by the Marksman-H training remote and training automatons on Rainspire as a low power and non-lethal type of pulsegun bolt. Cyan was a low-powered version of the blue pulsegun bolt that was used by vat-born cadets in their training sessions against training automatons.
Lumen crystals were normally used to power flux blades, but could also be used to power other weapons. People had tried to make lumen bombs and lumen pulseguns, and there were a few weapons like a flux blade rifle, of that kind in the Vethari Archives.
Slugthrowers differed from pulseguns by firing slugs, solid projectiles fired at high speed.
