# Vat soldier

Vat soldiers, also known as Vat-born soldiers, Commonwealth troopers, Commonwealth troops, Commonwealth soldiers, Regs, and nicknamed the " Boys in White," were highly trained soldiers in the Grand Army of the Commonwealth. Representing the future of stellar warfare, vat-born were designed to be far superior to battle automatons. During the last years of the Stellar Commonwealth, vat-born formed the backbone of the Commonwealth Military, which waged war against the automaton armies of the League of Sovereign Worlds. The Vat Wars were named after the Commonwealth's vat army, which became symbolic of the conflict that had spread throughout the galaxy after a millennium of peace.
Vat soldiers were grown on the planet Rainspire at the request of Vethari Elder Sifo-Dyas, who commissioned the Rainspirians to build an army for the Commonwealth. After Sifo Dyas' death, the project was taken over by the Korrun. Vat-born were modeled on a human template, the Ironholder contract tracker Dorn Sarvane, although their genetic structure was modified to make them less independent and more docile than their progenitor. They were also designed to age at twice the rate of natural humans, accelerating their growth and making them ready for combat in a decade's time. As the Secessionist Crisis gave way to open war, the Stellar Assembly empowered Supreme Praetor Orlan Dremmel to call the vat soldiers into action, leading to their debut on the battlefield of Hivecrag. For three years, vat-born fought the Secessionist automatons on all fronts across the galaxy. They were loyal to their Vethari Generals and the Supreme Praetor; though some vat-born questioned their service, leading to isolated cases of desertion and treason, most were proud to serve the Commonwealth as it was the only life they had ever known.
Stellar history was forever changed by a hidden behavioral modification biochip implanted in every vat-born, which was part of the Korrun's plot to destroy the Vethari. Praetor Dremmel, discovered by the Vethari to be the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn, as well as the reason behind the Vat Wars, branded the Vethari as the enemies of the state and issued Directive 9, with the latter being a call for the annihilation of the Vethari Circle. All across the galaxy, the vat-born turned against their generals, slaughtering them and storming the Vethari Sanctum on Halcyon Prime under the stewardship of the Korrun Overlord Vorth Velgor. Oskaryn subsequently assumed absolute power as the self-proclaimed Sovereign of the Obsidian Dominion. Vat-born initially served as Dominion greycoats, or vat-born greycoats, in the New Order but were eventually replaced by birth-born human recruits and conscripts. Their armor, weapons, and training inspired generations of armored warriors following on from their Dominion successors; with both the Ninth Mandate 's greycoats and the Korrun Eternal 's red-armored soldiers having been trained from early childhood to become super-soldiers much like the vat army. In particular, the latter were indoctrinated using a modified and improved upon version of the flash-training techniques previously used for the vat-born.

## History


## Origin

Years before the Invasion of Elowen, Vethari Elder Sifo-Dyas came to believe that the galaxy would soon become engulfed by war. He therefore advocated for the creation of an army to ensure the defense of the Stellar Commonwealth, but his views were ultimately rejected by his peers and he was cast out of the Vethari High Council. Undeterred by the lack of support from the other Vethari, Sifo-Dyas secretly contacted the Rainspirians —a species of experts in cloning, native to the planet Rainspire —and commissioned them to create an army of vat-born soldiers for the Commonwealth without authorization from either High Council or the Stellar Assembly. Dyas personally met with the Rainspirian Prime Minister, Lama Su. However, the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn learned of Dyas' order through unknown means.
The Korrun apprentice Vorth Morvane, who was actually Sifo Dyas' friend and the former Vethari Elder Haldrek, later claimed Dyas had worked with him to create the vat army. Shortly afterward, Sifo-Dyas was killed by the Pyke Syndicate at the behest of the Korrun, resulting in the Korrun's takeover of the vat soldier project. Morvane betrayed and orchestrated Sifo Dyas' death, and subsequently recruited the Ironholder contract tracker Dorn Sarvane to become the genetic template of the vat army. Various modifications were made to the Sarvane genome so that the vat-born would grow faster and be more susceptible to following orders. Hyper-aggressive and volatile tendencies were tailored; the Rainspirians resorted to growth acceleration in order to create mature vat-born in half the time a human took to reach maturity. Sarvane's genetic structure was further modified to make the vat-born less independent and more docile than their template. However, the vat-born were also designed to be capable of creative thinking, making them far superior to battle automatons. After being created on Rainspire, some vat-born were secretly raised on Halcyon Prime to become the shock troopers of the Halcyon Prime Guard. Sarvane became a near-mythologized figure to his vat-born, who regarded him as their father figure. Sarvane himself, however, did not feel a sense of pride in his vat-born, instead seeing them as "livestock" made to be "cannon fodder." As such, he considered them to be the Rainspirians' creations instead of his.
Sarvane was paid a considerable amount of credits for allowing the Rainspirians to use his genetic material as the vat army's template; his only other demand was the creation of an unaltered vat-born of himself, which the Rainspirians found unusual but nevertheless obliged. Whereas the vat army was genetically modified for growth acceleration and enhanced docility, Alpha was an example of pure genetic replication and therefore possessed pure, first generation Sarvane DNA. The only other vat-born like Alpha was an adolescent female known as Omega. Sarvane took Alpha to raise as his son, renaming the vat-born "Rhuk Sarvane." Omega remained in the custody of the Rainspirians and was serving as an aide of the Chief Medical Scientist Doctor Nala Se by the end of the Vat Wars. Like the rest of the Sarvane vat-born, they were regarded as Rainspirian property by Lama Su.
