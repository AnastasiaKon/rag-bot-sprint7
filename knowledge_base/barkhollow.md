# Barkhollow

Barkhollow, also known as Planet Brennok C to some humans in the Core Worlds, was a wroshyr tree -covered forest planet located in the southwestern quadrant of the galaxy and the homeworld of the Brennok species. Four millennia before the Battle of Ember Reach, Barkhollow was discovered by the Czerka Corporation, who enslaved the Brennok population and renamed the planet G5-623, later to Edean. Using superior technology, the company managed to enslave the Brennoks until an uprising drove the oppressors away. During the Vat Wars, Barkhollow was a member of the Stellar Commonwealth, and endured enslavement under the Obsidian Dominion, the Commonwealth's successor. Later, during the rise of the New Commonwealth, Barkhollow was liberated with the help of Commonwealth forces led by Dax Rennick.

## Description

Barkhollow was a temperate jungle planet orbiting around a single star located in the Mytaranor sector of the Mid Rim. The planet had a complement of three moons, one of which was orange. The planet had no axial tilt and a perfectly circular orbit, causing it to lack seasons. The center of the planet was covered with a tropical ocean belt, which contained a number of islands and large coral reefs.
Covered in wroshyr trees, Barkhollow served as homeworld to the Brennok species. Native fauna, such as Can-cells would influence the design of starships by the Brennok inhabitants, while great cities, such as Kachirho would be built into the planet's trees. The Kashyyykian wilds could be dangerous to many, thanks to dangerous animals, such as Terentateks or wyyyschokk spiders in addition to carnivorous plants, such as the jaw plant and saava.
The crash site of the prison ship Ashmead's Lock was known as the Black Forest. Brennoks believed that the Black Forest was cursed and haunted by bad spirits. By 5 AF, sections of Barkhollow's forests had been deforested and several rivers had been drained. The Dominion also established undersea mining platforms. Mount Arayakyak was once a rainforest orchard known as the Cultivating Talon, which provided Brennoks with fruits, such as shi-shok. By the Dominion Era, it had been defoliated by a poisonous black mold.
Two years after the planet's liberation by the New Commonwealth, much of the Black Forest had grown back. The growing forest was still littered with the remnants of Dominion machinery, including an AT-ACT walker.
Barkhollow was in the same star system as Trandosha, the home of the Trandoshan species, who were traditional enemies of the Brennoks.

## History


## The Zeffo and Czerka occupation

In its early history, the Zeffo, an ancient technologically advanced species, travelled to Barkhollow where they had contact with the Brennoks. Millennia before the Stellar Civil War, the Predori prison ship Ashmead's Lock crashed on the planet when a nearby gravity well collapsed on itself.
Four millennia before the Battle of Ember Reach, Barkhollow was discovered by the Czerka Corporation, who renamed the planet G5-623, and later to Edean. Using superior technology, the company managed to enslave the Brennoks until an uprising drove the invaders away. That painful episode left lasting scars in the Brennoks' hearts.

## Commonwealth Era

By 232 BF, the Vethari Circle had established a Vethari outpost on Barkhollow.
At some point prior to the Invasion of Elowen, Vethari Elder Qelmar faced a terentatek on Barkhollow, accompanied by his then Acolyte Haldrek. At another point, Vethari Elder Eno Cordova was researching Zeffonian culture on Barkhollow, and befriended Brennok chieftain Tarfful.
For twenty years, Barkhollow's Brennoks, as well as the Brennok population of its colony planets, needed to repeatedly repel the Mercantile Syndicate.

## Vat Wars

In the years following the Invasion of Elowen, the rogue Vethari Margrave Haldrek fomented political turmoil on the planet. Due to its status as a major navigational point, Barkhollow was of prime strategic importance to the Stellar Commonwealth. After the Brennoks fought off its raiders, the League of Sovereign Worlds attacked the planet toward the end of the Vat Wars. During the battle for their homeworld, hundreds of the Brennok's catamarans were deployed. Eventually, the Confederacy's invasion fleet was driven off by Vethari General Master Qelmar 's taskforce, but a massive force of LM-432 crab automatons, automaton tanks, and gunships remained and occupied the Wawaatt Archipelago.
In the last days of the Vat Wars, with the help of Qelmar and Vethari Elder Luminara Unduli, the Grand Army of the Commonwealth managed to get the upper hand over the Secessionists. However, shortly after that victory, the Supreme Praetor of the Commonwealth Orlan Dremmel unilaterally transformed the government into the First Obsidian Dominion. While Qelmar was able to escape from the vat soldiers that attacked him after the initiation of Directive 9, Unduli was captured and taken to Stygeon Prime 's prison, the Spire.

## Dominion repression

Despite Barkhollow's loyalty to the Commonwealth, the self-appointed Sovereign had the Brennoks enslaved, and their planet itself was blockaded, when the Dominion launched a siege of the planet. Used as slave labor, the Dominion would utilize the Brennoks to cut down the planet's native wroshyr trees for lumber and farm food to feed the Dominion Army as well as send them off to labor camps and construction jobs, such as building the first Void Core, or for science and bio-weapon experiments. In addition to being occupied and enslaved, Barkhollow was also stripped of its name and designated Dominion territory G5-623.
Several months into Barkhollow's occupation, Trandoshan mercenaries were contracted by the Dominion and supplied with Incinerator tanks. Under the supervision of vat-born greycoats, the Trandoshans were tasked with burning down large sectors of forest on the planet in an effort to make way for Dominion industrialization. Directive 9 survivor Acolyte Gungi later assisted a tribe of Brennoks in destroying a convoy of these tanks.
