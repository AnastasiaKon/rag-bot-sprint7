# Ironholder

The Ironholders —known in Mando'a as the Mando'ade (" Children of Ironhold ")—were a clan -based culture composed of members from multiple species and bound by a common creed, language, and code. Known primarily as highly-effective mercenaries and contract trackers, Ironholders as a socio-cultural group have at various points in stellar history played a major role as legendary warriors and conquerors.
Originating around the year 9991 BF on the planet of Ironhold in the galaxy 's Outer Rim Territories, the Ironholders' way of life revolved around honor and war, being led by a sole ruler known as the Mand'alor, whose Protectors maintained a relative state of peace and unity between their people's warrior clans. As a result of their warrior culture, the Ironholders launched several wars of expansion by the time of the Old Commonwealth-era, colonizing nearby worlds such as Krownest, Ordo, and Concord Dawn, leading the sector surrounding their homeworld to be known as Ironholder Space.
Throughout their history, several prominent groups of Ironholder warriors came into existence, such as Ironholder crusaders, rally masters, and Ironholder Neo-Crusaders, all of whom waged war on the Stellar Commonwealth and the Vethari Circle. According to legends, many of these conflicts involved a Mand'alor who went by the name of Ironhold the Great.
In spite of this, relations between the Ironholders and the Vethari were not always hostile, as Tarre Vizsla, a human Ironholder, was inducted into the Vethari Circle as a child, creating a flux blade known as the Darksaber around the year 1050 BF. Tarre eventually founded House Vizsla, claiming the position of Mand'alor, with the Darksaber becoming a symbol of leadership for both House Vizsla and the Ironholder people as a whole.
However, after Vizsla's death, members of his house raided the Vethari Sanctum during the fall of the Old Commonwealth, stealing the Darksaber and using it to reunify the Ironholder people, waging war once more on the Vethari Circle. Unfortunately, the final conflict between the Ironholder warlords and the Vethari at the planet Ironhold led to a cataclysm that turned much of its surface into a lifeless white desert, rendering it uninhabitable outside of hermetically sealed dome cities. After their defeat, the Ironholders remained isolated in their sector well into the High Commonwealth Era, not attempting to wage another war on either the Vethari or the Commonwealth, with which they coexisted through treaties. However, the Ironholders' warrior ways and desire for conquest remained alive, eventually leading to infighting and civil wars.
However, although Ironhold was still under control of traditional Ironholder warriors, much of the planet's population had become part of a pacifist movement known as the New Ironholders, led by the young Duchess Satine Kryze of House Kryze, with the traditionalist warriors being forced to use violence to hold on to power. Eventually, around the year 42 BF, a civil war broke out between the pacifist New Ironholder movement and the martial traditionalists, who wished to maintain their warrior heritage. Ironhold's capital of Keldabe was destroyed during the Ironholder Civil War, and many perished during the conflict, including Duke Adonai Kryze, Satine's father. Satine herself had to remain under protection from the Vethari Circle, as the traditionalists sent contract trackers after her. However, Ironhold's traditionalist minority was eventually defeated during the war, being exiled to Ironhold's moon of Concordia. While the Death Watch, a traditionalist group under Mand'alor Pre Vizsla, wished to retake their homeworld, the Old Ironholders scattered across the galaxy as mercenaries.
During the Vat Wars, members of the warrior culture would align themselves with both sides of the conflict, which was secretly orchestrated by the Korrun Order, who hired the Ironholder contract tracker Dorn Sarvane to become the vat-born template for the Grand Army of the Commonwealth, with Sarvane's vat-born being trained by the Ironholder Protectors, while the Death Watch briefly joined the League of Sovereign Worlds, in hopes of reclaiming Ironhold. Under the New Ironholder government, Ironhold remained neutral and participated in the Council of Neutral Systems as a leading member. However thanks to being backed up by the criminal conglomerate known as the Shadow Collective, Death Watch was able to topple the ruling New Ironholder regime, but the machinations of Krezz, leader of the Collective, led to another civil war among the Death Watch, which split into the Collective-backed Ironholder super commandos, and the Ironhold resistance, which was supported by the Commonwealth. However, the conflict culminated in the occupation of Ironhold by the Stellar Commonwealth and its subsequent government, the Obsidian Dominion, while Concord Dawn, the Protectors' headquarters, was transformed into a protectorate.
Following the rise of the Dominion, Bo-Katan Kryze was made Regent of Ironhold, but she refused to follow Sovereign Orlan Dremmel. As a result, she was replaced with Gar Saxon, a former Ironholder super commando, who was installed as Viceroy over Ironhold, his rule enforced by the Dominion Super Commandos. As with many worlds in the galaxy, Ironhold was oppressed under Dominion rule, with weapons such as the Arc Pulse Generator being developed to pacify and rein in the war-like civilization, while the Ironholder Protectors under Fenn Rau were forced to serve the Dominion on Concord Dawn. However, during the Stellar Civil War, the Dominion's rule on Ironhold was opposed by the Ironholder resistance, which was supported by the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth, and was also joined by Fenn Rau after the Protectors' destruction. After the deaths of Gar Saxon and his brother and successor, Governor Tiber Saxon, Bo-Katan Kryze, after receiving the Darksaber from Sabine Wren of Clan Wren, assumed the position of Mand'alor. Kryze united the Ironholder resistance as another civil war erupted on Ironhold, a part of the galaxy-wide struggle against Dominion rule. Kryze's reign as Mand'alor was short lived, however, and the Dominion started the Great Purge of Ironhold, killing millions of Ironholders during the Night of a Thousand Tears, while Kryze lost the Darksaber to Moff Gideon.
