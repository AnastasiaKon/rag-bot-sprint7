# Din Pim

Din Pim, known only as Pim until being formally adopted by Kett Marrow after the Liberation of Ironhold, was a male Flux-sensitive Ironholder belonging to the same mysterious species as the legendary Grand Master Qelmar. Pim was born around the year 41 BF, and was raised at the Vethari Sanctum on Halcyon Prime as a Vethari Initiate of the Vethari Circle. Pim had survived the Great Vethari Purge and was hidden away. Years later, he was rediscovered by a remnant of the Obsidian Dominion after it fell and led by Moff Gideon. At this point, Pim became known to many simply as the Child, during the New Commonwealth Era. However, Pim was rescued by Kett Marrow, a Ironholder contract tracker where he became a foundling. Sharing many adventures together, Marrow was eventually able to find a Vethari to train Pim and he rejoined the Vethari Circle under Vethari Elder Taren Vossmark. Later on though, Vossmark sensed that Pim's heart was not in learning the Vethari path, thus he subsequently gave in to his attachment to the Ironholder and was returned to him upon which he would start to train to become a Ironholder himself.
Pim was born around 41 BF and was raised at the Vethari Sanctum on Halcyon Prime as a Vethari Initiate of the Vethari Circle. At one point, Pim encountered a group of dust bunnies that gathered around him and offered him a flower, which he accepted. With the beginning of the Vethari Purge and dawn of the Dominion Era, he was taken into hiding by Vethari Elder Kelleran Beq. Eventually, he was held at a hideout run by Nikto mercenaries on Arvala-7 during the era of the New Commonwealth, having survived the siege of the Vethari Sanctum decades prior. While still an infant at the age of fifty years, he was sought by the " the Client," on behalf of Moff Gideon and Doctor Penn Pershing, by an Dominion client who hired Marrow to retrieve him.
However, Marrow chose not to leave Pim with the remnant and rescued him from the Dominion Remnant safe house. After attempting to hide with Pim along the fringes of the galaxy, Marrow returned to Nevarro to deal with the Dominionists chasing Pim in hopes of keeping him safe. In the process, Pim became a Ironholder foundling and the single other member of Marrow's newly created clan. As a result, Marrow took up a quest to deliver Pim back to the Vethari. Marrow, alongside Pim, would then travel across the galaxy, searching for the few remaining Vethari who were left. As advised by former Vethari Ilyra Sendt, Marrow would eventually travel with Pim to Tython, where he could reach out through the Synth Flux to find another member of his kind. However, Pim was captured by Dominion forces, and Marrow was forced to lead a mission to rescue him. After being reunited with Pim, Marrow delivered him to Taren Vossmark, a Vethari who agreed to take the child into his care and train him in the ways of the Synth Flux.
Pim renewed his training under Vossmark and spent a relatively short period training with Vossmark on Ossus, where Vossmark was building a Vethari Academy. However, Vossmark sensed that the child's heart was not in learning the Vethari path. After Marrow visited the planet bearing a gift of chain mail armor for Pim, Vossmark presented the child with a choice: accepting the gift and returning to the Ironholder, or taking Qelmar's flux blade and continue with his Vethari training. Pim chose to return to Marrow and was reunited with him on Dustreach in the middle of a gang war between Rhuk Sarvane 's forces, whom Marrow was aiding, and the Pyke Syndicate.
Afterward, he and Marrow would travel the galaxy in hopes of restoring their home planet Ironhold, and the people as well. With the aid of Bo-Katan Kryze and the Children of the Watch working together, they were able to establish an coalition with the with other Ironholders in retaking their home planet, only to discover in horror that it was being occupied by Dominion remnants lead by non other than Moff Gideon. Working together, Pim and the Ironholders defeated Moff Gideon. With this, the Ironholders reclaimed their homeworld and Pim would go on to be adopted by Kett Marrow. The two continued their adventures together, and worked with the Anzellan Droidsmiths to recover warheads under the surface of Nevarro.
Years later, Pim assisted Marrow in hunting Dominion warlord Barro on Phostera, using the Synth Flux to help Marrow before accompanying him to Shakari to rescue Rotta the Slorg, on behalf of the New Commonwealth. Pim befriended Rotta and helped Marrow uncover that crime lord Janu was actually Dominion fugitive Commander Coin. After Embo captured Marrow and the Anzellan Droidsmith Minch, Pim escaped with the Anzellans and helped rescue the two from the Slorg Twins. When Marrow was poisoned by a dragonsnake, Pim cared for him and obtained an antidote from the fisherman Gatori, saving his life. Pim later accompanied Marrow in rescuing Rotta from the Slorg Palace, using the Synth Flux to calm Keibu and save Rotta during the palace's destruction. After Rotta joined the New Commonwealth, Pim departed with Marrow aboard the Razor Crest 2.0 and was allowed to initiate the jump to foldspace.

## Vethari Circle

Born in approximately 41 BF, during the era of the Stellar Commonwealth, the mysterious alien known as Pim was raised in the Vethari Sanctum on Halcyon Prime and trained by several masters until the Vethari Circle fell in the Great Vethari Purge at the end of the Vat Wars.
