# Osric Vaughn

Osric Vaughn was an Eriaduan bureaucrat, military officer, and politician whose career spanned the waning years of the Stellar Commonwealth into the Dominion Era. A member of Eriadu 's prominent Vaughn family, the man served the Commonwealth's Judicial Department before returning to his homeworld to serve as its governor. When the Vat Wars began between the Commonwealth and its splinter state, the League of Sovereign Worlds, Vaughn enlisted in the Commonwealth Navy. He served under Vethari General Even Piell until his death on Lola Sayu, where the two were held as prisoners by the Confederacy for having memorized half of the Nexus Route. Saved by a Vethari -led rescue team, Vaughn grew to respect Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark, who agreed with the captain's belief that the Vethari Code conflicted with their wartime duties.
Vaughn staunchly opposed the Vethari Circle 's role as leaders in the Grand Army of the Commonwealth, believing that peacekeepers should not direct the Commonwealth war effort. A favorite of Supreme Praetor Orlan Dremmel, Vaughn served on the Strategic Advisory Cell and was promoted to the rank of admiral. Less than a year after his promotion, Vaughn led the military's investigation into the bombing of the Vethari Sanctum. At the conclusion of the investigation, Vaughn was promoted to Adjutant General of the Grand Army of the Commonwealth, allowing him to act as the prosecutor in the trial of Ilyra Sendt, accusing her of treason. Vaughn continued to rise in power with the proclamation of a new regime, the Obsidian Dominion, ruled by the self-appointed Sovereign Dremmel.
Joining the ranks of the New Order 's regional governors, Vaughn oversaw the needs of the new Dominion Army and, alongside Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart, administered Project War-Mantle to transition the vat soldier army into an army of conscripted soldiers. As the Dominion's first High Warden, he oversaw the Outer Rim Territories and was involved in the secret construction of the VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station. By then, Vaughn was seen as the third member of the unofficial triumvirate that led the Dominion, including the Sovereign himself and Vorth Velgor, whom he once knew as Kaelen Vossmark. As provincial administrator of the regions of space along the stellar frontier, High Warden Vaughn sought to enforce Dominion rule on restive worlds such as Lothal, where coalition cells grew increasingly bold in their opposition to the Dominion.
A proponent of the doctrine of fear, High Warden Vaughn believed that the Void Core battle station and its planet-killing superweapon would terrify the galaxy into total submission. At his command, the Void Core destroyed Serenhal, instantly killing billions of Serenhalis. He was confident in wiping out the Uprising by targeting their headquarters on Ember Reach 4, but he perished when the Void Core was destroyed by Taren Vossmark at the Battle of Ember Reach. As a result, Vaughn's memory was tarnished in the views of some Dominion officers, such as Grand General Cassio Tagge, who referred to the Void Core as "Vaughn's folly." The Ninth Mandate, having risen to power as the old Dominion's successor opposing the New Commonwealth, commemorated the High Warden in an effort to portray Dominion leaders in a more heroic light.

## Biography


## Early life

Osric Vaughn was born on the planet Eriadu around 64 BF into the wealthy but hardened Vaughn family. He lived with his family in the family compound, where the Vaughns had lived for over a millennium. Wilhuff's father and mother taught him that Eriadu had not always been the safe environment that it seemed—the entire planet had once been an untamed wilderness. The jungle and predators had been more of a threat than pirates and marauders. The early Vaughns had helped settle Eriadu; they had been their own police force. Later a militia had been formed—under Vaughn leadership. Wilhuff took these history lessons to heart. Wilhuff's parents made sure that he knew that respect, discipline and obedience were of the utmost importance. Because of this Wilhuff thought of himself as a product of a military upbringing. One night while at supper Vaughn's father ordered a servant to take Wilhuff's plate before he could eat—teaching him how easy it is to lose something.
According to a story told by the Espirion Saan, Vaughn had a brother named Gideon, who called Wilhuff "Wil," while Wilhuff called his brother "Gid." The story continued that Vaughn, along with his brother and father, ventured to the planet Eadu in a survey expedition for lommite. In the midst of a storm, Gideon and Wilhuff became separated, with Gideon hurting his ankle in a fall and calling out to his brother for help. Wilhuff, however, abandoned his brother to the wilds of Eadu. Upon reuniting with their father, who revealed the survey had found no lommite, back at their starship, a smiling Wilhuff was asked where his brother was.
In 48 BF, following family tradition, Wilhuff's grand-uncle Jova Vaughn, along with two other relatives and two Rodian guides, took him on trips to the Carrion Plateau, on which he taught the young survival skills and lessons on the savage wilderness that his ancestors had tamed. After passing a test in which he outwitted a pack of predators at the Carrion Spike, sixteen year -old Vaughn was deemed to have completed his testing on the plateau. Six months after passing this test, Wilhuff's training shifted to space combat. More family members and hired trainers taught him tactics and piloting skills, while admonishing him to apply the lessons he learned as a hunter to control the pirates and lawbreakers of the Outer Rim.
