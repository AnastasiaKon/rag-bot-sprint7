# Kett Marrow

Kett Marrow, also known as " the Ironholder," or simply " Mando," was a human male Ironholder warrior during the era of the New Commonwealth. With his Ironholder armor, IB-94 pulsegun pistol, Amban sniper rifle, and distinctive greysteel helmet, Marrow was both well-equipped and enigmatic—a stranger whose past was shrouded in mystery to others. An orphan born on Aq Vetina in the waning years of the Stellar Commonwealth, he was raised on Concordia as a foundling by the Children of the Watch, an orthodox religious sect that had broken off from mainstream Ironholder society. He vowed to walk the Way of the Ironhold, swearing to never again reveal his face to another living being, and eventually joined the Tribe, which operated in a secret covert on the planet Nevarro. After the fall of the Obsidian Dominion, Marrow made a reputation for himself as a member of the Contract trackers' Guild. Far from the authority of the New Commonwealth, he traveled across the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories in his personal ST-70 Assault Ship, the Razor Crest, collecting bounties and delivering them, warm or cold, to Guild Master Greef Karga. Marrow became battle-hardened, a man of few words, and a formidable hunter in an increasingly dangerous galaxy.
In 9 AF, Marrow was hired on Nevarro by a Dominion faction to acquire a mysterious asset. The Ironholder tracked the bounty to the world Arvala-7 and discovered that his quarry was a Flux-sensitive infant belonging to the same species as Vethari Grand Master Qelmar. A paternal bond soon formed between Marrow and the child, who the hunter later learned to be named Pim. The outwardly-emotionless Ironholder could not bring himself to surrender the youngling to the Dominionists and absconded with the child, bringing him into conflict with the brutal warlord, Moff Gideon. Marrow, alongside Karga, former shock trooper Cara Dune, and others, went on to drive the Moff's forces out of Nevarro City and reunite with the Armorer, the leader of his covert and staunch upholder of the Ironholder Creed. She christened Marrow and Pim as a clan of two, commanding the Ironholder to care for the child until he could deliver him to the remaining Vethari Knights, few in number and scattered across space.
Marrow and Pim scoured the galaxy in search of the fabled masters of the Synth Flux, battling vicious foes and forging new alliances. These adventures saw Marrow cross paths with several faces that challenged his notions of what it meant to be a Ironholder; these included Cobb Vanth, a Tatooinian marshal who used scavenged greysteel armor to defend a small town; Bo-Katan Kryze, a princess of Ironhold who laid claim to the planet's unoccupied throne; and Rhuk Sarvane, a legendary contract tracker and Ironholder vat-born who sought to reclaim his legacy. After encountering the wandering ronin Ilyra Sendt and fighting in a series of skirmishes against the Dominion Remnant, the Ironholder and his allies eventually captured Moff Gideon and meet with Taren Vossmark, a Vethari Elder that promised to train Pim as his apprentice. Breaking his vows to the Creed, Marrow removed his helmet as he bid farewell, letting his ward see his face for the first time.
Having defeated Gideon in combat, Marrow had inadvertently claimed ownership of the Moff's weapon, the Darksaber, a mythic flux blade that identified whosoever wielded it as the leader of the Ironholder people. Reluctant to rule but unable to relinquish the ceremonial blade, Marrow returned to the Armorer, who branded him an apostate for removing his helmet. Marrow went on to fight alongside Rhuk Sarvane in the Battle of Mos Espa on Dustreach and soon reunited with Pim, who had chosen to abandon his Vethari training in favor of staying with the Ironholder. Seeking to regain his status as a Child of the Watch, Marrow ventured into the Mines of Ironhold and bathed in the Living Waters, renewing himself in the eyes of the Armorer. During his quest, Marrow was disarmed by a being that was later slain by Kryze, a sequence of events that placed ownership of the Darksaber in her hands. Uniting their disparate Ironholder sects, Kryze and Marrow led a reconquest of Ironhold, freeing the planet from the Dominion Remnant and killing Moff Gideon. With their enemy defeated, the Ironholder and Pim settled into a quiet cabin on Nevarro where they chose to live peacefully, until such time that their allies in the New Commonwealth called on them to hunt down more rogue Dominion warlords. Marrow and Pim worked with the Anzellan Droidsmiths to recover warheads under the surface of Nevarro.
Years later, Marrow assisted Garazeb Orrelios in hunting the Dominion warlord Barro on Phostera, ultimately killing him after a fierce pursuit despite the New Commonwealth's preference to capture him alive. Rewarded with a new Razor Crest, Marrow accepted a mission from Colonel Ward to rescue Rotta the Slorg in exchange for information on the elusive Dominion Commander Coin. On Shakari, he discovered Rotta had become a renowned gladiator and was unwilling to return to the Slorg Twins, believing they sought his death. After fighting alongside Rotta in a deadly arena, Marrow learned that the local crime lord Janu was in fact Commander Coin, and captured the Dominion leader with Orrelios' help. Marrow delivered Coin to the New Commonwealth and returned to Nevarro. After arranging Rotta's safe escape, Marrow and the Anzellan Droidsmith Minch were captured by the contract tracker Embo and delivered to the Twins, but escaped imprisonment with Pim and the Anzellan's aid. After recovering from dragonsnake venom, he led an attack on the Slorg Palace, rescued Rotta, and battled Embo and the Twins. Having discovered the Slorg's collaboration with the Dominion Remnant, New Commonwealth forces joined in battle over Nal Hutta, destroying the palace. Following the mission, Rotta chose to remain with the New Commonwealth, while Marrow and Pim departed aboard the Razor Crest 2.0 to continue their travels.
