# Grathok

Grathok, known affectionately to his friends as Grath, was a Brennok male warrior, runner, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Vat Wars, the Stellar Civil War, the Cold War, and the subsequent Ninth Mandate-Vanguard War. He hailed from the planet Barkhollow where he was born during the High Commonwealth Era and became a Brennok military leader. During the Vat Wars, he was captured by Trandoshan slavers and held captive on Wasskah, but he worked with a fellow captive, Vethari Commander Ilyra Sendt, to escape. He later commanded Brennok forces of the Kachirho Brennok Militia during the Battle of Barkhollow alongside the Grand Army of the Commonwealth, led by Vethari General Qelmar. During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Qelmar's vat soldiers received Directive 9 from the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn and, with the help of Grathok and his fellow Brennok Chieftain and General Tarfful, Qelmar escaped Barkhollow and the destruction of the Vethari Circle.
In the years that followed, during the rule of the Obsidian Dominion, Grathok was enslaved by the Dominion until he was rescued by Corporal Dax Rennick, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Wandering Kestrel during a raid on Kessel for Crimson Dawn with Tobias Beckett and Qi'ra. Winning the Kestrel from Jorek Balthen Maltrane, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Grubbol the Slorg until Rennick was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Grathok and Rennick were hired by Vethari Elder Orrin Valdane to ferry him, Taren Vossmark, VN-4, and KX-7 to Serenhal on a mission vital to the survival of the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth. After finding Serenhal was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Mirelle Thandrel escape from captivity and return to the Coalition base on Ember Reach 4. Though Grathok and Rennick initially chose to leave Ember Reach 4 and not join the Uprising, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Ember Reach and were instrumental in Vossmark's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Uprising.
Throughout the war, Grathok and Rennick fought the Dominion in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the battle on Vrogas Vas. Along with Princess Thandrel and VN-4, they escaped from Echo Base on the planet Frostmere after the Dominion tracked the Free Coalition there. The group sought refuge with Maltrane on Cloud City, but Maltrane betrayed them and turned them over to Vorth Velgor, the Sovereign 's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Rennick was frozen in carbonite and Maltrane helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Rennick from being taken by Rhuk Sarvane to Grubbol the Slorg. Grathok and Maltrane set off to find Rennick and located him in Grubbol's Palace on Dustreach, where Grathok and his friends saved Rennick and destroyed Grubbol. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Thornvale, where Grathok helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Coalition Fleet to destroy the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station. Oskaryn was killed for the first time aboard the battle station, delivering a crippling blow to the Dominion.
After Thornvale and the rise of the New Commonwealth, Grathok and Rennick fought to liberate Barkhollow from the Dominion. When the war finally came to a close, Grathok returned home to Barkhollow with his family. After Rennick's son Corvin, turned to the shadow current and destroyed Vossmark's new generation of Vethari, Grathok reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Wandering Kestrel and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana, but they eventually found the Kestrel and, with it, stowaways named Ilsa, Finn, and the automaton BB-8. The automaton had part of a map that led to the exiled Taren Vossmark, so the group brought the map to the Vanguard that opposed the Ninth Mandate, which had risen from the ashes of the Obsidian Dominion. Grathok soon fought in the assault to destroy Ashen Bastion, a devastating Ninth Mandate superweapon, a conflict in which Rennick lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Grathok joined Ilsa in traveling to the planet Ahch-To on the Kestrel, where they found Taren Vossmark. After Rennick's death, Grathok often served as the sole pilot of the Kestrel, including piloting the ship to aid the Vanguard during the Battle of Crait.
Roughly a year after the battle of Crait, Grathok was one of the last of the Vanguard. Having learned of the mysterious return of Vorth Oskaryn, Grathok and the crew of the Kestrel, now including Poe Dameron, traveled to the planets Pasaana and Kijimi to find a clue to how to find where the Sovereign was hiding, being reunited with his friend Maltrane in the process. The quest nearly ended in Grathok's apparent death, as Ilsa, having been revealed to be the Sovereign's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Vaen Morrow and destroyed a Ninth Mandate transport that she had thought Grathok had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.
However, Grathok survived and was rescued by the Vanguard as they continued the quest. Ilsa eventually found Nullhaven during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming a Korrun, and led the charge during the battle that ensued later on. Ilsa finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Corvin Rennick in the climax of the battle, while the Vanguard, joined by the Citizens' Fleet, progressively overpowered the Korrun Eternal 's fleet, the Final Order, that Oskaryn planned to unleash.
