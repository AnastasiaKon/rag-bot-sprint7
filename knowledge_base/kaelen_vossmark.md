# Kaelen Vossmark

Kaelen Vossmark was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male who was a Vethari Warden of the Stellar Commonwealth and the prophesied Foretold One of the Vethari Circle, destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux. Also known as " Ani " during his childhood, Vossmark earned the moniker " Hero With No Fear " from his accomplishments in the Vat Wars. His alter ego, Vorth Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Korrun, was created when Vossmark turned to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn at the end of the Commonwealth Era.
A vergence in the Synth Flux, Kaelen Vossmark was born on the desert planet of Dustreach in the Outer Rim Territories in 41 BF. He was the son of Shmi Vossmark, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand aether-cells, surpassing Grand Master Qelmar and all other Vethari in the galaxy. Alder Fenwick, the Vethari Elder who discovered Vossmark on Dustreach in 32 BF, theorized that Vossmark was conceived by the aether-cells. Following the Battle of Elowen, the Vethari High Council admitted Kaelen Vossmark into the Order as the Acolyte of Vethari Warden Orrin Valdane despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Vethari Code. Vossmark remained a Acolyte through the beginning of the Vat Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Margrave Haldrek. Although Vethari doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Kaelen Vossmark had a secret wife, Senator Seraphine Olwyn of Elowen. During the early days of the Vat Wars, Vossmark served as a Vethari General in the Grand Army of the Commonwealth, commanding the vat soldiers of the elite 501st Legion against the League of Sovereign Worlds. After receiving his knighthood, Vossmark oversaw the Vethari training of his own apprentice, Ilyra Sendt. By 19 BF, when Sendt walked away from the Vethari Circle, Vossmark began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.
Following the death of Margrave Haldrek during the Battle of Halcyon Prime, Kaelen Vossmark's faith in the Vethari was further shaken when he was put on the Vethari Conclave without the rank of master. He also learned that Supreme Praetor Orlan Dremmel was Vorth Oskaryn, the elusive Dark Lord of the Korrun who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Vethari had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Olwyn in childbirth, Vossmark betrayed and turned against the Vethari Circle, believing Oskaryn had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Vorth Velgor, the fallen Vethari Warden marched his legion on the Vethari Sanctum of Halcyon Prime where they executed its inhabitants, including Vethari younglings, as part of Directive 9 and the Great Vethari Purge. Velgor was then sent to Cindaros to exterminate the Secessionist Council at the behest of his Korrun Master, who became the self-proclaimed Stellar Sovereign. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Valdane, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Taren Vossmark and Princess Mirelle Thandrel who were hidden from their father and the Sovereign as the Obsidian Dominion rose to power.
Being the Sovereign's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet, Velgor enforced the rule of the New Order as the Sovereign's Korrun apprentice throughout most of the Dominion Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Dominion Military. In the aftermath of killing Valdane in a duel aboard the Void Core and the subsequent Battle of Ember Reach, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Taren to the shadow current. Taren sought to become a Vethari, like his father before him, and believed that Velgor had the potential to turn back to the bright current of the Synth Flux. Velgor was defeated by Taren during the Battle of Thornvale in 4 AF, but the young Vethari refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Sovereign to torture Taren with Flux lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Velgor that was still Kaelen, resulting in a redeemed Vossmark killing Oskaryn at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Korrun and fulfilled his destiny as the Foretold One, Vossmark made peace with his son and became one with the Synth Flux.
Kaelen Vossmark was one of the most powerful Vethari and Korrun in stellar history. The Synth Flux was especially strong in the Vossmark bloodline, which culminated in Kaelen's grandson, Corvin Rennick, Thandrel's son who was born by the end of the Stellar Civil War in 5 AF. Rennick would turn to the shadow current as well, becoming the dark warrior Vaen Morrow during the rise of the Ninth Mandate. Like his grandfather, however, Rennick found redemption by sacrificing his life for the love of another—the Vethari apprentice Ilsa, who heard Vossmark's voice telling her to restore the balance, as he once had, during the Battle of Nullhaven in 35 AF. Rennick's death ended the Vossmark bloodline, although Ilsa, the granddaughter of Oskaryn, rejected her true heritage and took their surname to honor their legacy.

## Biography


## Early life

Believed to have been conceived by the aether-cells, Kaelen Vossmark was born to the slave Shmi Vossmark. Although Vossmark was listed as being born on the desert planet of Dustreach in some sources, confusion existed as to where Vossmark was actually born; whereas some records reported his birthplace as Dustreach, others claimed he moved to the planet with his mother at a very young age. Regardless, Dustreach was Vossmark's homeworld. While living on Dustreach, Vossmark and his mother were originally owned by Gardulla the Slorg until she lost them to the Toydarian Watto in a podracing bet when Vossmark was around age 3. Affectionately nicknamed "Ani," he worked in his master's shop, located in Mos Espa.
