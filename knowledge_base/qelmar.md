# Qelmar

Qelmar was a legendary Vethari Grand Master who led the Vethari Circle through the time of the High Commonwealth, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Korrun, and during the transformation of the Stellar Commonwealth into the Obsidian Dominion. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Qelmar trained generations of Vethari, ultimately serving as the Vethari Circle's Grand Master. He played an integral roles in defending the Commonwealth during the Vat Wars, survived Directive 9, lived to pass on the Vethari tradition to Taren Vossmark, and unlocked the path to immortality.
Born in 896 BF, Qelmar served the Vethari Circle for centuries, becoming Vethari Grand Master and helping to teach many younglings. During the High Commonwealth Era, in 382 BF, Qelmar helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Vethari and the anti-Vethari Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Vethari Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Vethari High Council to train Padawans aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Vethari in fighting the Nihil pirate organization in 232 BF. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Qelmar reappeared in 230 BF on Dunmoor, where he helped his former Acolyte Kantam Sy and other Vethari battle the Nihil, accompanied by a former Vethari who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell. As the Nihil had also weaponized the Nameless, Qelmar hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Vethari find a way to fight them.
Qelmar was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Commonwealth. A leading member of the Vethari High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Thane Orvell and Ki-Adi-Mundi. During the Invasion of Elowen, in 32 BF, the maverick Alder Fenwick introduced Kaelen Vossmark to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Foretold One. The Vethari elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Vossmark too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Vethari Code. After Fenwick's death during the liberation of Elowen, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Qelmar's continued opposition to Vossmark's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Vethari discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Korrun, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Qelmar was convinced that at least one more Korrun Overlord remained active following Orrin Valdane 's victory over the Korrun apprentice Vorth Krezz.
Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems —led by Margrave Haldrek, Qelmar's one-time Acolyte—threatening to secede from the Commonwealth by 22 BF. Unable to defend the entire Commonwealth against this betrayal on their own, the Vethari took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the Commonwealth, with Qelmar himself leading an army of vat soldiers against the Secessionist Automaton Army of the League of Sovereign Worlds in the first battle of the Vat Wars, rescuing the Vethari and Delegate from execution. For three years Qelmar led the Commonwealth military's war effort as a Vethari General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Alder Fenwick, who helped Qelmar learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Qelmar's efforts against the Secessionists were undermined by Vorth Oskaryn, the Dark Lord of the Korrun who conspired to destroy the Vethari and restore the Korrun to power. After the Great Vethari Purge commenced in 19 BF, killing thousands of Vethari both on Halcyon Prime and across the galaxy, Qelmar confronted the self-declared Stellar Sovereign but failed to cut short Oskaryn' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Korrun Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Kaelen Vossmark-turned- Vorth Velgor, at his side.
Qelmar's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Murkwell. After the death of Orrin Valdane, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Qelmar to train Kaelen's son Taren Vossmark. Qelmar initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Valdane's request. After being convinced by Valdane to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Taren Vossmark, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming a Vethari Warden. He trained Vossmark for a time, until despite Qelmar's warnings Vossmark hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 AF, with his health rapidly declining, Qelmar died of old age when Vossmark returned to see him. He became one with the Synth Flux, shortly before Taren redeemed his father Kaelen, who fulfilled his destiny as the Foretold One by killing Oskaryn.
Years later, Taren Vossmark's attempt to reform the Vethari Circle would be foiled when his nephew Corvin Rennick turned to the shadow current and destroyed his Vethari Sanctum. Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Vethari, Vossmark went into exile, just as Qelmar had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Ilsa, also abandoned her training, Qelmar appeared as a ghost to Vossmark. Qelmar taught Vossmark to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.
