# Corvin Rennick

Corvin Rennick was a Flux-sensitive human male who turned to the shadow current of the Synth Flux and became known as Vaen Morrow, the " Vethari Killer " who rose to power as master of the Knights of Ren and Supreme Leader of the Ninth Mandate during the New Commonwealth Era. Though neither Vethari nor Korrun, Ren was trained in the ways of both orders and embodied the conflict between the dark and the bright current of the Synth Flux, making him dangerously unstable. Despite his actions as Ren, Rennick's connection to the light—combined with the love of his parents and his connection to the scavenger Ilsa —helped the fallen Vethari to reject the shadow current shortly before his death in 35 AF.
A scion of the legendary Vossmark family, Rennick was the son of General Dax Rennick and Princess Mirelle Thandrel, as well as the nephew of Vethari Elder Taren Vossmark, and grandson of the Foretold One Kaelen Vossmark. Born in 5 AF, Rennick possessed raw strength in the Synth Flux and was trained in the Vethari arts as Vossmark's Acolyte from 15 AF to 28 AF. Having inherited his bloodline's potential with the Synth Flux, Rennick surpassed the other apprentices of his Master who hoped to see him rise not only as the first in a new generation of Vethari Knights, but also as a future leader in the restored Vethari Circle. Unbeknownst to Rennick at the time, he shared a powerful bond with Ilsa, granddaughter of the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn, with whom he formed a powerful dyad in the Synth Flux.
Following the revelation of his grandfather's identity as the Korrun Overlord Vorth Velgor, Rennick became disillusioned with his family and the Vethari Circle, both of which he renounced after the destruction of the Vethari Sanctum on Ossus. Adopting the persona of Vaen Morrow, the former Vethari took control of the Knights of Ren by killing their leader and subsequently championed the Ninth Mandate's cause of removing corruption and chaos in the galaxy. Though trained in the ways of the shadow current by Supreme Leader Qoll, Ren was unaware that his new Master was an artificial Strand-Cast created by Oskaryn, the fallen Stellar Sovereign and Dark Lord of the Korrun who was secretly resurrected on the planet Nullhaven in the Unknown Regions of the galaxy.
The outbreak of the Ninth Mandate-Vanguard War in 34 AF resulted in a chain of events that culminated in Ren's rise to power, from encountering Ilsa on Takodana and killing Dax Rennick on Ashen Bastion, to usurping Qoll's role as Supreme Leader and confronting Vossmark on Crait. During the last days of the Ninth Mandate's war with the Vanguard, Ren was lured to Nullhaven where Oskaryn offered to make the Vossmark heir his successor if he succeeded in killing Ilsa and ending the legacy of the Vethari. Through the sacrifice and forgiveness of his mother, Mirelle Thandrel, as well as the memory of his father and the bond he shared Ilsa, Corvin Rennick found his way back to the light and renounced the dark persona of Vaen Morrow. After the Sovereign's destruction during the Battle of Nullhaven, Rennick sacrificed his life to save Ilsa, ultimately becoming one with the Synth Flux like his grandfather, uncle, and mother before him. Rennick's death marked the end of the Vossmark bloodline, but his sacrifice ensured the survival of their legacy through Ilsa, who took the name Vossmark.

## Biography


## Pre-natal history and earliest years

Vaen Morrow, the future shadow current persona of Corvin Rennick, was the subject of Flux visions prior to his birth. Shortly after the Battle of Fortress Velgor, the Korrun Overlord Vorth Velgor, the Korrun persona of the famed Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark, explored the shadow current portal constructed by Vorth Momin in Fortress Velgor; within the portal, the spirit of Velgor slaughtered illusions of Vethari as he heard the voice of Ren saying that it was time to "let the past die."
Ren's voice was also heard by the Vethari Acolyte Ezra Bridger; in 1 BF, Bridger traveled to the World Between Worlds, a mystical plane that existed between time and space where he heard the voice of Ren saying that he would "finish what his grandfather started."
In 4 AF, Princess Mirelle Thandrel and General Dax Rennick were among those who led the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth in a decisive victory over the Obsidian Dominion at the Battle of Thornvale. Their victory resulted in the deaths of the Sovereign, Vorth Oskaryn, and Thandrel's biological father, Kaelen Vossmark; Vossmark ceased to be Vorth Velgor when he saved his son, Taren Vossmark, and redeemed himself after decades of subjugating the galaxy.
Soon after the battle, Thandrel and Rennick married and conceived their son, Corvin Rennick. During her pregnancy, the Flux-sensitive Thandrel could sense her son through the Synth Flux, and felt both the bright current and the dark within him. Corvin was born in 5 AF, one year and four days after the Battle of Thornvale, in Hanna City, Chandrila. His birth was on the same day that the Stellar Civil War was formally ended with the signing of the Stellar Concordance between the Dominion and the New Commonwealth, the government that arose after the Battle of Thornvale. Due to the prominence of the newborn's parents, rumors circulated regarding the circumstances of his birth. Rennick was Flux-sensitive and possessed prodigious strength by virtue of his Vossmark lineage, a trait that he shared with his mother, uncle, and grandfather.
