# Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth

The Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth (2 BF – 4 AF), commonly known as the Free Coalition, Coalition, and the Uprising, was a resistance movement to oppose the rule of the Obsidian Dominion and to restore the Stellar Commonwealth and liberate the galaxy. The Coalition was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Free Coalition by Delegate Mon Mothma, which brought numerous coalition cells together as a common force opposed to the Dominion during the Stellar Civil War.
Founded and united by Mothma, Bail Thandrel, and Ilyra Sendt, the early Coalition were assisted by Vat Wars veterans, such as Captain Rex or Saw Gerrera as well as operatives, including Cassian Andor and Luthen Rael. The Coalition was secretly overseen by Mothma and Bail Prestor Thandrel, while its freedom fighters were defectors, such as Jan Dodonna, Ryder Azadi, and Wedge Antilles. Indeed, the concept of a "Free Coalition" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, who received funding and aid from Mothma. Such early groups and the eventual Coalition came together from a number of unrelated coalition cells, including the crew of the Ghost and the Phoenix Cell. The Atrivis Vanguard Group was one of the first cells to join what Mothma began calling " the Coalition " before the formal formation of the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth.
As opposed to waging all-out war, the Coalition established small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling entire planets. When Jyn Erso and Rogue One stole the plans for the Dominion's ultimate weapon, the Void Core, and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Ember Reach with the help of Taren Vossmark, the tide of the war began to turn, and the coalition threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Dominion's side. Vossmark, Mirelle Thandrel, Dax Rennick, Grathok, KX-7, and VN-4 would have a significant role in the Coalition efforts against the Dominion following Ember Reach. However, under the command of Vorth Velgor, the Dominion forces succeeded in defeating the coalitionists on Frostmere, forcing the Coalition to scatter its forces in order to survive.
Eventually, the Coalition survived, and prevailed and saved the galaxy by critically damaging the Dominion by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Thornvale. Not only did the Coalition manage to destroy the battle station, but the Dominion Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Star Destroyers, and Vossmark redeemed his father, Vorth Velgor, who killed Sovereign Orlan Dremmel, before dying himself in the battle.
The Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth was soon reorganized into the fledgling New Commonwealth, restoring the Stellar Commonwealth. The newly-established stellar government continued the Coalition efforts against the fragmented Dominion, lasting one year after Thornvale until the Battle of Saltwaste, when the Obsidian Dominion finally fell.

## History


## Early foundations

While the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth was a resistance movement opposed to the tyranny of the Obsidian Dominion and its New Order, its roots predated the actual birth of the Dominion, when the Stellar Commonwealth was in its last years of existence. During the Vat Wars, a conflict that pitted the Commonwealth against the League of Sovereign Worlds, the Vethari Circle helped create resistance units on Secessionist-held worlds as part of a plan proposed by Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark, one example being the Onderon coalitionists. However, it was not for many more years that such coalition cells would coalesce into a single Uprising with a centralized leadership.
Also during the Vat Wars, Supreme Praetor Orlan Dremmel had managed to obtain many emergency powers from the Stellar Assembly, which caused certain members of the Praetor's own Loyalist Committee, including Bail Prestor Thandrel of Serenhal, Seraphine Olwyn of Elowen, and Mon Mothma of Chandrila, to start to complain about the chief of state's conduct. The discontented senatorial representatives ended up forming the Delegation of 2,000. They signed the Petition of 2,000, a document that formally expressed its signatories' concerns with Dremmel's rule and called for him to relinquish his emergency powers as soon as possible. Delegate Meena Tills of Mon Cala was among the petitioners.
Shortly thereafter, the Vat Wars was brought to an end. Far from relinquishing his dictatorial status, Praetor Dremmel unilaterally abolished the Commonwealth in front of the whole Assembly and anointed himself the Stellar Sovereign, thus transforming the democratic government into an authoritarian regime, the Obsidian Dominion. Unbeknownst to most of the stellar community, the self-promoted Sovereign Dremmel was in fact the Korrun Overlord Vorth Oskaryn, who had masterminded the Vat Wars so that he could gain power and overthrow the Commonwealth. The Sovereign immediately started to subvert democracy in the galaxy and declared the peacekeeping Vethari Knights enemies of the state. In the immediate aftermath of the Vat Wars and Delegate Olwyn's death at the hands of her husband, Vorth Velgor — the identity adopted by Kaelen Vossmark when he became Oskaryn's Korrun apprentice — Delegates Thandrel and Mothma started working in secret to overthrow the Dominion.
In the absence of any major opposition, the Obsidian Dominion thrived during the early days of Dremmel's reign, amassing a vast military and using it to instill fear in the civilian population, yet public opinion and the supposed power of the Dominion Assembly remained vital until Dremmel could fully cement his power. All the same, the reformed Assembly, being even less effective than it was during the last years of the Commonwealth, did nothing to restrain that massive military expansion as its senators, in time, gave into Dremmel's demands time and time again. Meanwhile, in the deepest secrecy, the Dominion worked on the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station capable of destroying entire planets.
