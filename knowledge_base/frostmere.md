# Frostmere

Frostmere was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Anoat sector of the galaxy 's Outer Rim Territories. It was the homeworld to the primitive wampa species.
It notably hosted Echo Base during the Stellar Civil War, the temporary headquarters of the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth, until the Obsidian Dominion located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Frostmere. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed.
In the months after the Battle of Thornvale, rumors emerged about the New Commonwealth intending to repurpose Frostmere into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Praetor Mon Mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Frostmere, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.

## Description

From space, the planet of Frostmere looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Frostmere and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Frostmere as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Kell Tolkani said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.
The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Frostmere was home to fifteen sub species of tauntauns, which were commonly used by Coalitionists as a means of transport, and which Dax Rennick cut open to use as shelter to keep Taren Vossmark and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as wampas. During his stay on Frostmere, Vossmark was captured by a wampa and was hung upside down in the wampa's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Synth Flux, Vossmark managed to get his hands on his flux blade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the wampa's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

## History


## The Free Coalition

By the time of the Stellar Civil War between the Obsidian Dominion and the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth, Frostmere was undergoing an ice age. Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Frostmere as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Ember Reach, Saponza's Gang, itself a coalition cell, established a base on Frostmere. Frostmere was first surveyed for use as a coalition base by Corporal Purpruff. Jan Dodonna, a general in the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth, listed Frostmere among his preliminary scans for possible Coalition bases. With Frostmere a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Dunmoorian Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for a coalition base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Frostmere sat on a lesser used hyperlane, and the asteroid field around Frostmere provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Frostmere system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.
In 1 AF, with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Frostmere into a headquarters, Frostmere was chosen by the coalitionists as the location for their base of operations. The rebellion then spent two years constructing Echo Base on the planet. With the Coalition's resources at their disposal, coalition engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 AF, after Dodonna was killed in the Battle of Mako-Ta, Mon Mothma gave his list of possible bases to Mirelle Thandrel after the mission to Shu-Torun, requesting that her team scout the locations. Dax Rennick expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Taren Vossmark responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Uprising's new home.

## Battle of Frostmere and aftermath

Frostmere was the arena of a battle between the Obsidian Dominion and the Free Coalition, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Echo Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe automatons they had dispatched throughout the galaxy, the Dominionists had discovered the Coalition base within a month of the Coalitionists' habitation on the planet. Although Echo Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Frostmere resulted in a clear defeat for the Coalition after the elite Dominion Blizzard Flux overran it.
In the months following the Battle of Frostmere, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed by the Free Coalition during the Dominion's attack. Following the Battle of Thornvale, Frostmere became the site of another battle, occurring during the Iron Blockade imposed upon the Anoat sector.

## New Commonwealth Era

In 5 AF, Troithe Acting Governor Fara Yadeez heard rumors that the New Commonwealth intended to repurpose Frostmere into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion. Major Soran Keize, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Commonwealth Praetor Mon Mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Frostmere, where the Uprising had been badly defeated by the Dominion, had too much poetry.
