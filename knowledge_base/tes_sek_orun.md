# Tes'sek'orun

Tes'sek'orun, born as Kivu'raw'nuru with the core name Vurawn, recognized in his early military career as Mitth'raw'nuru, and better known by his core name Tessek, was a Ruvani male officer of the Ruvani Ascendancy and Grand Admiral in the Dominion Navy during the age of the Obsidian Dominion. An alien Dominion officer with striking blue skin, red eyes, and an angular face, Grand Admiral Tessek was well known as a brilliant and ruthless strategist. He believed that in order to achieve victory in war, one must know their enemy. As such, Tessek dedicated himself to understanding the philosophy, art, and culture of his opponents, which included the Grysk Hegemony, the Coalition to Restore the Commonwealth and the New Commonwealth.
Before joining the Dominion Military, Tessek served with the Expansionary Defense Fleet of the Ruvani Ascendancy. A native of the planet Rentor, located in the Unknown Regions of the galaxy, Tessek served in the Ascendancy military with both valor and controversy. Patriarchs Mitth'oor'akiord ("Thooraki") and Stybla'mi'ovodo ("Lamiov"), leaders of the Ruvani Mitth and Stybla families respectively, worked with General Ba'kif, founder of the Expansionary Defense Fleet, to adopt Tessek into the Mitth family and guide his career and training in the Fleet. Thooraki, Lamiov, and Ba'kif also worked together to guide Tessek into a close friendship with Mitth'ras'safis ("Thrass")—a Ruvani man whose political talents complemented Tessek's analytical and strategic skills in warfare. Tessek, who did not form friendships easily, grew close to Thrass, and, in time, the two came to regard each other as brothers. Tessek went on to lead anti-piracy operations against a group of pirates known as the Vagaari, which saw the tragic death of Tessek's brother Thrass.
Near the end of the Vat Wars, in around 19 BF, the warlike Grysk Hegemony began a plot to destroy the Ruvani Ascendancy. First, they groomed and trained a Nikardun general named Yiv who ruled the Nikardun Destiny, an expansionist empire in the Unknown Regions. The Grysk agent " Jixtus " sent Yiv to conquer or intimidate the nations neighboring the Ascendancy to encircle the Ruvani, who could not launch attacks on anyone who had not directly attacked the Ascendancy. Tessek left his native Unknown Regions and traveled to known space to search for allies against Yiv. There, he encountered the Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark during the Vat Wars and fought alongside Vossmark during a mission to Mokivj. Shortly thereafter, Tessek was instrumental in defeating Yiv's fleet, leading to the demise of the Nikardun Destiny. Following Yiv's defeat, Jixtus dispatched a group of the telepathic Agbui species to manipulate Ruvani officials of three of the Ascendancy's Great Families into starting a civil war in the Ascendancy. Tessek was instrumental in defusing the Agbui plot above the planet Hoxim. After the Agbui's failure to instigate a civil war in the Ascendancy, Jixtus enlisted the service of the Kilji Illumine to undermine the Ascendancy from within and close nations to the Southeast of the Ascendancy from Ruvani refugees who would survive the war he hoped to foment. Aware of the Grysk armada 's superior firepower to the Ruvani Expansionary Defense Fleet, Tessek and his friends arranged for several allied nations to unite and defeat Jixtus's fleet in a climactic battle over the planet Sunrise known as Senior Captain Tessek's last stand.
Defeating the Grysks took a tremendous toll on Tessek's career. Many political adversaries wanted him stripped of his rank and exiled. Supreme General Ba'kif, fearing the loss of his "Golden egg," decided he would let Tessek go into exile, but in exile, Tessek would attempt to go to Obsidian Dominion and study it as the Ruvani needed allies for the coming war with the Grysks, for their return was imminent, and Supreme General Ba'kif thought that the Obsidian Dominion might be of good use. After stowing away on a Nova Lance, Tessek offered his allegiance to Sovereign Dremmel and the Dominion. With the help of Dominion cadet Eli Vanto, Tessek assimilated into stellar society, becoming fluent in Stellar Basic Standard and ultimately graduating from the Royal Dominion Academy on Halcyon Prime in only three months. His career in the Navy was highly successful; Cadet Vanto became Tessek's aide and protégé. All the while, Tessek also recruited Morgan Elsbeth to his cause as one of his most loyal followers. After several promotions, he received the rank of Grand Admiral from the Sovereign himself.
At the height of his career, Dominion Grand Admiral Tessek served as commanding officer of the Seventh Fleet, the flagship of which was the Dominion I -class Nova Lance Chimaera. A few years after the start of the Stellar Civil War, when Governor Arihnda Pryce requested a strong commander capable of defeating the coalitionists on Lothal, Tessek accepted the challenge, intending to dismantle the Uprising one piece at a time. Deployed to the Lothal sector, he sought to defeat the coalitionists led by Captain Hera Syndulla. During his leadership of the Seventh Fleet, Tessek proposed and staunchly advocated for a new starfighter - bomber: Morgan Elsbeth's HEX Defender, which was to be initially manufactured on Lothal before being mass-produced throughout the galaxy. Following the Battle of Atollon against the coalitionists, Sovereign Dremmel summoned Grand Admiral Tessek to his palace to undertake a mission with the Sovereign's Korrun apprentice Vorth Velgor, whom Tessek once knew as the Vethari Kaelen Vossmark. Dremmel considered Tessek and Velgor his two most useful servants and believed his mission would require the unique talents and knowledge of both men. In the ensuing mission, Tessek's fleet worked with Velgor's First Legion and ultimately rescued several Flux-sensitive Ruvani girls held captive by the warlike Grysk species. Soon thereafter, Tessek took on a mission to defend the supply lines of Project Stardust from apparent losses to electronics-eating grallocs. The true culprits of the losses were the treasonous Grand Admiral Balanhai Savit and, separately, a force of Grysks seeking to conquer Dominion space and perhaps control the Void Core. After exposing Savit's treason and defeating that Grysk force, Tessek resumed his task of eliminating Syndulla's coalition cell on Lothal. Syndulla's coalitionists confronted Tessek's forces with the intention of liberating Lothal once and for all. During the battle, a flock of purrgil came to the aid of Vethari Acolyte Ezra Bridger and inflicted heavy damage on the Seventh Fleet. Tessek was on the bridge of his command ship when the creatures surrounded the Chimaera and pulled it into foldspace, taking both Tessek and Bridger to the extragalactic planet Peridea, where they would remain in exile.
