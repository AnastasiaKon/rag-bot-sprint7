# Haldrek

Haldrek was a Flux-sensitive human male Serennian who served as a Vethari Elder during the waning years of the Stellar Commonwealth and later Margrave of House Serenno. He became dissatisfied with the Vethari Circle and fell to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, becoming the Dark Lord of the Korrun Vorth Morvane. After leaving the Vethari Circle, he served as Margrave of Serenno and Head of State of the League of Sovereign Worlds during the Vat Wars. He was a backup for Vorth Oskaryn, the Dark Lord of the Korrun, whose plan to conquer the galaxy relied on Haldrek leading a pan- stellar secessionist movement against the Commonwealth. As such, Haldrek immersed himself in the shadow current of the Synth Flux and worked tirelessly to advance his and his master's Grand Plan while being unaware that he was merely a pawn of his master.
Born in 102 BF during the High Commonwealth Era, Haldrek was taken by the Vethari Circle after being abandoned by his family and learned the Vethari arts as the Acolyte of Qelmar, the legendary Grand Master of the Vethari Circle; he went on to take Rael Averross and the maverick Alder Fenwick as his two Acolyte learners at different points, before eventually ascending to the Vethari Conclave. He went on to become a respected and legendary Vethari Elder in his own right. A political idealist, the corruption in the Stellar Assembly —as well as the traditions of the Vethari —disillusioned him. In 42 BF Haldrek left the Order and returned to his homeworld, where he reclaimed his title of Margrave and heritage as a nobleman after overthrowing his corrupt brother, Ramil. His former peers believed his decision was misguided but still held him in high esteem. However the Margrave of Serenno began to secretly conspire with Vorth Oskaryn. After the death of Alder Fenwick at the Battle of Elowen in 32 BF, and following the duel in the Works which saw the death of Vethari Elder Yaddle, Haldrek embraced the shadow current and adopted the name of Vorth Morvane upon fully becoming the apprentice of Vorth Oskaryn. Throughout the first decade of Orlan Dremmel's chancellery, Haldrek used his resources and charisma to recruit entire star systems to the Secessionist cause. At the same time, he oversaw the development of two vast armies that would be pitted against each other in the conflict to come: the Secessionist Automaton Army, which consisted of battle automatons manufactured on worlds such as Hivecrag; and the Grand Army of the Commonwealth, composed of vat soldiers bred on Rainspire and modeled on the contract tracker Dorn Sarvane.
After years of maneuvering the galaxy into a state of war, the Vat Wars began when the newly formed Commonwealth Military invaded Hivecrag in 22 BF. As leader of the Secessionists, Haldrek delegated military authority to a cadre of subordinates personally trained by himself, including General Grievous and Asajj Ventress. While Grievous was trained only in the art of flux blade combat, Ventress was strong with the Synth Flux, affording her the opportunity to learn the ways of the shadow current from Haldrek. The Korrun, however, remained faithful to Vorth Bane 's Rule of Two; and as such, Haldrek was compelled to betray his disciple after Oskaryn became suspicious of his own apprentice's intentions. Though he sought to replace Ventress with Savage Opress and later Quinlan Vos, Haldrek ultimately remained beholden to his master's will, which would eventually be his undoing.
By the third year of the war, Haldrek and Grievous succeeded in abducting Praetor Dremmel (per Oskaryn' plan) from the Commonwealth capital of Halcyon Prime in 19 BF. During the ensuing Battle of Halcyon Prime, he was confronted and defeated by the Vethari Warden Kaelen Vossmark, who summarily executed the Margrave via beheading at the Praetor's behest. In the days following Haldrek's death, Dremmel revealed to the Vethari that he was, in fact, Vorth Oskaryn, who betrayed his apprentice in order to replace him with the younger and more promising Vossmark. With the subsequent fall of the Vethari Circle, Oskaryn consolidated his power upon declaring himself Sovereign of the Obsidian Dominion, which Morvane had an instrumental role in establishing. As for Vossmark, he succeeded the late Vorth Morvane as Oskaryn' third and last apprentice, resulting in his transformation into Vorth Velgor and proving his slain victim's words of sensing fear, hate, and anger.

## Biography


## Early life

Haldrek was born in 102 BF during the High Commonwealth Era, on the planet Serenno to the planet's ruler, Margrave Gora, and his wife, Countess Anya. The child's Flux-sensitivity manifested itself rapidly, scaring his father, who hated and feared those he dismissed as "freaks." Gora contacted the Vethari Circle and told them to take his son. However, instead of waiting for the Vethari seekers to arrive, Gora abandoned the infant outside his palace on the edges of the forest without clothes or any form of identification. A seeker rescued Haldrek before he froze to death or was eaten by spine-wolves, and brought the child to the Vethari Grand Temple on Halcyon Prime.
Haldrek was integrated into Hawk-Bat Clan alongside fellow younglings Zang Arraira, Arath Tarrex, and Sifo-Dyas, under the direction of the Vethari Elder Tera Sinube. During his time as a Vethari youngling, he continuously strove to excel, working himself hard to achieve better results than his peers and always striving to remain within the boundaries of the rules set by his teachers. Haldrek therefore distanced himself from the other younglings rather than try to befriend them. His talents made him clash with fellow youngling Tarrex, largely due to the latter's jealousy over Haldrek's superior abilities. Tarrex frequently insulted Haldrek, calling him 'His Excellency' due to his imperious and somewhat aristocratic manner. Haldrek and Sifo-Dyas were close friends during their time as younglings, and on one occasion in 90 BF the two 12-year-olds accompanied Grand Master Qelmar and Masters Sinube and Yula Braylon to Serenno for the stellar festival being held there with other Vethari in tow.
