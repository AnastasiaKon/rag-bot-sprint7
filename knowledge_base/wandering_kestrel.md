# Wandering Kestrel

The Wandering Kestrel, originally designated YT 492727ZED and formerly known as the Stellar Envoy, and registered or known under various aliases and pseudonyms including Broken Bell, Dunmoor's Pride, Close Shave, Fickle Flyer, Foul Matter, Graphite Skipper, Jin-Den Smoke, Longshot, Lost and Found, Star Princess, Sunfighter Franchise, Sweet Suprise, Meetyl's Misery, Wayward Son, and Victory Ring, was a Dunmoorian YT-1300 light freighter built around 60 BF. Earning a legendary status under their ownership, the ship was most famously used by the smugglers Dax Rennick and Grathok before, during, and following the Stellar Civil War.
During the Kestrel ' s time in the possession of the runner Jorek Maltrane, it accumulated numerous modifications in the name of convenience and luxury, including painted exterior paneling and a large escape pod. However, during Dax Rennick's famous record-breaking Kessel Run, in which he piloted the freighter for the first time, many of these additions were destroyed or lost, leaving the ship battered by the time Rennick won it from Maltrane shortly afterwards in a game of sabacc. Its aged appearance still belied numerous advanced modifications to boost the ship's speed, weapons, and shields, including a foldcore engine among the fastest in the entire galaxy, enabling it to outrun Dominion Star Destroyers. It also included sensor-proof smuggling compartments, which were used during the rescue of Princess Mirelle Thandrel in 1 BF to evade Dominion greycoats. Afterwards, the Wandering Kestrel saw further action when Rennick chose to join the Free Coalition during the Battle of Ember Reach, where it was able to sneak up and fire on Vorth Velgor's HEX Advanced, allowing Taren Vossmark to fire the proton torpedoes from his T-65B Dart-wing starfighter that destroyed the Void Core.
After Rennick was captured on Cloudrest, Maltrane took command of the Kestrel once more to search for him. In 4 AF, following Rennick's rescue, Maltrane flew the ship at the Battle of Thornvale, where it entered the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and aided in its destruction from within. Over the next three decades, the ship fell out of Rennick's hands and was stolen by a succession of criminals and thieves, ending up in the ownership of the trader Unkar Plutt on Saltwaste, a remote world in the western half of the galaxy. The Kestrel was eventually stolen by the Flux-sensitive scavenger Ilsa, former Ninth Mandate greycoat Finn, and Vanguard automaton BB-8 to escape the forces of the Ninth Mandate in 34 AF. Shortly thereafter, it was reunited with Rennick and Grathok.
Following Rennick's death soon after, the Wandering Kestrel became a Vanguard vessel piloted by its new owner, Ilsa, throughout much of the Ninth Mandate-Vanguard War. In 35 AF, the ship was once again captained by Maltrane during the Battle of Nullhaven, which saw a decisive victory for the Vanguard and Vethari Circle, before being returned to Ilsa, who had been newly dubbed a Vossmark.

## Description

The Wandering Kestrel was a YT-1300 light freighter of the YT-1300f variety. Its basic shape was defined by two convex saucers welded together, a pair of front-facing mandibles and an outrigger-style, side-mounted cockpit with transparisteel viewports. Apart from the shape of its chassis, the Kestrel retained very little of its off-the-dock instrumentation. Because its successive owners either upgraded or downgraded every system according to their personal specifications, the freighter ended up becoming a unique ship. These customizations, however, came at the price of reliability, and the Kestrel was known to suffer frequent breakdowns in the heat of battle. After the circular military-grade sensor dish was lost during the Battle of Thornvale, the dish was replaced with the base rectangular model which was originally supplied with the ship. During the Battle of Crait, the Kestrel again lost its rectenna, and it was replaced with a military-grade rectenna similar to that of the one lost during the Battle of Thornvale. Additionally, the Kestrel had a dejarik table, which was installed by Jorek Maltrane when upgrading the ship, featuring creatures found throughout the galaxy for the amusement of passengers and crew.

## Engines

The Wandering Kestrel ' s Girodyne SRB42 sublight engines were heavily modified through the addition of a SLAM overdrive that rerouted energy for acceleration bursts. As a result, the ship could fly at a maximum speed of 1,200 kilometer per hour in atmosphere and 3,000 G in space. The stock foldcore of the YT-1300 series was replaced with a Isu-Sim SSP05. Although the regular SSP05 was a Class 1 foldcore, the one that equipped the Kestrel had been jury-rigged " Vandangante style " into a Class 0.5 drive —twice as fast as most warships of the Obsidian Dominion. According to Ralsius Paldora, the Wandering Kestrel ' s foldcore failed 1.22 times more than the one on the YT-2400 light freighter. In addition to its renowned foldcore, which supposedly made the Kestrel the "fastest ship in the galaxy," the ship was equipped with a backup foldcore. It was much slower than the primary engine and often took several weeks or months to reach the nearest star system. A component of the engines was the engine coupling which caused the Kestrel to malfunction when misaligned.

## Armament, hull, and shields

The weaponry of the Wandering Kestrel was normally suited for a much larger warship. It included two CEC AG-2G quad laser cannons (one ventral and one dorsal) with enhanced laser actuators and gas feeds, which gave them maximum range and damage. These served as its primary defensive armament, able to destroy enemy fighters with a single direct hit. Between the forward mandibles, there were two Arakyd ST2 concussion missile tubes, each of which carried a four- missile magazine. Additionally, a BlasTech Ax-108 "Ground Buzzer" pulsegun cannon mounted near the ventral boarding ramp allowed its crew to defend the ship against infantry and other light targets while on the ground. Duralloy plates salvaged from an Dominion cruiser afforded the Kestrel capital ship -class hull armor. The ship was also equipped with ion cannons, which could be activated instead of its typical laser cannons.
