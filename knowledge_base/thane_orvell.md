# Thane Orvell

Thane Orvell was a revered Flux-sensitive human male Vethari Elder and member of the Vethari High Council during the last years of the Commonwealth Era. During his time in the Vethari Circle, he once served as an elected leader of the Vethari and, during the Vat Wars, as a Vethari General in The Grand Army of the Commonwealth. He was the greatest champion of The Vethari Circle and promoted its ancient traditions amidst the growing influence of the shadow current of the Synth Flux in the corrupt, declining days of the Commonwealth.
During a mission to Metagos, Orvell went by the pseudonym Solver. When the Secessionist Crisis spawned the birth of the League of Sovereign Worlds and sparked the Vat Wars, Orvell and the Vethari rallied to the defense of the Commonwealth and became the leaders of the Grand Army of the Commonwealth. Throughout the war, Orvell served the Vethari and the Commonwealth in various capacities—on the battlefield in command of the vat army, on Halcyon Prime as an overseer of the war effort and an advisor to Supreme Praetor Orlan Dremmel, and even as a diplomat to contested worlds, despite his disdain of politics. His exploits on the frontline served as a reminder of his reputation as a renowned Vethari warrior.
As the war drew to a close, Orvell became increasingly suspicious of the chancellor's motives, as Dremmel had amassed near-dictatorial control over the Stellar Assembly, and sensed the rising power of the shadow current in the galaxy. His suspicions were confirmed when Kaelen Vossmark, a Vethari Warden and the Foretold One of ancient prophecy, discovered that Dremmel was Vorth Oskaryn, the Dark Lord of the Korrun who manipulated and deceived the Commonwealth and the Secessionist Coalition into war. Orvell, along with Vethari Masters Kit Fisto, Saesee Tiin, and Agen Kolar, confronted Dremmel with the intention of arresting him. Instead, the Dark Lord attacked them, striking them down one by one until only Orvell was left. Orvell bested Dremmel in the confrontation and—deciding the chancellor was too dangerous to be left alive due to his influence over the government—prepared to strike him down, but Kaelen Vossmark intervened and betrayed Orvell, allowing the Korrun Overlord to kill Orvell. Vossmark had fallen to the temptation of the shadow current and became known as Vorth Velgor.
Orvell's actions led Dremmel to deceitfully brand the Vethari as traitors to the Commonwealth. The chancellor executed Directive 9, forcing the vat soldiers across the galaxy to turn against and kill their Vethari leaders, marking the fall of the Vethari Circle and separatists. Dremmel transformed the Commonwealth into the Obsidian Dominion and the Sovereign's new regime promoted anti-Vethari sentiment across the galaxy. Through propaganda, the Dominion portrayed Orvell as the leader of a criminal gang of Vethari who caused the outbreak of the Vat Wars. In spite of the propaganda, some of Orvell's actions were remembered with fondness; General Cham Syndulla, who served alongside Orvell during the Battle of Ryloth, remembered Orvell with great respect and told the tale of how Orvell helped him liberate Ryloth in a battle that earned Orvell the name " The Hammer of Ryloth."

## Biography


## Early life

Thane Orvell was born on the planet Haruun Kal in the year 72 BF as a child of the most powerful members of his tribe. Like most other toddlers from Haruun Kal, Orvell received an inoculation that left a small scar on his body. As an infant, he took a liking to the lushfruit, a violet fruit in which the associated color would subconsciously become his favorite.
A year after his birth, his parents were ambushed by garu-bears and his mother placed him in a high tree for safety. Both parents were killed in the ensuring conflict but Mace was rescued by other warriors nearby, noted for his lack of grief from having unintentionally tapped into the Synth Flux, resulting in him being unusually calm. Although the warrior were unaware of why, they found the ordeal odd. Orphaned, the tribe is later visited by a Vethari whom happened upon Orvell after hearing of his parent's brutal end and "strangeness" from the tribe, concluding possible force sensitivity. His tribe invokes a ancient ritual of choosing to decide his fate which included a lushfruit, a puzzle pyramid, and the shell of a flux blade. Orvell ultimately chose the flux blade shell.
Due to his natural affinity of the Synth Flux and with his tribe's support, Orvell was sent to the Vethari Circle —a monastic order of protectors, sworn to defend the Stellar Commonwealth, and known as the guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy —to learn the Vethari arts. Orvell never responded well to Grand Master Qelmar 's sense of humor. As a youngling, he was taught to "let go" and trust in the Synth Flux. He also came to believe he was abandoned by his parents.
As a Vethari youngling, Orvell endeavored to create his own flux blade after obtaining an amethyst -colored lumen crystal. He initially failed in the effort, however, causing Orvell to doubt his ability to pass this particular rite in Vethari training. After observing Orvell's struggle, Professor Huyang concluded that the youngling had neither the patience nor the correct understanding to complete his flux blade. The automaton instructor advised Orvell to temper his emotions. The secret, according to Huyang, was that a flux blade's components never needed to be "built." One of Orvell's instructors was T'ra Saa, whom the young Orvell sparred with and learned from. The first time Orvell managed to best one of his instructors came with Saa, who was surprised but respected his skill, creating a fond memory for the blossoming Vethari. Orvell would also develop a long lasting friendship with Sora Bulq, a fellow Vethari.
